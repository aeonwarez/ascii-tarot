# Princess of Cups

Definitive working reference for the court card. Synthesizes all project
sources; follows the same structure as the Star sheet, adapted for a court
card (no zodiacal decan). A Harris scan of this court card IS available in
this repo, so the palette section is scan-derived, not text-derived.

## Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Card / name | Princess of Cups (Thoth court, suit of Cups) |
| Attribution | Earth of Water (the earthy part of Water); in particular "the faculty of crystallization"; the power of Water to give substance to idea, support life, and form the basis of chemical combination |
| Esoteric titles | "The Princess of the Waters; Lotus of the Palace of the Floods; Princess and Empress of the Nymphs or Undines; Throne of the Ace of Cups" (DuQuette). DuQuette also hails her "Throne of the Holy Grail" |
| Tetragrammaton | He final of the Name; the ultimate issue, crystallization, materialization, and re-absorption of the Energy; "the Silence into which all things return"; permanent and non-existent at once; an audit of 0=2 |
| Rank meaning | A Throne, not a rider. Together with the Ace of Cups she is its throne; a Princess is set upon her Mother's throne only when the Prince wins her. She is the end product, the crystallization and materialization of what was purely potential in the Ace (DuQuette: the Ace "may be worshipped, but the Princess can be adored") |
| Astrology | NONE. Princesses have no zodiacal attribution. As a throne of Water she rules a quadrant of the heavens (Libra/Scorpio/Sagittarius) around the North Pole, not a decan range |
| Region ruled | With the Ace of Cups, the Libra-Scorpio-Sagittarius celestial quadrant above the North Pole, and the area of the Pacific (DuQuette) |
| Yi King | 41st hexagram, "Sun": diminution, the dissolution of all solidity; people described are very dependent on others yet helpful to them, rarely of individual importance, unsurpassed as helpmeets |
| Central figure | A dancing figure, robed in a flowing garment on whose edges crystals form; eyes closed, head thrown back in rapture (DuQuette compares Bernini's Ecstasy of Saint Theresa) |

Note on the throne status: Crowley is explicit that the Princesses "have no
Zodiacal attribution" and "represent the He' final of the Name" (BoT lines
5967, 5974). With the Ace of Cups she rules the Libra/Scorpio/Sagittarius
quadrant around the North Pole (DuQuette lines 6838-6840, 7039-7040), not a
zodiac decan. State this correctly and do not invent a sign or degree range.

## Element ranking (art priorities)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The dancing figure in a flowing scalloped robe** - "represented as a
   dancing figure, robed in a flowing garment on whose edges crystals are seen
   to form." Harris's image is "graceful fluid movement": eyes closed, head
   thrown back, pure rapture. The robe is the card's dominant mass, a sweep of
   pale silk fanning across the whole frame, its scallops the same motif as the
   Ace of Cups. The body must read as dance, not a standing pose.
2. **Crystallization at the hem** - the doctrinal core (Earth of Water = "the
   faculty of crystallization"). Clear water-crystals form along the flowing
   edges of the garment. This is the visible proof of vaporous Water grounding
   into substance; scatter them down the robe, densest at the hem.
3. **The swan crest, open wings** - "For her crest she wears a swan with open
   wings," the swan of oriental philosophy that is the word AUM / AUMGN, "the
   symbol of the entire process of creation." Perched/rising above her head,
   wings spread. Crest, not an accessory.
4. **The covered cup with the tortoise issuing from it** - "She bears a covered
   cup from which issues a tortoise," the Hindu tortoise that supports the
   elephant on whose back is the Universe. The cup she holds/tends is central;
   the tortoise emerges from under its lid.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The dolphin in the foaming sea** - "She is dancing upon a foaming sea in
   which disports himself a dolphin, the royal fish, which symbolizes the power
   of Creation." In the scan it reads as a coiling green sea-serpent / dolphin
   at lower left. The royal fish sports in the foam beneath her.
6. **The lotus** - DuQuette lists Lotus among her symbols (also encoded in her
   title, "Lotus of the Palace of the Floods"). A lotus bloom sits high in the
   field (upper right of the scan) and a lotus/rose crowns the covered cup.
7. **The foaming sea beneath her** - she dances upon it; "The water is alive
   with large smooth waves that suggest the heavy environment of ocean's
   depth" (DuQuette). The base of the card is moving water, not ground.

Tier 3 - supporting / coloring:
8. **Brown hair; blue or brown eyes** (DuQuette plate note). Eyes closed in the
   painting, head tilted back.
9. **Fluid, undulating field** - the negative space is water and drifting
   robe, all curve and wave; no dead flat space. Everything undulates.

## Meanings

**Crowley's descriptive meaning (BoT, court chapter):** The character of the
Princess is infinitely gracious. All sweetness, all voluptuousness,
gentleness, kindness and tenderness are in her character. She lives in the
world of Romance, in the perpetual dream of rapture. On a superficial
examination she might be thought selfish and indolent, but this is a quite
false impression; silently and effortlessly she goes about her work.

**Yi King (BoT):** the 41st hexagram, "Sun" - diminution, the dissolution of
all solidity. People described by this card are very dependent on others, but
at the same time helpful to them. Rarely, at the best, are they of individual
importance; as helpmeets they are unsurpassed.

**Court-theory keynote (BoT, Four Dignitaries):** As He final she is the
ultimate issue of the original Energy in its completion, crystallization,
materialization; also its counter-balancing and re-absorption; "the Silence
into which all things return." Permanent and non-existent at once. No zodiacal
attribution; an "elemental" type whose moral qualities can seem to "lack
bite," subdivided by planetary predominance.

**DuQuette keynote:** The earth subelement of Water; the end product, the
crystallization and materialization of what was purely potential in the Ace of
Cups ("the Ace may be worshipped, but the Princess can be adored"). She is not
all dreams: as earth of water she can literally ground vaporous and romantic
ideas into manifestation, providing a fixed and fertile medium where the
water-nourished life of ideas can flourish. Because Princesses do not rule
degrees of the zodiacal year, there are no celebrated-personality examples for
her.

## Observed Harris palette (from the card scan)

`reference/princess-cups-card.jpg` (scan source: esotericmeanings.com
court-cards page). Unlike the Princess of Wands, a Harris scan of this court
card IS available, so this palette is observed from the painting:
- **Field:** soft lavender / pale violet-white upper background, deepening to
  teal and slate blue in the sea at the base. Cool blues and blue-greens
  dominate, "as they do the other Cups court cards" (DuQuette).
- **Robe:** the great fanning garment is near-white / pale silver-lilac silk
  with soft violet fold-shadows; scalloped edges catch the palest blue.
- **Swan crest:** green-bodied swan with open wings above the head, the same
  jade / sea-green that recurs through the card.
- **Covered cup + tortoise:** olive-and-gold cup crowned with a pink lotus /
  rose; the tortoise and cup read jade-green and brass-gold, the warmest mass
  on the card.
- **Dolphin / sea-serpent:** a coiling green (jade to olive) creature at lower
  left in the foam.
- **Lotus:** pale pink bloom upper right; pink rose atop the cup.
- **Water-crystals:** faint rose / mauve faceted solids scattered down the robe
  and along the hem, echoing the crystalline shore of Atu XVII.
- **Sea:** deep teal and blue-green waves at the base, foam in cream-white.
- **Hair:** brown (DuQuette plate); eyes closed, head thrown back.
- Suggested ANSI mapping: robe in pale silver-lilac / white; swan, cup,
  tortoise, and dolphin in jade / sea-green with brass-gold accents; lotus and
  crystals in soft rose / pink; sea in teal and blue-green; hair brown.
  Introduce no color with no referent in the scan or text.
