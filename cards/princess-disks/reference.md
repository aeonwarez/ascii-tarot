# Princess of Disks

Definitive working reference for the court card. Synthesizes all project
sources; follows the same structure as the Star sheet, adapted for a court
card (no zodiacal decan). Unlike most court cards, a Harris scan of this
card IS available in the repo and the palette is read from it.

## Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Card / name | Princess of Disks (Thoth court, suit of Disks) |
| Attribution | Earth of Earth (the earthy part of Earth); "on the brink of transfiguration" |
| Esoteric titles | "The Princess of the Echoing Hills; The Rose of the Palace of Earth; Princess and Empress of the Gnomes; Throne of the Ace of Disks" (DuQuette plate note) |
| Tetragrammaton | Hé final of the Name; the last of the sixteen court cards, the ultimate crystallization and re-absorption of the Energy. As Earth of Earth she is the Malkuth of Malkuths, the lowest court card of the lowest suit |
| Rank meaning | A Throne, not a rider. She is the Throne of the Ace of Disks; a Princess is set upon her Mother's throne only when the Prince wins her |
| Astrology | NONE. Princesses have no zodiacal attribution. As a throne of Earth she rules a quadrant of the heavens (Aries/Taurus/Gemini) around the North Pole, not a decan range |
| Region ruled | With the Ace of Disks, the Aries-Taurus-Gemini celestial quadrant above the North Pole, and the area of Europe and Africa (DuQuette) |
| Yi King | 52nd hexagram, "Kan": the meaning is "a mountain"; its characteristic is rest, repose described part by part through the body (toes, calves, loins, spine, jaws) |
| Central figure | A pregnant priestess of Demeter, standing in a grove of trees before an altar suggesting a wheatsheaf, wrapped in an enormous fur cape, crowned with a ram's head and horns |

Note on the throne status: Crowley is explicit that the Princesses "have no
Zodiacal attribution" and "represent the He' final of the Name" (BoT court
intro, lines 5886-5995). She rules a quadrant around the North Pole
(Aries/Taurus/Gemini per DuQuette), NOT a zodiac decan. State this correctly
and do not invent a sign or degree range. NOTE: this quadrant differs from
the Princess of Wands (Cancer/Leo/Virgo); do not copy the Wands figure.

## Element ranking (art priorities)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The brooding pregnant priestess** - "She is strong and beautiful, with
   an expression of intense brooding, as if about to become aware of secret
   wonder." She "bears within her body the secret of the future"; she is the
   Earth about to give birth. Harris insisted on painting her pregnant even
   after Crowley resisted (Harris to Crowley, Dec 11 1942). The swelling body
   under the cape must read.
2. **The diamond-tipped sceptre descending into the earth** - "her sceptre
   descends into the earth. There its head becomes a diamond, the precious
   stone of Kether." The highest and purest light born in the deepest, darkest
   Element. A long rod plunging DOWN into rock, its lower tip a faceted diamond
   (DuQuette: diamond-tipped rod, the tetrahedral essence of all carbon life).
3. **The great disk as a sprouting seed** - the disk she bears is a giant
   seed "composed of thirty-six sections" (DuQuette; perhaps the source of the
   36 small cards). At its centre is the Chinese ideogram of "the twin spiral
   force of Creation in perfect equilibrium" (the yin-yang), "from this is born
   the rose of Isis, the great fertile Mother." In the scan this reads as a
   round shield-disk carrying a golden rose/spiral rosette.
4. **The ram-horned crest** - "Her crest is the head of the ram." DuQuette's
   plate note specifies a WINGED ram's head. Capricorn/ram = the New Year, the
   autochthonous godhead thrust up by Hidden Fire; ties to the Kan mountain.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The grove of sacred trees** - "She stands within a grove of sacred trees
   before an altar." DuQuette: a grove of BARREN and DYING trees "that her
   fertile presence will now restore to green health." In the scan they are a
   ring of leafless golden-brown trunks flanking her.
6. **The altar suggesting a wheatsheaf** - "before an altar suggesting a
   wheatsheaf, for she is a priestess of Demeter." Her station; the Eleusinian
   grain. A pale sheaf-form mass at or below her feet.
7. **The enormous fur cape** - DuQuette: "wrapped in an enormous cape of what
   appears to be animal fur." It dominates the lower silhouette in the scan,
   a heavy cream-white pelt falling to the ground.

Tier 3 - supporting / coloring:
8. **Rich brown hair, dark eyes** (DuQuette plate). Warm earth tones on the
   figure herself.
9. **The mountain (Kan)** - the Yi King attribution: "The mountain is the most
   sacred of all terrestrial symbols, stark, rugged, and immoveable in its
   aspiration to the Highest, thrust up by the Titan energy of Hidden Fire."
   The rock the sceptre plunges into can carry this reading.

## Meanings

**Crowley's descriptive meaning (BoT, court chapter):** She represents the
earthy part of Earth, and is consequently on the brink of transfiguration.
Strong and beautiful, with an expression of intense brooding, as if about to
become aware of secret wonder. She bears within her body the secret of the
future. Her characteristics are "too various to enumerate"; she is "Womanhood
in its ultimate projection," containing all the characteristics of woman,
whichever manifests depending entirely on the influences to which she is
subjected. Each attribute is pure in itself.

**"Bewildering inconsistency" (BoT):** her general reputation is like a
lottery wheel from which "the extraction of any number does not predict or
influence the result of any subsequent operation." To the adept every turn of
the wheel is equally probable and equally a prize, for every Event is "a play
of Nuit."

**Yi King (BoT):** the 52nd hexagram, "Kan," meaning "a mountain"; its
characteristic is rest. Each line of the comment describes repose in the parts
of the body in turn (toes, calves, loins, spine, jaws). The mountain is the
most sacred terrestrial symbol, thrust up by the Titan energy of Hidden Fire,
no less an hieroglyph of the Inmost Godhead than the Phallus itself, even as
Capricornus (the New Year) is exalted in the Zodiac.

**Court-theory keynote (BoT, general remarks):** As Hé final she is the
ultimate issue of the original Energy in its completion, crystallization,
materialization, and re-absorption. Princesses have NO zodiacal attribution;
they represent the Hé final of the Name and rule a quadrant of the heavens
around the North Pole. The Princesses / Empresses represent the earthy
sub-element, so the Princess of Disks is Earth of Earth.

**DuQuette keynote:** The most beautiful female figure in the deck, likely the
last card Harris painted (Dec 1942 note). As Earth of Earth she is the Malkuth
of Malkuths, the lowest court card of the lowest suit, "the ultimate princess."
She is pregnant not merely with the highest and lowest of the suit of Disks but
of ALL the suits: she carries "the potential of all possible possibilities, and
the key to perpetuating the life of the universe." A priestess of Demeter
arising from the Earth itself, establishing her altar in a grove of barren and
dying trees her fertile presence will restore. In a reading she signifies a
person of "bewildering inconsistency" (womanhood in ultimate projection), her
potential limitless. Crowley closes the court cards here as if ending the whole
book, with the benediction to the student to attain Knowledge and Conversation
of the Holy Guardian Angel and accomplish the Great Work.

## Observed Harris palette (from the card scan)

`reference/princess-disks-card.jpg` (scan source: esotericmeanings.com
court-cards page). Unlike the other Princesses, a painting scan IS available,
so this palette is read from the image and cross-checked with the text:
- Field / atmosphere: warm ochre and dark yellow, an almost humid golden light
  (DuQuette: "Dark yellows and browns radiate a warm and almost humid
  atmosphere"). Textured grays make it "almost tactile."
- **Fur cape: cream / pale ivory-white**, the large soft mass filling the lower
  figure; shaded with gray textures.
- **Ram horns / crest: greenish-gold**, curling from her head; red-pink
  streamers (pendant ribbons) fall from the crest down the figure.
- **Diamond sceptre: warm ochre shaft** plunging lower-left into gray rock,
  the tip a small pale faceted diamond.
- **Seed-disk / shield (right of figure): golden-orange rose-rosette** on a
  paler disk; the spiral germ at center.
- **Grove: golden-yellow and warm-brown bare trunks**, leafless, flanking both
  sides against the ochre sky.
- **Altar / rock base: grays and browns**, the wheatsheaf-form mass pale at her
  feet.
- Hair rich brown; eyes dark (DuQuette plate). No cool accents beyond the
  green-gold horns; the card is dominated by earth warmth.

Suggested ANSI mapping: cape and wheatsheaf in cream/ivory-white; sky field in
ochre/dark-yellow; horns green-gold with a red-pink streamer accent; disk-rose
in golden-orange; grove in yellow-brown; rock and altar in gray-brown; hair
brown. Introduce no color with no referent in the scan or text.
