## Element ranking (BoT p.112-113 + DuQuette + the painting)

Tier 1:
1. **Bilateral symmetry** — the whole card mirrors on the vertical axis
2. **The great moon orb, top center** — pale gold, waning, "moon of
   witchcraft and abominable deeds," with a red serpentine swirl inside
   (painting), flanked by the towers
3. **The V-cone of tainted light** — pale stream widening from the moon
   and narrowing DOWN to a flame-point at bottom center (painting
   corrects "path receding to horizon"); serum tinged with blood
4. **Nine drops of impure blood, shaped like Yods**, falling inside the
   cone — exactly nine
5. **Khephra the scarab bearing the solar disk** at the very bottom,
   under the water, inside a gold-ringed aura — the sun carried through
   midnight; the emotional heart of the card

Tier 2:
6. **Two black towers of nameless mystery** on the hills, upper corners —
   hooded, cloak-like, tiny lit windows (Harris makes them almost figures)
7. **Twin Anubis** in double form between the Ways — large orange-tan
   jackal-headed guardians with tall staffs, Mercury symbol (☿ → `+o`
   romanized glyph or omit) in hand
8. **The jackals at their feet, on watch** — small black beasts

Tier 3:
9. Pool of blood-tinged water with horizontal ripple lines
10. Red-and-blue bell-curve wave-mountains flanking the aura (Harris's
    signature waveforms at the bottom third)
11. Starless indigo sky — keep it empty; the darkness IS the atmosphere

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, line 9535):** Illusion, deception,
bewilderment, hysteria, even madness, dreaminess, falsehood, error,
crisis, "the darkest hour before the dawn," the brink of important change.

**Mnemonic verse:** "Let the Illusion of the World pass over thee,
unheeded, as thou goest from the Midnight to the Morning."

**BoT texture:** guarded by Tabu (uncleanliness and sorcery); black towers
of horror and fear = prejudice, superstition, dead tradition, ancestral
loathing; the fiery sense is baulked — the knight relies on touch, taste,
smell; light deadlier than darkness, silence wounded by the howling of
wild beasts; Anubis the watcher in the twilight; the jackals devour those
who have not known His Name; threshold of life and death; the dreadful
madness of pernicious drugs; the Dark Night of the Soul — and the answer
at every stage: "How splendid is the Adventure!"

**DuQuette:** the light that darkness creates; illuminates dreams AND
nightmares; the obligatory hero's-quest chapter through poisoned darkness;
"the most cheerful thing I can think to say about it is: it will change."

## Observed Harris palette (reference/18-moon-card.jpg)

- Moon orb: pale gold rim, interior pale blue with RED serpent-swirl
- Towers + cloak-hills: deep indigo/black-blue; buff/stone background
- Light cone: pale silver-blue, widening downward to a red-gold flame peak
- Yods: crimson, staggered column of 9 inside the cone
- Anubis: orange-tan bodies, gold kilts, dark staffs; black jackals
- Bottom: pale water with grey ripple lines; wave-mountains outlined red
  and blue; great pale-gold aura ring; dark scarab; ORANGE sun disk
- Card frame: pale warm gray deco
