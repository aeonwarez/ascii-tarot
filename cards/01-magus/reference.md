# I - The Magus

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the three project sources -
Crowley's *Book of Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the
esotericmeanings.com tutorial - plus the color card scan.

## Element ranking (BoT p.70-72 + DuQuette Atu I + esotericmeanings + scan)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The airborne androgynous Magus in the Mercury-glyph posture** - a naked,
   fair, androgynous youth (masculine + feminine, like the Fool), his whole
   figure forming the alchemical glyph of MERCURY: the two serpents at his head
   = the horns, the huge stylized wings at his feet = the arrowhead cross,
   projected on the caduceus. His attitude suggests a swastika / thunderbolt of
   dynamic motion (nothing static - "its rate that of the limit c").
2. **The eight juggled objects in midair** - he joyfully juggles EIGHT: the four
   elemental weapons - Wand (fire, the phoenix wand), Cup (water, two-handled
   Grecian), Dagger/Sword (air, a stiletto), Disk (earth, the 8-fold star of
   Mercury) - plus four more: the Winged/Orphic Egg (spirit), the Stylus and
   Papyrus/Scroll (Thoth the scribe), and the Wand of Double Power (Horus / the
   New Aeon / Tiphareth).
3. **The great caduceus** - far larger than it first appears: its rod runs from
   below his feet to the very bottom (penetrating the membrane of space), its
   winged head spans the full width of the top and reaches down behind his neck.
   Head = a winged phallus; the circle = the eye of Horus; a DOVE descends in
   the circle (spirit into creation; links to the Empress). "The balanced energy
   that has created the illusion of existence."
4. **The Kether-light V + Binah-dark field** - a V-shaped wedge of WHITE Kether
   light behind/above him; darkness (Binah) below. The background crossing lines
   = the making of form, which do NOT penetrate the formless inverted triangle
   of Kether; an arc/semicircle at the bottom = Binah birthing form.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The lemniscate of serpents** - an infinity sign of two serpents above his
   head (his infinite source in Kether), stretching down through his feet into
   Binah. LEFT serpent wears the throne-headdress of Isis (Binah); RIGHT serpent
   a plain crown (Kether) - together, the Magus uniting Kether and Binah.
6. **Winged helmet / winged sandals of Hermes** - winged head and heels (the
   messenger, the channel for spirit arising in Kether). The sandal-strap is a
   serpent shedding its skin (life anew).
7. **The Ape of Thoth (Cynocephalus)** - a monkey groping up from the LOWER
   RIGHT, by his right foot: the distortion of truth inherent in all speech and
   form. "Manifestation implies illusion." (Reappears as HermAnubis in Fortune.)

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The golden sunburst** - barely visible behind the left wing at his feet =
   Mercury as herald of the Sun (the creative Mercury is of the nature of the Sun).
9. **The yellow caduceus outline** = Vau of Tetragrammaton (union of Yod/phallus
   and He/dove).
10. **The two-faces read** - the face is drawn to read looking up-left OR
    down-right depending on focus (an ambiguity fitting his dual/trickster nature;
    optional garnish).

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- This is a MOTION card, the counterpart to the Fool's stillness: everything
  flung outward, swastika/thunderbolt dynamism, objects orbiting in midair.
- The figure = the Mercury glyph: two horned serpents up top, a body, wide wings
  at the feet as the arrowhead cross. Keep it legible as the alchemical mark.
- Big vertical CADUCEUS rod down the center behind him; winged head spanning the
  top; a small descending dove in its circle.
- Ring the figure with the 8 juggled objects as small distinct glyphs (wand,
  cup, dagger, disk-with-star, egg, stylus, scroll, double-wand).
- White V of Kether light behind the head narrowing up; dark Binah mass below;
  faint crossing form-lines in the field; arc at the very bottom.
- Ape of Thoth crouched in the lower-right corner.
- Palette in the `.ans`: warm YELLOW-GOLD figure + caduceus, WHITE light V,
  indigo/violet dark below, small color accents on the juggled weapons.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, p.290):** "Skill, wisdom, adroitness,
elasticity, craft, cunning, deceit, theft. Sometimes occult wisdom or power,
sometimes a quick impulse, a brain-wave. It may imply messages, business
transactions, the interference of learning or intelligence with the matter in
hand."

**DuQuette verse (title-page):** "The True Self is the meaning of the True Will:
know Thyself through Thy Way! Calculate well the Formula of Thy Way! Create
freely; absorb joyously; divide intently; consolidate completely. Work thou,
Omnipotent, Omniscient, Omnipresent, in and for Eternity."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** You have all the skills and tools to move
forward - bring them together well. Mind the purity of your thoughts (actions
have consequences). Discipline and focus to turn ideas into action. Clear
communication. Assess your skill set honestly. Bring spirituality in - be a
channel for spirit (the lemniscate joining Kether to the material beam).
Separate truth from what deceives you. A good time to share spiritual knowledge.

**Reversed (esotericmeanings):** Skills used to others' detriment (tricksters,
con men, claiming skills not possessed). Out of touch with reality; lost insight
and objectivity. Creating distractions detrimental to your path. Poor
communication. Lost the "magic," life feels mundane; reassess skills and
motivation. Not sharing spiritual insight that would help others.

**BoT texture:** Beth ("house"), Mercury - the bearer of the Wand, Energy sent
forth; the Wisdom, the Will, the Word, the Logos by whom the worlds were created
(St John I). He is the Son, the manifestation in act of the idea of the Father;
the male correlative of the High Priestess. Mercury = action in all forms; the
first "electric charge," continuous creation. Being the Word he is the law of
reason / necessity / chance; being duality he is truth AND falsehood, wisdom AND
folly - the unexpected, tricky, without conscience because creative; the
Unconscious Will. Second emanation from the Crown; the adult Fool. The Egyptian
Thoth: ibis-headed (concentration, the meditative spirit), bearing the phoenix
wand (resurrection through the generative process) and the Ankh (crux ansata, a
form of the Rose and Cross); followed by the cynocephalus ape that distorts the
Word ("Manifestation implies illusion"). Crowley warns no true image is possible
- the perpetual motion at the rate of light means "this picture is hardly more
than mnemonic jottings."

**DuQuette:** "The Magus of Power," planetary trump of Mercury, Beth, Path 12
Kether→Binah. Only ONE Magus is canonical (the two extra "magi" are a Swiss
publisher's bonus filling the 80-card print sheet - no "Three Wise Men"). The
first alchemical trump = Mercury; the figure forms the Mercury glyph (serpents =
horns, foot-wings = arrowhead), projected on a caduceus whose rod reaches the
card's bottom and whose wings span the top; the arrow in the caduceus disk is
actually a descending dove. Behind the left foot-wing, a faint golden sunburst
(Mercury as herald of the Sun); behind the right, the Cynocephalus/Ape of Thoth
groping up from the lower right (the curse of distortion on all who attain the
grade of Magus). He juggles the four weapons plus style, papyrus, torch, and
winged egg (the pre-existent zero / Orphic egg that begins its alchemical
adventure across the trumps).
