# XV - The Devil

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the project sources: Crowley's *Book of
Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the Harris color scan. No
esotericmeanings.com tutorial on file for this card, so the Upright / Reversed
readings are expanded from Crowley's divinatory entry and DuQuette.

## Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Number / name | Atu XV - The Devil. DuQuette title: "The Lord of the Gates of Matter, the Child of the Forces of Time." NOT the card of evil; Crowley: "A devil who had unity would be a god." Completely misunderstood in the Dark Ages of Christianity; Levi re-drew it as Baphomet, and at least "succeeded in identifying the goat portrayed upon the card with Pan." |
| Esoteric figure | PAN PANGENETOR, the All-Begetter, in the form of a three-eyed Himalayan HE-GOAT (the Goat of Mendes / Baphomet / Priapus) standing before the Tree of Life, his creative energy veiled in the WAND OF THE CHIEF ADEPT crowned with the winged globe and the twin serpents of Horus and Osiris. Saturn the ruler is Set, the ass-headed god of the Egyptian deserts. |
| Hebrew letter | Ayin ("eye") - hence the third eye in the goat's forehead. One of the three vowel-consonants Aleph / Yod / Ayin that spell I A O; Atu 0, IX and XV are a threefold explanation of the male creative energy, and this card is "the masculine energy at its most masculine." |
| Astrology | CAPRICORN (cardinal earth, the Goat). SATURN rules (selfhood and perpetuity); MARS exalted (the fiery, material energy of creation in its best form). Capricornus occupies the Zenith, the most exalted of the signs; the Sun turns northwards on entering it, so the meditative madness of winter already foresees the divine madness of spring. |
| Tree of Life | Path 26, Tiphareth (Beauty / human consciousness) to Hod (Splendour / Thought). Symmetrically placed with Atu XIII (Death, Tiphareth to Netzach / Bliss); Atu XIV runs between them to Yesod. The three together are a hieroglyph of the processes by which idea manifests as form. |
| Alchemy / generation | Generation itself rather than lab alchemy: the phallic wand as the axis of the world, the two translucent globes as testes, the dancing homunculi inside them as the nuclei of male and female, the broken paired lines as chromosomes and the equatorial starry rays as cellular division. Creative energy in its most material form. |
| Color scale (GD/DuQuette) | Indigo; Black; Blue-black; Cold dark Grey nearing black. |
| Painting palette | An ORANGE transparent tree-trunk up the centre; a pale CREAM/WHITE goat; INDIGO and grey-blue spiral horns and caduceus-shaft; a salmon-PINK ground webbed with grey-blue tenuous forms and network lines; two great ORANGE globes at the base holding pale blue-white figures over YELLOW chromosome bands. |
| Keynote | Divine mirth and exuberant materiality; "the complete appreciation of all existing things ... he transcends all limitations; he is Pan; he is All." The impulse to create taking no account of reason, custom or foresight: "divinely unscrupulous, sublimely careless of result." Be strong, then canst thou bear more joy. |

## Element ranking (BoT p.105-107 + DuQuette Atu XV + the Harris scan)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The three-eyed goat of Pan** - the hero figure: a shaggy Himalayan HE-GOAT
   head and standing body, a THIRD EYE (Ayin) open in the centre of the forehead,
   long ears out, hooves below. Frontal and hieratic, head and spine on col 23,
   drawn ON TOP of the trunk behind it. "The whole card is the Devil."
2. **The great spiral horns** - the widest mark on the card, sweeping out and
   round to left and right in a double curl. "Even the horns of the goat are
   spiral, to represent the movement of the all-pervading energy"; Zoroaster
   defines God as "having a spiral force." Mirror them about the axis.
3. **The phallic Wand of the Chief Adept** - a great erect shaft rising the full
   height of the card ON the axis, crowned at the top with the WINGED GLOBE and
   the TWIN SERPENTS of Horus and Osiris, its shaft going down indefinitely to
   the centre of earth. The creative energy veiled as the badge of the Adept.
4. **The two translucent globes with dancing figures** - at the base, two great
   round TESTES flanking the shaft, transparent, holding tiny human forms in
   motion: four FEMALE in the left, four MALE in the right, the topmost male
   wearing the classic horned Devil's head, "who appears to have fought his way
   to the top." The spermatozoa-homunculi, life unrestrained.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The Tree of Life behind** - the card is "the Tree of Life as seen against a
   background of ... madness." The trunk fills the centre and PIERCES THE HEAVENS
   above; the roots are made TRANSPARENT "in order to show the innumerable
   leapings of the sap" plunging into the depths below.
6. **The ring of the body of Nuit** - at the very top, about the trunk where it
   pierces the sky, an indicated RING (the cosmic Ring-Pass-Not backdrop): "If I
   lift up my head, I and my Nuit are one."
7. **The background of divine madness** - "the exquisitely tenuous, complex, and
   fantastic forms of madness, the divine madness of spring, already foreseen in
   the meditative madness of winter": ghost-shapes, membranes and network lines
   webbing the whole ground left and right of the trunk.

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **Chromosomes and cellular division** - inside the globes, paired BROKEN LINES
   suggesting chromosomes and starry RAYS at the equator of each globe suggesting
   cell division; keep at least the ray-bursts.
9. **The garland and the collar** - a wreath of vine / ivy blossom across the
   goat's brow between the horns, and a winged blue COLLAR with a RED disk at the
   chest. "A collar of gold for thy throat, a scarlet bow for thy horns."
10. **The high places** - barren summits, the mountain cult; "the goat leaping
    with lust upon the summits of earth," goat-forms on the slope. The
    surroundings are essential to the symbolism: barren places, especially high.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- The most rigorously AXIAL card of the run: goat head, trunk, wand-shaft and the
  gap between the globes all stack on col 23, and the horns and globes mirror
  about it. Build it with `PM`/`PMB` about `AXIS = 23.0` almost throughout and
  verify with `--axis`; a head centred over a shaft that leans two columns is the
  classic tell.
- It is also a strong VERTICAL: the composition is one unbroken column from the
  winged globe at the top rule to the roots at the bottom rule. Full-bleed the
  trunk top and bottom; do not float the goat in air.
- Cells are 1:2, so draw the two basal globes ~2:1 WIDER than tall or they read as
  eggs, and dither them as translucent masses with the figures reserved out of the
  fill rather than outlined on top of it.
- The single most important read: a horned three-eyed goat standing before a great
  tree, an erect wand on the axis, two glowing globes full of dancing figures at
  its feet.
- The horns are the silhouette. Give them the full width they need before spending
  columns anywhere else; the third eye and the horn-spirals are what make it the
  Devil rather than a generic beast.
- Palette: keep the ORANGE trunk and ORANGE globes hot against the cold indigo
  GD scale, with the goat pale cream. That warm-on-indigo split is what separates
  this card from Death's murk and the Hanged Man's green-and-pale.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu XV, line 9490):** "Blind impulse,
irresistibly strong and unscrupulous, ambition, temptation, obsession, secret plan
about to be executed; hard work, obstinacy, rigidity, aching discontent,
endurance."

**Title-page verse (BoT appendix, line 9487):** "With thy right Eye create all for
thyself, and with the left accept all that be created otherwise."

**Upright (expanded from Crowley + DuQuette):** Creative force at its most
material and most unembarrassed: ambition, drive, appetite, the will to build and
to enjoy what is built. The complete appreciation of all existing things, ecstasy
found in every phenomenon however naturally repugnant. Hard work, obstinacy and
endurance that grinds through what refined approaches cannot. A secret plan about
to be executed. Permission to want things, to be embodied, to laugh; the material
plane is not an estrangement from God. "Be strong, then canst thou bear more joy."

**Reversed / lower expression:** The same force with no eye on it: blind impulse
that takes no account of reason, custom or foresight; obsession, compulsion,
temptation used as an anaesthetic; rigidity and aching discontent, appetite that
has become a cage; ambition detached from purpose; treating matter as a trap and
then acting the trapped animal. Not evil arriving from outside, but generative
energy misnamed and mishandled.

**BoT texture:** Attributed to Ayin, an Eye, and to Capricornus. Creative energy
in its most material form; Capricornus occupies the Zenith, the most exalted of
the signs, the goat leaping with lust upon the summits of earth. Saturn rules
(selfhood and perpetuity), Mars exalted (the fiery material energy of creation in
its best form). Pan Pangenetor, the All-Begetter. The Tree of Life against a
background of the fantastic forms of madness, the divine madness of spring; the
roots made transparent to show the leapings of the sap; before it the Himalayan
goat with an eye in the centre of his forehead, his creative energy veiled in the
Wand of the Chief Adept crowned with the winged globe and the twin serpents. The
sign is "rough, harsh, dark, even blind ... divinely unscrupulous, sublimely
careless of result" (AL I:42-44, line 3986). The trunk pierces the heavens with
the ring of the body of Nuith about it and the shaft goes down to the centre of
earth (AL II:26, line 3997). Saturn as Set, god of the south, whose name is in
Shaitan and Satan; the essential surroundings are barren and high places, the cult
of the mountain, the Witches' Sabbath on a desolate summit. Aleph, Yod and Ayin
spell I A O; Atu 0, IX and XV explain the male creative energy threefold, and this
card is the masculine at its most masculine.

**DuQuette:** "The Lord of the Gates of Matter, the Child of the Forces of Time,"
zodiacal trump of Capricorn, Saturn rules and Mars exalted, Ayin (eye), Path 26
Tiphareth to Hod, colours indigo / black / blue-black / cold dark grey. Original
design: "the figure of Pan or Priapus"; Levi's Baphomet is sound commentary but
should not be found in the text. The most universally misunderstood card in the
deck and the fortune-teller's scapegoat; every other trump can carry evil well
enough, so no card is needed just for evil. The Devil is "just God as
misunderstood by the ignorant and wicked." A magnificent three-eyed Himalayan goat
stands before a tree and its two great transparent globular roots; the man and
woman bound beneath the Devil in traditional decks are translated into four female
forms in the left testicle and four male in the right, the top male bearing the
horned head, with broken lines insinuating chromosomes and starry equatorial rays
suggesting cellular division. "He is life itself, unrestrained, in mad love and
seeking to grow and unite with absolutely everything." In *The Wake World*, Lola
meets him on path 26 at a midnight banquet on a desolate heath and is told his
name is Yeheswah, the Saviour of the World.

## Expected Harris palette (scan at reference/15-devil-card.jpg)

- Trunk / world-tree: a broad ORANGE-ochre transparent column up the centre,
  widening to a bell at the base where it meets the roots
- Goat: pale CREAM and warm white body and face, grey shading, dark hooves
- Horns: heavy INDIGO / grey-blue spirals, ridged, sweeping wide left and right
- Third eye: a small pale eye with a dark pupil, high on the brow; garland of
  blue-violet blossom between the horns
- Chest: a winged blue-grey COLLAR with a RED disk at its centre
- Wand: a dark near-black SHAFT on the axis running down between the globes
- Crown of the card: a pale blue-green RING (Nuit) around the trunk, with dark
  twin SERPENTS coiling up left and right to the winged globe
- Base: two great ORANGE globes, pale blue-white dancing figures inside, YELLOW
  chromosome bands, black starburst RAYS at each equator
- Ground: salmon-PINK washed with grey-blue tenuous membranes and fine network
  lines (the fantastic forms of madness)
- Frame: pale warm gray art deco; `XV` plaque top, "The Devil" band
