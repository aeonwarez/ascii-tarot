# Princess of Wands

Definitive working reference for the court card. Synthesizes all project
sources; follows the same structure as the Star sheet, adapted for a court
card (no zodiacal decan, no Harris scan available).

## Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Card / name | Princess of Wands (Thoth court, suit of Wands) |
| Attribution | Earth of Fire (the earthy part of Fire; "the fuel of Fire") |
| Esoteric titles | "The Princess of the Shining Flame; the Rose of the Palace of Fire; Princess and Empress of the Salamanders" (DuQuette) |
| Tetragrammaton | Hé final of the Name; the ultimate issue, crystallization, materialization, and re-absorption of the Energy; "the Silence into which all things return"; an audit of 0=2 |
| Rank meaning | A Throne, not a rider. Together with the Ace of Wands she is its throne; a Princess is set upon her Mother's throne only when the Prince wins her |
| Astrology | NONE. Princesses have no zodiacal attribution. As a throne of Fire she rules a quadrant of the heavens (Cancer/Leo/Virgo) around the North Pole, not a decan range |
| Region ruled | With the Ace of Wands, the Cancer-Leo-Virgo celestial quadrant above the North Pole, and the area of Asia (DuQuette) |
| Yi King | 27th hexagram, "i" (nourishment): omnivorous passion, reckless in the means of gratification, insatiable |
| Central figure | Unclothed dancing princess, virgin priestess of the Lords of Fire, leaping in a Yodh-shaped flame |

Note on the throne status: Crowley is explicit that the Princesses "have no
Zodiacal attribution" and "represent the He' final of the Name" (BoT line
5967, 5974). She rules "the Heavens for one quadrant of the portion around
the North Pole" (line 6171), not a zodiac decan. State this correctly and do
not invent a sign or degree range.

## Element ranking (art priorities)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The dancing, leaping princess** - a nude priestess in motion, "the
   dance of the virgin priestess of the Lords of Fire." She creates her own
   beauty by essential vigour and energy; the body must read as motion, not a
   standing figure.
2. **The surging Yodh-flame she leaps in** - "she is leaping in a surging
   flame which re-calls by its shape the letter Yodh." The flame around/under
   her is the card's signature form: a single rising Yodh-tongue of fire that
   frames the leap.
3. **The plumes of justice, streaming like flames from her brow** - her
   headdress reads as flame, not a static crown. "shewn with the plumes of
   justice streaming like flames from her brow."
4. **Nudity as chemistry** - she is unclothed "shewing that chemical action
   can only take place when the element is perfectly free to combine with its
   partner." The bareness is doctrinal, not decorative.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The wand crowned with the disk of the Sun** - "She bears a wand crowned
   with the disk of the Sun." A long club/wand topped by a solar disk. (BoT.
   DuQuette's plate note calls the symbols "Gold Altar, long club, largest at
   bottom.")
6. **The golden altar with rams' heads** - "she is in attendance upon the
   golden altar ornamented with rams' heads) symbolizing the fires of
   Spring." Aries/ram = the spring fire; the altar is her station.
7. **The tiger** - DuQuette's plate note gives Crest: Tiger's head, and
   Symbols: "Tiger, leaping flames." Harris shows the tiger at her side /
   leaping with her; the beast doubles her ferocity.

Tier 3 - supporting / coloring:
8. **Red-gold hair, blue eyes** (DuQuette plate). The hair carries the fire
   colour into the figure.
9. **Leaping flames filling the field** - the whole card is combustion; the
   negative space is fire, not void.

## Meanings

**Crowley's descriptive meaning (BoT, court chapter):** Brilliant and
daring; she creates her own beauty by essential vigour and energy, imposing
the impression of beauty upon the beholder. In anger or love she is sudden,
violent, and implacable; she consumes all that comes into her sphere.
Ambitious and aspiring, full of enthusiasm often irrational. She never
forgets an injury; her only patience is the patience with which she lies in
ambush to avenge.

**Ill-dignified (BoT):** superficial and theatrical, shallow and false, yet
without suspecting it, because she believes entirely in herself even mid-mood;
cruel, unreliable, faithless, and domineering.

**Yi King (BoT):** the 27th hexagram, "i" - a person omnivorous in passion of
whatever kind, entirely reckless in the means of obtaining gratification, and
insatiable; the commentary alternates warning and encouragement.

**Court-theory keynote (BoT, Four Dignitaries):** As Hé final she is the
ultimate issue of the original Energy in its completion, crystallization,
materialization; also its counter-balancing and re-absorption; "the Silence
into which all things return." Permanent and non-existent at once. No
zodiacal attribution; an "elemental" type whose moral qualities can seem to
"lack bite," subdivided by planetary predominance.

**DuQuette keynote:** The earth subelement of Fire. She officiates before the
ram's-headed altar as Princess of the flaming sacrifice AND is herself the
fuel of the fire. We recognize her in people so energetically self-assured,
independent, and irrationally audacious that they are thrilling but downright
dangerous to be around. Ill-dignified, the ultimate drama queen, so shallow
and self-absorbed it never occurs to her that nobody sympathizes; unchecked,
she will incinerate herself needlessly and set fire to the whole
neighborhood. Because Princesses do not rule degrees of the zodiacal year,
there are no celebrated-personality examples for her.

## Observed Harris palette (from the card scan)

`reference/princess-wands-card.jpg` (esotericmeanings.com court-cards page).
The painting confirms the text and adds the striking rainbow flame:
- **Field: scarlet red**, the hot ground of the card (DuQuette: "fiery reds,
  yellows, and gold dominate," as in all the Wand courts).
- **The great flame swirl: rainbow-banded** - a broad S-curve sweeping up the
  right, banded red / orange / yellow / green as it rises. The single most
  distinctive color event in the four Wands courts.
- **Figure and tiger: golden-orange**; the tiger leaps with her, tawny gold
  with darker markings.
- **Wand:** topped with a small bright solar disk, upper left.
- **Altar: golden**, ornamented with rams' heads, lower left, with rose-pink
  flames rising from it.
- **Hair: red-gold; eyes: blue** (DuQuette plate note), the lone cool point.
- **Read:** the freest and most kinetic of the four - a leaping body inside a
  rainbow tongue of fire, not enthroned or charioted.
