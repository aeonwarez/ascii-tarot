## Element ranking (BoT p.80-84 + DuQuette Atu VI + esotericmeanings)

Tier 1 — the card IS these:
1. **The hooded Hermit officiant, central on the axis** — a closely shrouded
   figure (a form of the Hermit / of Mercury) at the CENTER, standing in the
   SIGN OF THE ENTERER (also Benediction and Consecration), arms thrust forward
   over the couple, a SCROLL twisted round his arms into an eternal mobius loop
   (the Word / marriage certificate). WHITE LIGHT of Kether behind his head.
2. **The Hermetic Marriage: black King + white Queen, hands joined** — to one
   side the black/Moorish KING (Emperor, red tincture / Sulphur, GOLD crown =
   Sun, 5 points = Geburah, snakes-in-lemniscate robe, holds the SACRED LANCE);
   to the other the white QUEEN (Empress, white tincture / Salt, SILVER crown =
   Moon, orb + cross = Chesed, bees on her robe, holds the HOLY GRAIL bearing the
   descending dove + 5 white rays). Their free hands are JOINED, consenting.
3. **Cupid / Eros above, blindfolded, arrow downward** — a blindfolded winged
   Eros over their heads, GOLDEN wings and a golden quiver inscribed THELEMA
   (Greek), aiming his ARROW downward (directed Will; shot from Kether toward
   Chokmah). "Love is the Law, Love under Will."
4. **The winged Orphic egg below, between lion and eagle** — at the bottom, the
   grey egg speckled black-and-white (the Supernal Triangle), WINGED, coiled by a
   WINGED SERPENT (Hadit / Kether). The product of the union; unity about to open
   into diversity.

Tier 2 — named and emphasized:
5. **The Red Lion and the White Eagle** — flanking the egg: the RED LION (Leo,
   Fire; tail rising between the legs, 5 kinks = Geburah) and the WHITE EAGLE
   (Scorpio, Water; tail 4 feathers = Chesed). The Chesed-Geburah axis, male and
   female principles.
6. **The Arch of Swords overhead** — the whole ceremony stands beneath an ARCH
   of steel swords (Zain), the gateway to the white Light of Kether.
7. **The counterchanged Twins** — the Gemini children attending: the WHITE child
   (opposite the black King) with 4 white ROSES (Chesed, receptive); the BLACK
   child (opposite the white Queen) with a CLUB and the father's Lance (Geburah,
   phallic). They stand on the egg's wings, white-haloed.

Tier 3 — atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **Lilith and Eve in the upper corners** — LILITH upper-left (death, Kali-like,
   seductive); EVE upper-right (life, the serpent entwined with its hood spread
   behind her head). The duality of Binah: creation and decay.
9. **The concealed bow at the bottom** — the black twin's lance doubles as an
   arrow for a hidden bow; links the Lovers forward to Art (Sagittarius).
10. **The orange overlap-robe + yellow Tiphareth field** — orange where the
    King's red and Queen's yellow meet (serpent + bee motifs); warm yellow hues
    reading the path into Tiphareth.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- Build on strong bilateral symmetry: hooded Hermit dead-center on col 23, arms
  forward; Cupid centered above; Orphic egg centered below. Mirror King+lion on
  one side against Queen+eagle on the other about `AXIS = 23.0`.
- The single most important read is the CENTRAL OFFICIANT joining two crowned
  figures under an ARCH OF SWORDS, with a blindfold Cupid above and a winged egg
  below — a vertical Kether-to-egg spine on the axis.
- Contrast the two monarchs by value: dark King vs. pale Queen; keep the gold vs.
  silver crowns legible. Lion left-low, eagle right-low, egg between them.
- Lilith UL / Eve UR as small corner figures; nine or so sword-tips forming the
  overhead arch. Blindfold Cupid small, arrow pointing down the axis.
- Palette in the `.ans`: orange central robe, black-vs-white monarchs, red lion /
  white eagle, steel-grey sword arch, pale-blue Cupid, grey egg, yellow field.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu VI):** "Openness to inspiration,
intuition, intelligence, second sight, childishness, frivolity, thoughtfulness
divorced from practical consideration, indecision, self-contradiction, union in
a shallow degree with others, instability, contradiction, triviality, the
'high-brow.'"

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "The Oracle of the Gods is the
Child-Voice of Love in thine own Soul; hear thou it. Heed not the Siren-Voice of
Sense, or the Phantom-Voice of Reason: rest in Simplicity, and listen to the
Silence."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Essentially CHOICE — who you want to be, how and
on what level you connect with others. Often a moral dilemma; weigh consequences
and take the higher path. Can signify a beautiful soul-connection relationship /
soul mate; self-love is the key to a lasting bond. Time among like-minded people;
a longing for union of the soul with its spiritual source. The inner marriage of
opposites.

**Reversed (esotericmeanings):** Feelings not mutual; restore balance or accept
you have grown apart. Out of sync with loved ones; strained relationships and
hard communication. Not enough self-love / self-respect; no one to share values
with; imbalance within, lost sight of self; loneliness; cut off from the
spiritual source through inner conflict.

**BoT texture:** The card of Analysis followed by Synthesis; a glyph of duality,
the continuous see-saw of contradictory ideas (each sentence of its old Liber 418
form reverses the last). Simplified from *The Chymical Marriage of Christian
Rosenkreutz*. The hooded figure = a form of the Hermit/Mercury, standing in the
Sign of the Enterer, scroll about his arms, celebrating the Hermetic Marriage.
The Royal persons: the black/Moorish King (gold crown, Red Lion) and the White
Queen (silver crown, White Eagle); he bears the Lance, she the Grail, free hands
joined. Twin children counterchanged (white holds Cup + roses, black holds Lance
+ club). At the bottom the winged Orphic egg — grey (the three Supernals), the
serpent purple (Mercury, Queen scale), wings crimson (Binah, King scale). Cupid
in the air, blindfolded, golden wings, "Thelema" on the quiver, the intellectual
aspect of Binah's influence upon Tiphareth.

**DuQuette:** "The Children of the Voice, the Oracle of the Mighty Gods." Zain
(sword), Gemini (Mercury rules, Dragon's Head exalted), Path 17 Binah→Tiphareth.
The 4th alchemical trump: *solve* (Lovers) to Art's *coagula*, a story of
marriage and pregnancy whose true main character is the CHILD of the union (the
Orphic egg, "the essence of all life under the formula of male and female"). The
egg has wings as if flown in from the Magus; it sits between white eagle and red
lion (the white/red tinctures of Empress and Emperor, which reverse in Art). The
attending children are Cain and Abel; Lilith and Eve in the upper corners (from
the Second Aethyr of *The Vision and the Voice*). The officiating priest is the
Hermit (Atu IX) making the Sign of the Enterer, the scroll a mobius marriage
certificate. Cupid's downward arrow is shot "by the topmost point of the Yod,"
Kether to Chokmah. The whole beneath an arch of steel swords.
