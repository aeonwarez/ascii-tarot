# Prince of Wands

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes all project sources; mirrors the
depth of the trump sheets, adapted for a court card (no numeral, elemental
title, no Harris scan available).

## Element ranking (art priorities)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The lion-drawn chariot.** "The chariot which is fortified by a wheel
   radiating flame," drawn by a single lion he reins with his right arm. The
   Prince is Vau, the Son "represented as in a chariot, going forth to carry
   out the combined Energy of his parents." The chariot is the card's spine.
2. **The Phoenix wand of the Second Adept**, borne in his LEFT hand. "The
   wand of Power and Energy." Air-of-Fire made into a scepter; the head is a
   phoenix. This is his single most identifying object.
3. **The prince himself, warrior in scale mail with bare arms.** "In complete
   armour of scale mail, but his arms are bare on account of his vigour and
   activity." Seated in Crowley's Thunderbolt asana (DuQuette). The nudity of
   the arms is the tell of restless Air-of-Fire energy.
4. **The rayed crown, winged lion's head, curtain of flame.** "He wears a
   rayed crown surmounted by a lion's head winged, and from this crown depends
   a curtain of flame." Crown to shoulders is one continuous fire-fall.
5. **The sea of flames he rides.** "He rides upon a sea of flames, both waved
   and salient." The whole floor of the card is fire, curling (waved) and
   leaping (salient). No water horizon; fire IS the ground.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
6. **The sigil of To Mega Therion on his breast.** The Mark of the Beast,
   Crowley's personal seal. DuQuette: this device appears in only two other
   places in the deck (5 of Wands, Ace of Disks). This card is "Crowley's
   idealized image of himself" (his Ascendant was 3 degrees Leo).
7. **The flame-radiating chariot wheel.** A wheel that throws off fire, not a
   plain cart wheel; reads as a second small sun low in the frame.

Tier 3 - supporting:
8. **Salamanders / elemental Fire creatures.** He is "Prince and Emperor of
   the Salamanders"; the fire field can carry salamander suggestions.
9. **The steady solar force of Leo.** BoT court theory: "the airy part of
   Fire is sympathetic with Leo, the steady force of energy, the Sun." The
   fire here is sustained and radiant, not the Knight's lightning flash.

Tier 4 - fine detail, as space allows:
10. **Scale-mail texture** on the torso where armour shows, contrasted against
    the bare arms so the two read as different surfaces.

## Meanings

**Crowley's character portrait (BoT chapter, the court cards have no separate
divinatory appendix; the chapter description IS the meaning):** swiftness and
strength; noble, generous, romantic to the point of folly. Sometimes acts on
impulse, is easily led by external influence, and in trifles is a prey to
indecision. Often violent in the expression of an opinion without necessarily
holding it ("states a vigorous proposition for the sake of stating it"); slow
to make up his mind because he "always sees both sides of every question."
Essentially just, but feels justice is unattainable in the intellectual world.
An extravagant boaster who laughs at his own boast. His courage is fanatically
strong, his endurance indefatigable: "always fighting against odds, and always
wins in the long, the very long, run," through "enormous capacity for work,
which he exercises for its own sake, without lust of result."

**Ill-dignified (BoT):** each quality inverts. Great cruelty, partly sadistic
and partly from callous indifference and laziness; intolerant, prejudiced,
idle "principally because it saves trouble"; an empty boaster and a great
coward.

**As a person (DuQuette shortcut):** somebody's birthday card, July 12 to
August 11. The card is Crowley's self-portrait (rising sign 3 degrees Leo).
Other named Princes of Wands by Sun sign: Julius Caesar, John Dee, Nelson
Mandela, Mick Jagger, Jacqueline Kennedy, Madame Blavatsky, Christopher Hyatt.

**Vau / the Son:** "the active issue of their union, and its manifestation;
the intellectual image of their union." His action is more enduring than the
Knight's or Queen's. Also "the Dying God, redeeming his Bride in the hour, and
by the virtue, of his murder."

**I Ching correspondence (BoT):** the airy part of Fire is the 42nd Hexagram,
Yi, "addition, increase." "With sincere heart seeking to benefit all below"
(line 5); immense success, but "fraught with commensurate danger."

## Observed Harris palette (from the card scan)

`reference/prince-wands-card.jpg` (esotericmeanings.com court-cards page).
The painting confirms the text and adds the arrangement:
- **Field: a radiant sunburst of orange and yellow rays** filling the whole
  frame, the dominant register (DuQuette: "fiery reds, yellows, and gold
  dominate this card, as they do all the Wand court cards").
- **The prince: golden / tawny nude skin**, seated, a rayed solar crown with a
  winged lion-head crest and a curtain of flame falling from it.
- **Chariot: a palisade of vertical orange-red flame-bars**, with a green
  accent low under the seat (the one cool note).
- **Lion: golden-orange**, couchant at the very base, drawing the chariot.
- **Phoenix wand:** red-gold flame at the head, borne in the left hand.
- **Beast sigil (To Mega Therion):** small, high-contrast on the breast.
- **Read:** the most purely radiant of the four - a sustained solar blaze
  (steady Leo fire), not the Knight's diagonal nor the Queen's ordered rays.
