# XXI - The Universe

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the project sources - Crowley's *Book of
Thoth* and DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot* - against the Harris scan at
`reference/21-universe-card.jpg`. (No esotericmeanings tutorial on file for this card;
the upright/reversed reading below is built from the Crowley divinatory entry plus the
BoT/DuQuette body.)

## Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Number / name | Atu XXI - The Universe (old decks: The World). DuQuette title: "The Great One of the Night of Time." The last Atu, the complement of the Fool: Aleph + Tau spell ATh, "Essence," so all reality is compassed between them. The Fool is the negative issuing into manifestation; the Universe is that manifestation, its purpose accomplished, ready to return. |
| Esoteric figure | The DANCING MAIDEN - the naked shining figure with female breasts, eyes closed, posed in the sign of Earth (right foot advanced, right hand raised, left hand lowered and thrown back), manipulating the RADIANT SPIRAL FORCE in her hands, one ray dextro-rotary and one levo-rotary. She is the final Heh of Tetragrammaton, the Daughter set upon the throne of the Mother; at once Nuit/Babalon of Binah and the virgin princess of Malkuth. Her dancing partner is Heru-Ra-Ha of Atu XIX: "The Sun, Strength & Sight, Light; these are for the servants of the Star & the Snake." |
| Hebrew letter | Tau ("the Sign of the Cross," extension). Value 400, hence the original design's ellipse of 400 lesser circles. |
| Astrology / element | SATURN, the outermost and slowest of the seven sacred planets; because of those dull, heavy qualities the element EARTH was thrust upon the symbol. Planetary Trump of Saturn AND Elemental Trump of Earth. Saturn is Binah's representative, the only planetary sphere above the Abyss: the highest high and the lowest low on one card. |
| Tree of Life | Path 32, Yesod (Moon/Foundation) -> Malkuth (Kingdom/Earth). The last path, the bottom of the descent. "The influence of the path descends upon the earth, Malkuth, the daughter" - the doctrine of setting the daughter upon the throne of the Mother, and so the end returning automatically to the beginning (the Malkuth-Kether loop). |
| Geometry (this card's "alchemy") | The QUADRATURE OF THE CIRCLE. Both the traditional and the Harris design are built on the New Jerusalem figure: two equal circles intersecting at their centres open the VESICA PISCIS (Kether wedded to Chokmah opening the yoni of Binah), the vesica enclosed in a third circle (an eye), squared, four corner circles struck on the diagonals, all enclosed in a final square. Squaring the circle is the geometric New Jerusalem: the eternal circle of heaven married to the terrestrial square of Earth. Proven impossible in 1882 by von Lindemann, which is the point. |
| Color scale (GD/DuQuette) | Saturn: Indigo; Black; Blue-black; Black, rayed blue. Earth: Citrine, Olive, Russet and Black; Amber; Dark Brown; Black, flecked Yellow. |
| Painting palette | An indigo/midnight ground gone bright: a blue-green LATTICE oval starred with gold, a gold-and-dark spotted SERPENT coiling through it, a pure bright GREEN sphere at centre, a flesh-and-copper DANCER, ORANGE-terracotta Kerubs in dark red-brown corners, pale blue-white RAYS from the eye at the top, a pale blueprint pyramid at the bottom. |
| Keynote | Completion, synthesis, the end of the matter; the Great Work accomplished and celebrated. Also Saturn's weight: delay, inertia, patience, persistent stubbornness in difficulty. "The crystallization of the whole matter involved." |

## Element ranking (BoT p.117-119 + DuQuette Atu XXI + the Harris scan)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The dancing maiden** - the hero figure: a naked shining dancer with female
   breasts, eyes closed, spiral-coiled hair, posed in the sign of Earth (right foot
   advanced, right hand raised, left lowered and thrown back), a red scarf at the
   loins. She is the completed Great Work in a body: the Daughter, Malkah, Betulah,
   Persephone, the Virgin of Eternity. Centre her on col 23; draw ON TOP of the
   lattice and the sphere.
2. **The great serpent / the radiant spiral force** - a huge gold, dark-spotted
   serpent-ribbon coiling in a great S from the top of the card down across her body;
   this is the "radiant spiral force, the active and passive, each possessing its dual
   polarity" that she manipulates, right hand dextro-rotary and left levo-rotary. The
   Snake to Heru-Ra-Ha's Star. The card's central action: she does not stand still,
   she DANCES the force.
3. **The great oval mandorla of 72 circles** - the vesica piscis touching top, bottom
   and both sides of the card, woven as a lattice of seventy-two circles for the
   quinaries of the Zodiac, the Shemhamphorasch (the original design called for 400,
   the value of Tau; Harris gives 72). DuQuette: the ribbing reads as the skeleton of
   an infinitely huge serpent. A woven NET starred with gold, never a bare outline.
4. **The four Kerubim in the corners** - Man/Aquarius, Eagle/Scorpio, Lion/Leo,
   Bull/Taurus: the four fixed signs, the four Elements, the established Universe,
   cornered exactly as in the Hierophant. Harris paints them as ornate orange-terracotta
   heads in dark red-brown scrollwork. Harris to Crowley: "The elemental Cherubims are
   quite jolly but the Little Lady has twisted and turned 'til I am insane with the
   wiggling lines and I've done about forty drawings of her."

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The wheel of Light and the green sphere** - at the centre, "a wheel of Light
   initiates the form of the Tree of Life, shewing the ten principal bodies of the
   solar system. But this Tree is not visible except to those of wholly pure heart."
   What is visible in the painting is a great PURE BRIGHT GREEN sphere (New Aeon Earth,
   no longer black or of mixed colours) carrying a triple-half-twist Mobius strip -
   Harris's endless glyph standing in for the invisible Tree.
6. **The eye of the vesica and its rays** - a second small eye-vesica set high in the
   great oval, either projecting all the images onto the card or drawing them back
   inside itself: the eye of the Great Architect. Pale rays erupt from it. The dancer's
   raised hand drives a black crescent (DuQuette's guess: a sickle, apt for a Saturnian
   goddess) into the middle of it.
7. **The house of matter at the foot** - at the bottom point of the vesica, the
   skeleton plan of the building of the house of Matter: the ninety-two then-known
   chemical elements as tiny verticals ranked in hierarchy and joined by diagonals
   (after J. W. N. Sullivan, *The Bases of Modern Science*), projected onto the largest
   of three Giza-like pyramids, its apex swelling with finishing stones.

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The ambience of loops and whorls** - "all these symbols swim and dance in a
   complex but continuous ambience of loops and whorls." Keep the whole field in
   motion; scatter gold stars through the lattice.
9. **Saturn's indigo ground turned bright** - the traditional card is subfusc, "the
   confusion and darkness of the material world," but the New Aeon has brought fullness
   of Light: the indigo of Saturn derived from the blue velvet of the midnight sky,
   and this card "as bright and glowing as any in the Pack."
10. **The Tau of extension** - the fourfold cross implied under the whole design: the
    squares of the New Jerusalem figure boxing the vesica, the red scarf shaped to the
    letter, the four corners as the arms of the cross. Extension, the hard facts of
    nature, the limitation that makes a Universe.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- Radially symmetric, not hieratic-frontal: the great oval and the four corner Kerubs
  are bilaterally symmetric about col 23 and should be built with `PM`/`PMB` about
  `AXIS = 23.0`; the DANCER and the SERPENT are deliberately asymmetric inside that
  symmetric shell. Verify with `--axis`.
- Cells are 1:2 - the great oval mandorla must be drawn ~2:1 WIDER than tall or it
  renders as a squashed egg. Same for the green sphere and the eye-vesica. This card
  lives or dies on that ratio; it is nearly all curved geometry.
- The single most important read: a dancing maiden wound about by a great coiling
  serpent inside a woven oval of circles, four beast-heads at the corners.
- The 72-circle lattice is the hardest problem. Do NOT attempt 72 legible circles at
  47 wide; render it as a woven MESH (diagonal cross-hatch with `o` nodes at the
  crossings) that reads as a net of circles, and let the node count suggest rather than
  count out. Break the mesh behind the figure and the serpent.
- The serpent needs a spotted texture (`o` / `:` / `·` on a solid body) to read as the
  Harris snake and not as a plain ribbon; give it one clear great-S sweep rather than
  many small coils.
- Palette: an indigo/midnight ground, a blue-green starred lattice, a GOLD spotted
  serpent, a PURE BRIGHT GREEN sphere, a flesh/copper dancer, ORANGE Kerubs in
  red-brown corners, pale blue-white rays. Bright, not murky - explicitly the opposite
  of Death's ground even though both are dark-attributed.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu XXI):** "The matter of the question itself,
synthesis, the end of the matter, may mean delay, opposition, obstinacy, inertia,
patience, perseverance, persistent stubbornness in difficulty. The crystallization of
the whole matter involved."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Treat time and all conditions of Event as
Servants of thy Will, appointed to present the Universe to thee in the form of thy
Plan. And: blessing and worship to the prophet of the lovely Star."

**Upright:** The end of the matter and its synthesis - a cycle completed, a work
delivered, the whole of a question finally visible at once. Crystallization: the thing
sets, takes solid form, becomes fact. Embodiment and incorporation, spirit brought all
the way down into matter and body rather than left in theory. Completion that is also a
return: the end begets the beginning, so this is arrival AND the doorway back to the
Fool. Mastery of time and circumstance as servants of Will; celebration of the Great
Work accomplished.

**Reversed / lower expression:** Saturn's weight without Saturn's structure - delay,
opposition, obstinacy, inertia; a thing that will not move on schedule. Stuckness in
the material, heaviness, coldness, dulness, immobility; the subfusc reading of the
world as mere confusion and darkness. Stubbornness mistaken for perseverance, or a
refusal to let a finished cycle end. Also the failure to descend: keeping the Work
abstract and never letting it crystallize into Malkuth.

**BoT texture:** The card comes at the end of all and is therefore the complement of
the Fool; Aleph and Tau spell ATh, Essence. The beginning was Nothing, so the end must
be Nothing too, but Nothing in its complete expansion. Tau means the Sign of the Cross,
that is, of extension, symbolized fourfold for the revolving Tetragrammaton: the number
4 carries both the rigid extension, the hard facts of nature, and the transcendence of
space and time by continuously self-compensating change. Tau is attributed to Saturn,
outermost and slowest of the seven sacred planets, and because of those dull heavy
qualities the element of Earth was thrust upon the symbol. Saturn and Earth share
heaviness, coldness, dryness, immobility, dulness; yet as soon as the end of a process
is reached it returns automatically to the beginning (in chemistry it is the heaviest
elements that radiate the most tenuous and active particles; the Cefalu essay imagines
an element heavier than uranium capable of reconstituting the entire series - the
chemical reading of 0=2). Hence blackness is also attributed to the sun: "Osiris is a
black god." Saturn is masculine, the old god, the god of fertility, the sun in the
south, but equally the Great Sea, the great Mother; and the letter Tau on the Tree
emanates from the moon of Yesod and descends upon Malkuth, the daughter - "setting the
daughter upon the throne of the Mother." So the card is a glyph of the completion of
the Great Work in its highest sense, and the image of the Universe is a maiden, the
final letter of Tetragrammaton. She is a dancing figure manipulating the radiant spiral
force, active and passive, each with its dual polarity; her dancing partner is
Heru-Ra-Ha of Atu XIX. The Universe is the Celebration of the Great Work accomplished.
In the corners are the four Kerubim showing the established Universe; about her an
ellipse of seventy-two circles for the quinaries of the Zodiac, the Shemhamphorasch. In
the centre of the lower part, the skeleton plan of the building of the house of Matter,
the ninety-two known chemical elements ranked in hierarchy (after J. W. N. Sullivan).
In the centre a wheel of Light initiates the form of the Tree of Life showing the ten
principal bodies of the solar system, invisible except to those of wholly pure heart.
All these symbols swim and dance in a complex but continuous ambience of loops and
whorls. The traditional colour is subfusc, the confusion and darkness of the material
world; but the New Aeon has brought fullness of Light - Earth is no longer black or of
mixed colours but pure bright green, the indigo of Saturn is derived from the blue
velvet of the midnight sky, and this card is today as bright and glowing as any in the
Pack. The appended essay, "The Virgin Universe" (Vision and the Voice, 9th Aethyr), is
the card's own gloss: a palace whose every stone is a jewel set with millions of moons,
and the palace is nothing but the body of a woman; the daughter of BABALON the
Beautiful; "This is the Daughter of the King. This is the Virgin of Eternity. This is
she that the Holy One hath wrested from the Giant Time, and the prize of them that have
overcome Space. This is she that is set upon the Throne of Understanding."

**DuQuette:** "The Great One of the Night of Time," Planetary Trump of Saturn and
Elemental Trump of Earth, Tau ("cross"), Path 32 joining Yesod to Malkuth. The original
design: a demonstration of the Quadrature of the Circle; an ellipse composed of 400
lesser circles; at the corners a man, an eagle, a bull and a lion; within the circle a
naked shining figure with female breasts, eyes closed, in the sign of Earth, gripping
in each hand a spiral ray of dazzling light, dextro- and levo-rotary, a red scarf
concealing the fact of male genital organs and suggesting the letter by its shape.
Saturn is Binah's representative and the only planetary sphere above the Abyss, the
highest of the high, yet path 32 runs down into Malkuth, which is only pendant to the
Tree; the same card holds the highest high and the lowest low. The card is built on the
New Jerusalem figure (vesica piscis, enclosing circle, square, four diagonal circles,
final square) - the marriage of the eternal circle of heaven to the terrestrial square
of Earth. The vesica appears twice: as the great oval touching all four edges, and as
the EYE high on that oval, which seems either to project the images onto the card or to
draw them back into itself. Harris gives seventy-two circles rather than 400, which
suggests the ribbed skeleton of an infinitely huge serpent. At the bottom point of the
vesica, Sullivan's house of matter (the ninety-two elements) superimposed on the
largest of three Giza-like pyramids. At the centre, where Crowley's invisible Tree of
Life should be, a beautiful green sphere carrying a triple-half-twist Mobius strip -
Harris's glyph of the infinite. In the goddess's right hand a black crescent, most
likely a sickle, driven into the middle of the great eye. Is she Nuit/Babalon of Binah
or the virgin princess of Malkuth? "She is, in truth, both the great mother and the
virgin daughter of eternity." DuQuette closes the trumps here with the 9th Aethyr
vision and with Lola's entry into path 32 in *The Wake World*: "Come on! This is only
the Servants' Hall, nearly everybody stays there all their lives."
