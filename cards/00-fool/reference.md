# 0 - The Fool

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the project sources - Crowley's *Book of
Thoth* and DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot* - against the Harris scan at
`reference/00-fool-card.jpg`. No esotericmeanings.com tutorial exists for this card in
`reference/` (the folder holds 01-14 and 17 only), so the Upright / Reversed readings
below are built from Crowley's divinatory appendix, the title-page verse, and DuQuette.

## Element ranking (BoT p.68-69 + DuQuette Atu 0 + the Harris scan)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The central horned figure** - a pale-eyed, broadly smiling GREEN-clad Fool
   standing frontal and full height, legs spread, arms raised and open, bursting
   unexpectedly upon the world. Gold Dionysus HORNS on a golden head, a white phallic
   CONE of light rising between them (Kether). DuQuette: both feet planted unfirmly in
   AIR, not the cliff-edge vagabond of older decks. Body/spine on col 23, drawn on top.
2. **The great spiral loops / radiant whorl** - three swirling rings that issue from
   and return to his heart, coiling outward as a great ELLIPSE around the whole figure:
   the three Veils of the Negative (Ain, Ain Soph, Ain Soph Aur) manifesting in divided
   light. Crowley calls them "rainbow-hued spirals," the Form of the Universe; Harris
   paints them as pale SILVER-WHITE coils with only faint iridescence, so render a
   luminous spiral shell, not a saturated rainbow (see ASCII notes). The spiral is the
   card's negative-space fill and its structure at once.
3. **The solar emblem at the groin** - a small golden WINGED SUN / solar disk over the
   Fool's generative centre, the focus of the microcosm, "the radiant sun" in which all
   his many forms are resumed. Iconic and small; DuQuette stresses the genitals are
   deliberately hidden by the Sun. Do not omit. The "0 = All" made flesh.
4. **The dove ascending** - a white DOVE (the Holy Spirit, the dove of Venus / Isis /
   Mary) rising at the upper left near an open hand, pouring with the other spirit-birds
   from the Holy Grail. Spirit lifting off.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The tiger** - an ORANGE tiger at his lower right, fawning and leaping, teeth near
   his thigh. The beast tearing at him, unheeded; fear met with innocence. Clearly a big
   cat mid-lunge, small but legible.
6. **The crocodile (Sebek)** - a green CROCODILE crouched below in the Nile with its
   lotus stems, at the Fool's feet, half in blue water. The devourer-initiator whom
   Harpocrates treads upon; a low horizontal jagged form, a faint MOON above its head.
7. **The grapes and the coin-hive** - a bunch of dark PURPLE grapes hanging from his
   left shoulder (fertility, sweetness, the basis of ecstasy, Bacchus), and beside them
   the satchel of the universe rendered as a cluster of pale coins marked with the
   PLANETARY and ZODIACAL glyphs. The vagabond's bag, holding all creation lightly.

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The vulture of Maut** - the Egyptian mother-goddess bird in the upper field, among
   the attributions of godhead pouring from the Grail; impregnated by the breath of the
   wind, like the Virgin Mary. A tiny background mark.
9. **The butterfly** - a many-coloured BUTTERFLY of "many-coloured air" fluttering at
   the left, the Greek psyche, symbol of transformation.
10. **The winged globe with twin serpents, the twin infants, the three flowers** - the
    caduceus-like winged globe echoed by two INFANTS embracing on the middle spiral,
    with the benediction of "three flowers in one" hanging above them, and the ivy sacred
    to Bacchus. Union of opposites; tiny marks or drop if crowded.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- Center on the axis (col 23): the figure is frontal and near-symmetric, so place the
  body sprite about col 23 and let the spiral shell radiate from the axis. Verify with
  `--axis`. The classic bug tell is a centred head over a body that leans a few columns
  left. The great ellipse of loops IS bilaterally symmetric about the axis; build it with
  `PM`/`PMB` about `AXIS = 23.0` and break it behind the figure and the creatures.
- Cells are 1:2 - draw the enclosing spiral ellipse and the winged sun ~2:1 WIDER than
  tall, or the loops render as a standing hoop and the sun as a squashed egg.
- COLOR is closer to the hero here than on any other card, but resist over-saturating.
  Crowley wrote "rainbow-hued spirals"; Harris executed them as pale silver-white coils.
  Read the spiral as a luminous silvery whorl with faint prismatic edges, warm gold
  ground behind, hot orange accents (tiger, boots, flame), cool blue-green at the coins
  and water. A clean banded/luminous spiral beats a muddy rainbow.
- The single most important read: a horned green figure bursting out with arms wide at
  the centre of a great silvery spiral, a golden sun at his groin, a dove above, a tiger
  at his thigh, a crocodile in the water below.
- Density trap: the Fool is the deck's densest scene, DuQuette calls it a whirlwind and
  says many symbols "reveal themselves only after long meditation (and the aid of a
  magnifying glass)." At 47 wide you cannot draw all ten elements and stay legible: nail
  1-4, add 5-7 as clear small creatures, render 8-10 as tiny marks or drop them.
- Palette: a GOLD lozenge ground, a GREEN figure with a GOLD horned head, pale
  SILVER-WHITE spiral loops, ORANGE tiger and boots, PURPLE grapes, pale BLUE-GREEN
  coins, a GREEN crocodile in BLUE water. The `.ans` should read bright and airy, unlike
  any night card.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu 0):** "In spiritual matters, the Fool means
idea, thought, spirituality, that which endeavours to transcend earth. In material
matters, it may, if badly dignified, mean folly, eccentricity, or even mania. But the
essential of this card is that it represents an original, subtle, sudden impulse or
impact, coming from a completely strange quarter. All such impulses are right, if
rightly received; and the good or ill interpretation of the card depends entirely on the
right attitude of the Querent."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Know Naught! All ways are lawful to innocence.
Pure folly is the key to Initiation. Silence breaks into Rapture. Be neither man nor
woman, but both in one. Be silent, Babe in the Egg of Blue, that thou mayest grow to
bear the Lance and Graal! Wander alone, and sing! In the King's palace his daughter
awaits thee."

**Upright (derived; no esotericmeanings tutorial on file):** A beginning that arrives
out of nowhere. An original, subtle, sudden impulse from a completely strange quarter,
which is right if it is received rightly; the leap taken before it can be justified, in
innocence and trust rather than calculation. Pure potential, spirit endeavouring to
transcend earth, the fresh green of spring, divine madness and ecstasy held without
shame. The zero that contains all: entering a thing empty-handed and open, "both feet
in air," and finding the whole universe already in the satchel. Silence that breaks into
rapture; the fool's freedom to wander alone and sing.

**Reversed / lower expression:** The same impulse badly received or badly dignified -
folly, eccentricity, or even mania; recklessness mistaken for freedom, the step taken
blindly rather than in trust. Scattered air with no earth to land on; a beginning
refused, or begun and abandoned. Innocence used as an excuse, silence gone empty rather
than pregnant. The querent's own wrong attitude toward a right impulse, which is what
turns the card ill.

**BoT texture:** "The Fool is of the gold of air. He has the horns of Dionysus Zagreus,
and between them is the phallic cone of white light representing the influence from the
Crown upon him." He dawns from space against a background of air, in the attitude of one
bursting unexpectedly upon the world, clad in green after the tradition of Spring, his
shoes of the phallic gold of the sun. In his right hand the wand tipped with a white
pyramid of the All-Father; in his left the flaming pine-cone of vegetable growth; from
his left shoulder a bunch of purple grapes (fertility, sweetness, the basis of ecstasy),
their stem developing into rainbow-hued spirals, the Form of the Universe, the Threefold
Veil of the Negative manifesting by his intervention in divided light. On the spiral
whorl are further attributions of godhead: the vulture of Maut, the dove of Venus, the
ivy of his devotees, the butterfly of many-coloured air, and the winged globe with its
twin serpents, echoed by twin infants embracing on the middle spiral, with the
benediction of three flowers in one hanging above. Fawning on him is the tiger; beneath
his feet in the Nile with its lotus stems crouches the crocodile. "Resuming all his many
forms and many-coloured images in the centre of the figure, the focus of the microcosm
is the radiant sun. The whole picture is a glyph of the creative light." Elsewhere
Crowley names him the Great Fool of the Celts and the Sagas, coming in green like Spring,
Parsifal "der reine Thor," and the Image of Nuit; "Pure Folly is the key to Initiation."

**DuQuette:** "The Spirit of the Aether," Elemental Trump of Air, Aleph ("ox"), Path 11
Kether -> Chokmah. He opens the Fool by quoting the Universe: the Fool symbolizes the
beginning of the Great Work exactly as the Universe symbolizes its completion, "the
negative issuing into manifestation." In essence there are not twenty-two trumps but one,
the Fool, and all the others live inside him. He is Key 0, not Key 1: his first and
greatest trick is creating the one, and everything else, out of nothing; "the Fool is
more than God, the Fool is the nothing we refer to when we say nothing is greater than
God." Harris's Fool has both feet planted unfirmly in air, not the cliff-edge vagabond of
tradition. He is the pagan Green Man of spring, Parsifal the pure fool who wins the Grail,
Hoor-pa-kraat the innocent lord of silence treading on the crocodile-god Sebek, and the
secret essence of the cosmos worshipped as Dionysus Zagreus and Bacchus Diphues; silent
like Harpocrates, like Dionysus before Pentheus, like Christ before Pilate (and, in two
drafts Crowley rejected, unmistakably like Harpo Marx). He bursts into midair from behind
three swirling rings that issue from and return to his heart, the three Veils of
Negativity (Ain, Ain Soph, Ain Soph Aur); his satchel holds the entire universe as
planetary and zodiacal coins. He is the Holy Spirit itself: the dove, the butterfly of
transformation, the winged Mercurial globe, and the vulture-goddess Maut pour from the
Holy Grail in his right hand. DuQuette closes on the Sun covering the groin and the almost
invisible Moon above the crocodile, with the twin infants embracing between them - likely
an allusion to the sexual alchemy of "the creative light."

## Expected Harris palette (scan at reference/00-fool-card.jpg)

- Head: GOLD-YELLOW face, wide pale eyes, a broad grin, two gold Dionysus HORNS with a
  small pale phallic cone of light rising between them
- Body: mid to deep GREEN, leaf/scale texture on the torso, arms raised
- Feet: hot ORANGE-GOLD boots at lower left and right, toes pointing outward
- Ground: a bright GOLD field patterned with pale lozenges / diamonds behind the figure
- Spiral: pale SILVER-WHITE loops with faint blue and iridescent edges, sweeping in a
  great ellipse around the whole figure and touching all four edges
- Groin: a small GOLD winged SUN disk
- Upper left: a white DOVE and a small BLUE bird; a crystal / diamond wand tipped white
- Upper right: ORANGE-red FLAME and a hand; the wand of the All-Father
- Right: a dark PURPLE-GREEN grape cluster over a hive of pale BLUE-GREEN coins marked
  with black planetary and zodiacal glyphs
- Mid: a small tan / brown bird and corn near his hands; twin pale infants faint on the
  middle loop; a pale ORANGE-gold BUTTERFLY at the left
- Lower right: an ORANGE TIGER, leaping, head low at his thigh
- Foot of card: a GREEN CROCODILE in pale BLUE water with lotus stems and a pink lotus;
  a faint MOON above it; pale flowers ("three in one") hanging at lower centre
- Frame: pale warm gray art deco; `0` plaque top, "The Fool" band with the Aleph and the
  air-triangle glyphs, "TRUMPS" faint behind the title
