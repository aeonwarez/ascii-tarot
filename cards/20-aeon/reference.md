# XX - The Aeon

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the project sources - Crowley's *Book
of Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the Harris scan
(`reference/20-aeon-card.jpg`). No esotericmeanings.com tutorial exists for this
card; the Upright / Reversed readings below are derived from Crowley's
divinatory line plus DuQuette's Aeon-of-Horus commentary.

## Element ranking (BoT p.115-116 + DuQuette Atu XX + the painting)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **Hoor-pa-kraat, the great transparent child** - the hero read: a large milky
   TRANSLUCENT child standing full height on the axis, feet at the very bottom
   edge, finger to his lips in the sign of SILENCE, head shaven but for the
   Horus lock and crowned with two Uraeus serpents. Everything behind him shows
   THROUGH him. Body/spine on col 23; drawn on top, but see-through.
2. **Ra-Hoor-Khuit enthroned inside the egg** - the small hawk-headed active god
   seated on his throne at the child's chest height, ON the axis, phoenix wand in
   the right hand, left hand empty, coming forth in golden light. He is inside
   the child, not beside him: the twofold god read in one column.
3. **Nuit arching over the whole card** - the star-goddess, deep blue body filled
   with stars and breasts alive with the milk of whirling galaxies, bending in a
   great ecstatic OMEGA over the top of the card and down both sides to the
   mid-card, enclosing everything. She is the frame, not an ornament.
4. **The letter Shin at the foot, with the three figures** - a large flame-shaped
   SHIN drawn like a flower across the bottom, its three Yod-prongs occupied by
   three small human/embryonic figures rising to partake in the Essence of the
   new Aeon (the three risen dead of the old Last Judgment, reinterpreted).

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **Hadit, the winged solar globe** - a globe of fire, eternal energy, WINGED to
   show his power of Going, set beneath Nuit's heart and nearly camouflaged by
   the whole scene: in the painting a pair of gold-and-orange wings spread wide
   and low across the card. "In the sphere I am everywhere the centre, as she,
   the circumference, is nowhere found."
6. **The golden egg / mandorla of light** - concentric bands of gold, emerald and
   orange forming an immense egg around the enthroned Ra-Hoor-Khuit, drawn ~2:1
   wider than tall, dithered as a glow and never as an outline. The womb of the
   Aeon inside the child's body.
7. **The scarlet ground** - the field behind everything is glowing orange-scarlet
   (the King-scale of Fire), not sky-dark. The card is a fire card; keep the
   background hot right out to the border.

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The stylized scales behind the Shin** - a very stylized set of scales / the
   sign of LIBRA behind the letter, foreshadowing the Aeon to follow this one:
   "the fall of the Great Equinox; when Hrumachis shall arise and the
   double-wanded one assume my throne and place." Maat's balance, held faint.
9. **Nuit's stars** - scatter stars through her deep-blue arching body; "Every man
   and every woman is a star" is the doctrine the card exists to state.
10. **The two Uraeus serpents and the Horus lock** - tiny marks at the child's
    crown; the serpent heads reading left and right off the top of the head,
    the sidelock falling past the ear.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- Strongly axial and near-symmetric: child on the axis, throne on the axis, Shin
  centred at the base, Nuit's arch symmetric about col 23. A `--axis` card. Nuit's
  arch is the one place to allow asymmetry (the painting's coils are heavier on the
  right), so place her by mirror-offset from col 23, never by left edge.
- The hard problem is TRANSPARENCY, which inverts the standard occlusion rule: the
  foreground child must NOT erase what is behind him. Draw the throne, the egg and
  the wings first at full density, then lay the child over them as a light outline
  plus a sparse veil (`.` `:` `'`), so the enthroned god reads THROUGH the body.
  This is the only card in the run where the front figure is the thinnest mark.
- Cells are 1:2 - draw the egg/mandorla and Hadit's globe ~2:1 wider than tall as
  dithered ramps, and let Nuit's arch be a wide flattened omega, not a circle.
- The single most important read: a huge see-through child with a finger to his
  lips, a small enthroned hawk-god glowing inside his chest, a star-goddess arching
  over the whole card, a flaming Shin with three little figures at the feet.
- Three tiers of scale must survive at 47 wide: Nuit (card-sized), the child
  (figure-sized), Ra-Hoor-Khuit and the three Yod-figures (sprite-sized). If the
  small figures blur, lose detail elsewhere, not them.
- Palette: TEAL-BLUE Nuit, SCARLET ground, GOLD/EMERALD egg, WHITE translucent
  child, GOLD-ORANGE wings. The `.ans` should read hotter and more saturated than
  any neighbour - the opposite pole from the Moon's indigo murk.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu XX):** "Final decision in respect of the
past, new current in respect of the future; always represents the taking of a
definite step."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Be every Act an Act of Love and Worship.
Be every Act the Fiat of a God. Be every Act a Source of radiant Glory."

**Upright (derived from BoT + DuQuette):** The Equinox of the Gods in a personal
key - an old order in you has already ended and the new current is here whether or
not it has been named. Judgment, but self-judgment: you pass sentence on your own
past and step. A decision that cannot be un-made, taken cleanly and without
regret; the end of an identity that was built on sacrifice, atonement, and waiting
for permission. Fire, force, and the innocence of the child: acting from your own
radiance rather than from a debt owed. Announcement, awakening, rebirth, the call
you answer.

**Reversed / lower expression:** Reading the change as apocalypse instead of
birth - the fear of those "who cannot accept the possibility that old spiritual
points of view can ever be replaced by new ones." Clinging to a dead Aeon; guilt,
atonement, and self-sacrifice used as a hiding place. Refusing the definite step,
or taking it half-way and calling it done. The fire misused: force without
silence, Ra-Hoor-Khuit without Hoor-pa-kraat, noise and violence in place of the
finger at the lips. Dark-ages exhaustion; judging others by a law that has already
lapsed.

**BoT texture:** The old card was The Angel, or The Last Judgment: an Angel
blowing a trumpet with a flag bearing the symbol of the Aeon of Osiris, the graves
opening below and three of the dead rising, the central one forming the letter
Shin with his arms. That card meant the destruction of the world by Fire, "and
this was accomplished in the year of the vulgar era 1904," when the fiery god
Horus took the airy god Osiris's place in the East as Hierophant. So the new card
becomes an adaptation of the Stele of Revealing: Nuith around the top, the category
of unlimited possibility; her mate Hadit, the ubiquitous point-of-view, a winged
globe of fire; and of their marriage the child Horus born, known under his special
name Heru-ra-ha, extraverted as Ra-hoor-khuit and introverted as Hoor-pa-kraat,
solar and therefore shown coming forth in golden light. The name Heru is identical
with Hru, the great Angel set over the Tarot, so the deck itself may be read as a
series of illustrations to the Book of the Law. At the bottom, Shin as a flower
with three Yods holding three human figures rising into the Essence of the new
Aeon, and behind it Libra, the Aeon still to come. Crowley closes with the
warning that an Aeon's birth is marked by concentrated political power, better
travel and communication, advancing science and a need for religious consolidation,
and that 500 years of Dark Ages are likely upon us: "Fortunately, to-day we have
brighter torches and more torch-bearers."

**DuQuette:** "The Spirit of the Primal Fire," elemental trump of Fire and of
Spirit, Shin ("tooth"), Path 31 Hod -> Malkuth. The traditional Judgment trump is
obsolete because the event it depicted has already occurred: the world destroyed
by fire was the spiritual Age of Osiris, and the Aeon card shows the forces that
began the age after it. Horus is the god of Force and Fire; his ascension only
looks like the end of the world. The whole thing is a magical changing of the
guard, foreshadowed by the Golden Dawn's six-monthly rotation of officers, which
the Book of the Law calls the Equinox of the Gods (Cairo, March 1904). Harris
interprets the stele: Nuit, body filled with stars, breasts alive with the milk of
whirling galaxies, forming a beautiful blue omega as she bends in ecstasy around an
immense egg containing the seated Ra-Hoor-Khuit, phoenix wand in the right hand,
left hand empty; before him, as a transparent essence, his twin Hoor-pa-kraat,
finger at his lips, head shaven but for the Horus lock, crowned with two Uraeus
serpents; Hadit the winged solar disk nearly camouflaged by the whole scene, which
suits him. A large Shin containing three embryonic figures flames at the bottom in
front of a very stylized set of scales, perhaps pointing to the Aeon of Maat to
follow. The formula being replaced is sacrifice; the formula replacing it is the
self-radiant, ever-living sun.

## Expected Harris palette (scan at reference/20-aeon-card.jpg)

- Ground: hot ORANGE-SCARLET filling the field, edge to edge
- Nuit: deep TEAL-BLUE, arching across the top and curving down both sides to
  mid-card, ending in bracelet-marked hands; dark green-blue coils upper right
- Egg / mandorla: concentric GOLD-YELLOW, EMERALD and ORANGE bands behind the
  throne, brightest gold at the centre
- Ra-Hoor-Khuit: small seated figure in GREEN and GOLD on a pale throne, teal
  head, red-and-gold wand at the right hand
- Hoor-pa-kraat: milky WHITE / pale cream translucent body over everything, legs
  tinted teal toward the feet, a small RED-PINK hand at the mouth
- Hadit: GOLD and ORANGE wings spread wide and low across the lower third
- Base: golden Shin with three small TAWNY-ORANGE embryonic figures; faint scales
- Frame: pale warm gray art deco; `XX` plaque top, "The Aeon" band with the Shin
  glyph and the fire triangle
