Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the three project sources -

## Element ranking (BoT p.88-90 + DuQuette Atu IX + esotericmeanings)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The cowled Hermit, Yod-shaped, on the axis** - an ancient hooded PROPHET
   bent in the shape of the letter YOD, IBIS-headed (Thoth / Mercury), only his
   HAND visible, turned inward and downward in contemplation. He is the seed of
   Wisdom in man; centered on col 23.
2. **The Lantern with the Sun at its heart, held aloft** - a DIAMOND lantern
   whose center is the SUN (the Sigil of the King of Fire), raised in his hand,
   casting FOUR yellow rays. The card's light-source and hero read; keep it lit
   and high.
3. **The green Orphic egg + iridescent serpent** - before him, the green (Venus)
   Orphic EGG wound by a MANY-COLOURED serpent (Mercury-iridescent), the
   subjective universe he gazes into to know himself. Low and forward of him.
4. **The spermatozoon-wand (the Logos / seed of life)** - his WAND is a
   spermatozoon (the Father's physical representative), a homunculus / foetus
   coiled within it and a KETHER-diamond at its head, lower-left, lit by a ray.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **Cerberus, the three-headed hound, tamed** - at his heel the three-headed dog
   (speech / thought / action of the lower self), tamed and following - the lower
   nature raised to serve the higher.
6. **The field of wheat** - a background of golden WHEAT (Virgo, Persephone,
   agriculture): inner wisdom grown slowly like a crop, surrounding him.
7. **The rays and the pyramid of light** - the lantern's four yellow rays touch
   the spermatozoon, the apex of a PYRAMID (the phallus energised), Cerberus, and
   the egg; two separate WHITE rays cross the top (not from the lamp), the upper
   forming the pyramid, the lower the horizon.

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The ibis head of Thoth** - the god of the written Word; keep the head-profile
   distinctly ibis / beaked if space allows.
9. **The diamond motifs** - Kether-diamonds in the lantern and in the
   spermatozoon; the vesica/diamond thread that runs through Fool → Hermit.
10. **The slate-and-plum, green-grey palette** - keep the field cool, earthy,
    yellowish-green with slate and plum, the wheat the one warm gold.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- Frontal-ish but built on the vertical Yod spine: cowl-peak → ibis head →
  lantern-hand → egg → toe, all read down col 23; the wheat field and rays kept
  balanced about `AXIS = 23.0`. Less strictly mirror-symmetric than VII/VIII -
  the read is a single stooped figure, not a mirrored shrine.
- The single most important read: a hooded figure holding a glowing lantern,
  bent over a luminous egg, in a field of wheat. Vertical spine =
  cowl → ibis head → lantern → egg.
- Value-light the lantern's Sun-core brightest; let the four yellow rays actually
  reach the egg (bottom), the pyramid-apex (top), the sperm-wand (lower-left) and
  Cerberus so the "completed cycle" reads.
- Keep Cerberus small and low at his heel; the sperm-wand a small lit mark
  lower-left, not a second focus.
- Palette in the `.ans`: slate / green-grey figure, golden wheat, a bright
  Sun-lantern with yellow rays, a green egg, a pale sperm-wand.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu IX):** "Illumination from within, secret
impulse from within; practical plans derived accordingly. Retirement from
participation in current events."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Wander alone; bearing the Light and thy
Staff. And be the Light so bright that no man seeth thee. Be not moved by aught
without or within: keep Silence in all ways."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Getting away from a situation to gain better
insight into it; illumination (the lamp) where you were previously in the dark.
Time alone, withdrawing to your still inner centre; soul-searching for what gives
life meaning. The need to get in touch with untapped wisdom.

**Reversed (esotericmeanings):** Too isolated / introspective - the need to get
out into the world; so self-absorbed you ignore others' needs. Out of touch with
your inner guide. Too much secrecy, not open about yourself. Single but wanting a
relationship, or in one without enough space for personal reflection.

**BoT texture:** Yod, the seed-letter, the Father, the highest Mercury and Logos;
his physical representative the spermatozoon - hence the card. Virgo (Mercury
rules and is exalted). The figure is shaped like Yod, contemplates the Orphic egg
(the essence of all life hidden), wound by the many-coloured serpent (the
mercurial nature). He bears the Lamp whose center is the Sun; his wand a
spermatozoon; Cerberus follows, tamed. The Hermit is the psychopompos, the guide
of the soul through the lower worlds. Virgo also connects him to Persephone and to
the wheat of the fields.

**DuQuette:** "The Prophet of the Eternal, the Magus of the Voice of Power." Yod
("hand"), Virgo (Mercury rules and exalted), Path 20 Chesed→Tiphareth. The Hermit
is the solitary Mercury / Logos, the creative Word rendered as the spermatozoon
(Hartsoecker's 1694 homunculus curled inside it). He holds the Lamp (the Sun / the
light of the higher self), leans on the Serpent-wand, and is followed by the
tamed three-headed hound. The whole card is the wisdom that grows in silence and
solitude, the light carried so bright "that no man seeth thee."
