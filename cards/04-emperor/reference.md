# IV — The Emperor

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the three project sources —
Crowley's *Book of Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the
esotericmeanings.com tutorial — plus the color card scan.

## Element ranking (BoT p.77-78 + DuQuette Atu IV + esotericmeanings + scan)

Tier 1 — the card IS these:
1. **The enthroned crowned Emperor + Sulphur posture** — a crowned male figure
   in imperial vestments and regalia, seated frontal, facing (toward the
   Empress). His whole body forms the alchemical glyph of SULPHUR: arms + head
   an upright TRIANGLE, crossed legs the CROSS below. The still active axis of
   the card.
2. **The Ram throne** — the throne's capitals are the heads of the Himalayan
   wild RAM (Aries = a Ram). Confirmed on the lower plane by the Lamb & Flag at
   his feet.
3. **Dominant red / fire field** — scarlet, brilliant flame, glowing red
   everywhere = Mars in Aries, and Sulphur the fiery element. Sudden, volatile,
   authoritative. The diagonal white light descends from the upper right.
4. **Sceptre + orb** — a SCEPTRE surmounted by a RAM'S head (held to catch the
   light from the right, channelled to the shield); an ORB surmounted by a
   MALTESE CROSS in his left hand over the navel / procreative chakra (energy
   reached a successful issue; government established; his creative rulership).

Tier 2 — named and emphasized:
5. **The shield: red double-headed eagle + crimson disk** — a two-headed
   golden/red eagle crowned with a crimson disk = the alchemical RED TINCTURE,
   of the nature of the Sun and GOLD; consort to the Empress's white eagle
   (Moon, silver). The recipe: find the white tincture, then the red, then
   unite them (Atu XIV, Art). The left corner of the shield is markedly brighter
   than the right.
6. **The Lamb and Flag (Agnus Dei), couchant at his feet** — the tamed ram: the
   docile, subservient Lamb of God, blood-sacrifice of the Old Testament / Aeon
   of Osiris, having lost its wild independence. "This is the theory of
   government."
7. **The four-point crown + Chesed / the number four** — a gold crown of 4
   points, jewels of 4 facets; his legs also form a Four = CHESED (the paternal
   / Jupiter sephira). The gold crown = the Sun (Sol exalted in Aries) behind
   him.
8. **Star disks on the throne arms** — a star on each arm of the throne with 16
   points = the atomic number of Sulphur = the gematria of ChACh ("hook,"
   Tzaddi).

Tier 3 — atmosphere / as space allows:
9. **Bees + fleur-de-lys** — paternal power symbols on the card: bees (energy,
   industriousness, organised rulership) and the fleur-de-lys (monarchic
   rulership).
10. **Looping lines terminating in arrowheads** — directed energy driven to
    specific tasks; the Emperor wastes nothing.
11. **The diagonal white light** from the upper right — Harris's placement that
    triggered the Tzaddi/Heh debate; keep it as a shaft, do not omit.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- The Emperor is the fiery MASCULINE counterweight to the Empress's cool green:
  angular, red, upright, martial. Where she was soft vegetal masses, he is hard
  flame and cut stone.
- Body = the Sulphur glyph: read the head+shoulders as a triangle, the crossed
  legs as an X/cross below. Keep the silhouette geometric.
- Crown: 4-point gold crown; a Sun disk / glow behind the head.
- Throne: two RAM heads as the capitals (curled horns `((` `))`); a 16-point
  star disk on each arm.
- Right hand: ram-headed sceptre catching a diagonal light shaft from upper
  right; left hand: orb + Maltese cross at the navel.
- Lower area: the heater SHIELD with a two-headed eagle + crimson disk (bright
  left, dark right); the white LAMB & FLAG couchant at his feet.
- Robe: bees + fleur-de-lys + looping arrow-tipped lines.
- Palette in the `.ans`: scarlet / flame-red field, gold crown+regalia, crimson
  shield disk, white lamb, a white diagonal light shaft.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, p.290):** "War, conquest, victory, strife,
ambition, originality, overweening confidence and megalomania, quarrelsomeness,
energy, vigour, stubbornness, impracticability, rashness, ill-temper."

**DuQuette verse (title-page):** "Use all thine energy to rule thy thought:
burn up thy thought as the Phoenix."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Taking responsibility for others (family relying
on you for security); a natural, fair leader and organiser who prefers to be
their own boss; break problems into manageable chunks and work systematically;
someone who has lived many life lessons and can help others; success through
self-discipline, logic, rationality; learning to sacrifice yourself for a
greater cause — only by surrender do we gain spiritually.

**Reversed (esotericmeanings):** Not sticking to goals; lacking focus and
discipline; under an overly dominant figure who stifles creativity; an
overbearing, possessive partner; poor relationship with the father;
over-intellectualising; lack of ambition and direction; wanting to break free
from rules; viewing the spiritual path as what you can get not give;
over-controlling, cruel, violent or insecure; suppressing the feminine/softer
side.

**BoT texture:** Attributed to Tzaddi, referring to Aries (ruled by Mars, Sun
exalted) — energy in its most material form joined to authority (TZ/TS roots:
Cæsar, Tsar, Senate, Signor, Seigneur). Crowned male on a throne of Himalayan
ram-heads, Lamb & Flag couchant — the wild ram tamed into "the docile, cowardly,
gregarious and succulent beast. This is the theory of government." One of the
alchemical cards: with Atu II and III he makes the triad Mercury-Salt-Sulphur;
his arms+head = triangle, crossed legs = cross = SULPHUR, the male fiery Rajas,
the initiative of all Being — sudden, violent, impermanent; if it persists it
burns and destroys (distinguish from the Creative Energy of Aleph/Beth: this
card is below the Abyss). Sceptre with ram's head; orb + Maltese cross =
government established. Shield: two-headed eagle + crimson disk = the red
tincture (gold/Sun) to the Empress's white eagle (silver/Moon). The white light
descending indicates his authority derived from Chokmah, exerted on Tiphareth.

**DuQuette:** "Sun of the Morning, chief among the Mighty." Aries, Mars rules,
Sol exalted. He wrestles openly with the "Tzaddi is not the Star" swap: the
Emperor is Atu IV, sign Aries, letter Tzaddi, path 28 (Netzach→Yesod) per
Crowley's charts — yet BoT's closing paragraph (white light from Chokmah onto
Tiphareth) describes the traditional Heh path the Star should occupy. His read:
Harris painted from the traditional placement; Crowley's words neglected to say
so. The third alchemical trump = Sulphur (arms=triangle, legs=cross). Red eagle
on the shield = red tincture (Sun/gold); the great recipe = find the white
tincture, then the red, then unite them — the Empress and Emperor are far more
intimate than mere officials; they get cozy in Atu XIV, Art.
