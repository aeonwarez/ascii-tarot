# VII - The Chariot

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the three project sources -
Crowley's *Book of Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the

## Element ranking (BoT p.85-86 + DuQuette Atu VII + esotericmeanings)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The armoured Charioteer, throned frontal on the axis** - a King in full
   AMBER armour, no flesh exposed, VIZOR lowered ("no man may look upon his face
   and live"), in a MEDITATIVE posture. He is throned, not driving; he holds no
   reins. Centered on col 23, symmetric.
2. **The Holy Grail, borne in his hands, center** - the card's most important
   feature: a pure AMETHYST cup (Jupiter) shaped like the FULL MOON / the Great
   Sea of Binah, RADIANT BLOOD at its center whose rays REVOLVE. His one true
   function is to bear it; keep it central and glowing.
3. **The four sphinxes drawing the chariot** - below/in front, FOUR sphinxes
   composed of the four Kerubs (Bull, Lion, Eagle, Man) with elements
   counterchanged in each = the 16 sub-elements. Two dark, two light; mirror them
   in pairs about the axis.
4. **The starry blue canopy with ABRAHADABRA** - a night-sky-BLUE canopy of
   Binah, starry, spanning the top, with the word ABRAHADABRA lettered across it
   (drawn as ABRACADABRA - the Harris typo). The lid of the shrine-on-wheels.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The four pillars of the canopy** - the four pillars of the Universe / the
   regimen of Tetragrammaton holding up the canopy. Mirror two per side.
6. **The scarlet wheels** - SCARLET wheels (Geburah / Mars), the original energy
   causing the revolving motion. One to each side of the car.
7. **The crab crest + ten stars of armour** - a CRAB crest on the helmet
   (Cancer); TEN Stars / sapphires of Assiah studding the amber armour (the
   celestial dew inherited from his mother Binah).

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **Red cloak over white** - a RED cloak (passion, Geburah) with WHITE beneath
   (purity of purpose).
9. **The Moon he is seated on** - the Moon under him (not visible), riding above
   the tides of emotion; imply it in the base.
10. **The dark maroon/russet structure** - keep the car body warm and heavy:
    maroon, rich russet, dark greenish brown, against the blue canopy.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- STRICTLY FRONTAL and symmetric - a stationary throne, not a racing chariot.
  Charioteer's spine, helmet crest, Grail and canopy peak all on col 23; mirror
  the four pillars, the two wheels and the sphinx-pairs about `AXIS = 23.0`.
- The single most important read: an armoured seated figure holding a glowing
  cup at his center, under a starry canopy, over four sphinxes. Vertical spine =
  crab-crest → visor → Grail → sphinxes.
- Contrast the sphinxes by value (two dark, two light, counterchanged) so the
  quartet reads as the four Kerubs, not a blur; keep them small and low.
- Letter a short ABRAHADABRA band along the canopy if space; otherwise imply it.
- Palette in the `.ans`: amber armour, deep-blue starry canopy, scarlet wheels,
  amethyst/violet Grail with a red center, black-and-white sphinxes.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu VII):** "Triumph, victory, hope, memory,
digestion, violence in maintaining traditional ideas, the 'die-hard,'
ruthlessness, lust of destruction, obedience, faithfulness, authority under
authority."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "The Issue of the Vulture, Two-in-One,
conveyed; this is the Chariot of Power. TRINC: the last oracle."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Victory through unwavering progress and dominion
over wild forces (the sphinxes). Faith in going with the flow of the universe
(the Charioteer does not steer); the path will have challenges, like a river
routed around obstacles. Not passive - inner strength and determination (the
meditative posture); act toward a chosen goal, even out of your comfort zone,
protected by the armour of inner confidence.

**Reversed (esotericmeanings):** Slow down; make sure actions rest on solid
foundations; don't get out of your depth too fast. A tendency to over-control
(forcing the sphinxes), hampering the natural flow - ease off, loosen the reins.
Connect with what you truly think and feel and align with the forces pulling you
before acting.

**BoT texture:** Cancer, the house of the Moon (analogies to the High Priestess),
with Jupiter exalted (analogies to Fortune). Canopy = the night-sky-blue of
Binah; four pillars = the regimen of Tetragrammaton; scarlet wheels = the energy
of Geburah causing the revolving motion. Four sphinxes of the four Kerubs,
counterchanged = the sixteen sub-elements. The Charioteer in amber armour is
throned rather than conducting, "because the whole system of progression is
perfectly balanced"; his only function is to bear the Holy Grail. Ten Stars of
Assiah on his armour (celestial dew from his mother); crab crest; visor lowered,
no part of the body exposed. The Grail is amethyst (Jupiter), shaped like the
full moon and the Great Sea of Binah; in its center radiant blood, the rays
revolving - spiritual life, light in the darkness.

**DuQuette:** "The Child of the Powers of the Waters, the Lord of the Triumph of
Light." Cheth ("fence") = 418 = ABRAHADABRA (embroidered, with a typo, on the
canopy). Cancer (Luna rules, Jupiter exalted), Path 18 Binah→Geburah, crossing
the Abyss - its mysteries are among the most sublime on the Tree. The Grail bears
the "Two-in-One": the elixir brewed in the Holy Grail by Babalon/Nuit in Binah,
offered to the Beast/Hadit in Chokmah - both drink (TRINC) and annihilate
separateness. "The Issue of the Vulture" - the vulture (sacred to Maat) was said
to conceive without mating, the child of an internal marriage of two balanced
things. Cancer's glyph read as a technique of tantric yoga: male and female
energies balanced and exchanged to make the Two-in-One elixir, then conveyed as a
eucharistic talisman.
