## Element ranking (BoT p.90-92 + DuQuette Atu X + esotericmeanings)

Tier 1 — the card IS these:
1. **The great ten-spoked Wheel, centered** — a WHEEL of ten spokes (the ten
   Sephiroth / Malkuth = manifestation) spinning counter-clockwise, its hub on the
   axis; the whole card revolves about col 23. The hub = Kether / Hadit / the
   point, often a Sun-symbol; "the axle moveth not."
2. **The Sphinx at the top** — a golden SPHINX (SULPHUR, the four Kerubs
   composited) crowning the wheel, a SWORD upright between its lion-paws (mind /
   willpower); the summit of the wheel, dead-center on the axis.
3. **Hermanubis ascending (left) + Typhon descending (right)** — on the LEFT a
   blue MERCURY-ape / Hermanubis (Anubis + Hermes, the psychopomp) CLIMBING; on the
   RIGHT the red-SALT crocodile-headed TYPHON (inverted Ankh, death / stagnation)
   DESCENDING. Mirror the pair about the axis, one rising one falling.
4. **The firmament of distorted stars + churning plumes** — above, a FIRMAMENT of
   distorted five-pointed STARS (Nuit, the cosmic clock; "every man and woman is a
   star") issuing LIGHTNINGS; the whole set in a whirlpool of blue-violet PLUMES
   drawn out by the spin.

Tier 2 — named and emphasized:
5. **The three gunas read as three tinctures** — value/color-separate the three
   riders so Sulphur (Sphinx, bright/gold), Mercury (Hermanubis, blue) and Salt
   (Typhon, red/dark) read as the three principles, not a blur.
6. **The triangle behind the wheel** — a great TRIANGLE (apex up) behind/within
   the wheel, the three gunas stabilised; the hub sits in its center (the
   all-seeing eye / *Centrum in Centri Trigono*) = enlightenment, clarity.
7. **Lightnings striking Typhon** — Jupiter's LIGHTNING bolts lance down, striking
   the descending Typhon on the right.

Tier 3 — atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The ten spokes = the ten Sephiroth** — keep exactly ten spokes if legible;
   a 10-pointed star may echo in upper and lower wheel ("as above, so below").
9. **The hand / fist of fortune** — Kaph = the closed hand; a fist with six rays
   may turn the wheel from below (the hand of God); include only if space allows.
10. **The electric blue-violet ground** — keep the field cool, stormy, Jupiterian:
    violet, rich purple, bright blue rayed with yellow lightning.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- Radial, not bilateral, but still centered: the hub on col 23, the Sphinx's crown
  and sword on the axis, Hermanubis (left, rising) mirrored against Typhon (right,
  falling). Verify the hub and Sphinx sit dead on `AXIS = 23.0` with `--axis`.
- Cells are 1:2 — draw the wheel-rim as a ~2:1 wide ELLIPSE, not a tall circle,
  or it will read as an egg.
- The single most important read: a great spoked wheel with a sphinx on top and
  two beast-figures climbing/falling on its sides, under a starry storm.
- Value-separate the three riders (bright Sphinx, blue Hermanubis, red Typhon) and
  keep the spokes clean so the wheel reads as a wheel, the storm as plumes.
- Palette in the `.ans`: blue-violet field, a bright/gold sphinx, a blue ascending
  ape, a red descending crocodile, yellow lightning, a purple triangle.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu X):** "Change of fortune (generally good
fortune, as the card is generally beneficent)."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Follow thy Fortune, careless where it lead
thee! The axle moveth not: attain thou that."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Be ready to act on unexpected opportunities. Go
with the flow; let good fortune come; don't cling to the status quo. Enjoy good
times without complacency; in hard times don't despair (things can only get
better). Keep optimistic, have faith in the Universe. Change is coming soon; you
may need to alter course. Struggle against the flow and you struggle to ascend the
Wheel, or are crushed beneath it.

**BoT texture:** Jupiter, the Greater Fortune. The wheel of ten spokes = the ten
Sephiroth of Malkuth, the manifested universe. Three figures: the Sphinx (Sulphur,
sword upright between its paws), Hermanubis (Mercury, the ape, ascending), Typhon
(Salt, descending) — the three gunas of the Hindus (Sattvas, Rajas, Tamas). Above,
the firmament of distorted stars, the cosmic clock, issuing lightnings; the plumes
churn as the wheel drives blue and violet whirlpools. The whole is the mechanism of
the universe: perpetual, impersonal, generally beneficent; the wise stay at the
motionless axle.

**DuQuette:** "The Lord of the Forces of Life." Kaph ("palm / fist"), Jupiter, Path
21 Chesed→Netzach. DuQuette notes the great TRIANGLE (apex up) behind the wheel,
the hub sitting within it (*Centrum in Centri Trigono*, the point in the center of
the triangle), and reads the three riders as the three gunas / alchemical
principles. Zeus/Jupiter as the Demiurge, meddling like Fortune / Karma in the
lives of men; the axle that does not move is Kether / Hadit / the point, the thing
to attain.
