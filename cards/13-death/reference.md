## Element ranking (BoT p.100-101 + DuQuette Atu XIII + esotericmeanings)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **Osiris the dancing black skeleton with the scythe** - the hero figure: a black
   skeleton DANCING (not standing) on the bed of the sea, sweeping a great scythe.
   His body/spine reads on col 23; the scythe sweeps diagonally across. Draw him ON
   TOP.
2. **The Saturnian scythe creating bubbles of new life** - the scythe (handle a Tau)
   is Saturnian; where it sweeps it raises BUBBLES in which new forms take shape -
   NOT mowing the living down. Keep the scythe reading as generative.
3. **The crown of Osiris + feathers of Maat** - on the skull, the crown of Upper
   Egypt topped with the feathers of Maat (as in Adjustment / 5 of Wands); marks him
   as Osiris in the waters of Amennti, not a generic reaper.
4. **The three forms of Scorpio around him** - SCORPION (lowest, lower right, between
   a lily/alpha and a lotus/omega), SERPENT (middle, lower left), EAGLE (highest,
   upper left, exalted, threads to the creative process). Give each a distinct
   silhouette in its corner.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The bubbles of soul-incarnations** - luminous bubbles rising from the scythe's
   sweep holding figures of various ages (an adult man, an adult woman, a child, a
   single sperm), threaded together - the phallus of Osiris (he creates with the
   phallus as he destroys with the scythe).
6. **The Saturn-glyph legs** - the skeleton's legs arranged to trace the glyph of
   SATURN; between them, the death-process figures (below).
7. **The beheading / rising-figure process** - two small figures at the legs: a
   LOWER one being beheaded as the scythe crosses its neck, an UPPER one rising from
   the head after death wrapped in bright light - the higher mind freed from the
   lower vehicles.

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The lily and lotus (alpha & omega)** - a lily left, a lotus right, Saturnian,
   shaped as alpha/omega, framing the scorpion (physical life trapped in time).
9. **The three states of water** - read Scorpion as ice, Serpent as water, Eagle as
   gas, if the corners allow the distinction.
10. **The murky underwater field** - keep the whole scene subaqueous: blue-green over
    brown/indigo depths, the black skeleton against it.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- Dynamic diagonal composition (a cousin of Lust): the skeleton's spine pinned to
  col 23, the SCYTHE sweeping across, the three Scorpio symbols anchoring the
  corners. Verify the spine sits on the axis guide with `--axis`; the classic tell
  is a centred skull over a body/scythe that leans.
- The single most important read: a DANCING skeleton with a scythe, underwater,
  raising bubbles - not a stiff reaper on land. Keep the pose active, knees bent,
  scythe mid-sweep.
- Cells are 1:2 - draw the bubbles and the eagle/scorpion bodies ~2:1 wider than
  tall; a cluster of small wide discs for the bubbles.
- Distinguish scorpion / serpent / eagle by silhouette in their corners; keep the
  crown + Maat feathers legible on the skull.
- This is a DARK card - the `.ans` should read murky blue-green and brown/indigo with
  a black skeleton and a few luminous bubble highlights, distinct from Lust's heat
  and the Hanged Man's green.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu XIII):** "Transformation, change, voluntary
or involuntary, in either case logical development of existing conditions, yet perhaps
sudden and unexpected. Apparent death or destruction, but such interpretation is
illusion."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "The Universe is Change; every Change is the
effect of an Act of Love; all Acts of Love contain Pure Joy. Die daily! Death is the
apex of one curve of the snake Life: behold all opposites as necessary complements,
and rejoice."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Something is dying/dissolving - a structure, pattern,
habit, or self-image - necessary for new beginnings; a period of great transformation,
facing your darkest fears (darkest hour before dawn); abandoning the outgrown; death
as protection against stagnation and servant to your evolution; a crisis met with
confidence; a time to cut away what does not serve.

**Reversed / lower expression:** Resistance to change, clinging to the past; a life
stagnated, stuck in a rut; unresolved issues holding you out of the present; feeling
trapped like the cornered scorpion; a fear of death needing higher insight.

**BoT texture:** Nun, the fish; Scorpio, ruled by Mars. Alchemically the card is
PUTREFACTION - developing the final form of life from the latent seed in the Orphic
egg. Scorpio's three forms: the Scorpion (lowest, stinging itself when ringed with
fire), the Serpent (middle, Lord of Life and Death, male energy, the rhythmical
undulation of life/death), the Eagle (highest, exaltation above solid matter, the
tenuous elements given off as vapour). The skeleton bearing a scythe is Saturnian:
Saturn = the indestructible essential structure, crowned with the crown of Osiris,
"the original secret male creative God," the Redeemer. Fish = Nun = Scorpio = Serpent;
"a compendium of universal energy in its most secret form."

**DuQuette:** "The Child of the Great Transformers," Path 24 Tiphareth→Netzach, Nun
(fish), Scorpio (Mars rules, Pluto exalted). "Something smells fishy" - the secret of
life is in the stinky putrefying stuff. The Death card is essentially the Art card's
cauldron: the caput mortuum (raven on skull) painted on Art's cauldron is a sign that
reads "this is the Death card." The snake-wrapped Orphic egg (from the Magus, married
in the Lovers, fertilized by the Hermit) is now in its last stage before hatching. The
three aspects of Scorpio generate the gentle heat that incubates it. This Death is
vivacious and flexible, crowned with Osiris's crown, dancing on the seabed and stirring
up bubbles of new lives.
