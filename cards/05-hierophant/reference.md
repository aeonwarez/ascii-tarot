# V — The Hierophant

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the three project sources —
Crowley's *Book of Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the
esotericmeanings.com tutorial — plus the color card scan.

## Element ranking (BoT p.78-80 + DuQuette Atu V + esotericmeanings + scan)

Tier 1 — the card IS these:
1. **The enthroned Hierophant, central and frontal** — an ornate, confident
   priest-king crowned with the CROWN OF OSIRIS, seated upon the BULL of Taurus,
   in rich heavy vestments. RIGHT hand raises the three-ringed wand; LEFT hand
   gives the blessing (two fingers up, two down — a bridge between heaven and
   earth). His whole body is enclosed by a large HEXAGRAM (the macrocosm).
2. **The dancing Child of Horus in the pentagram on his breast** — on his
   macrocosmic breast a PENTAGRAM (an inverted pentagram containing an upright
   one) holds a glad, wanton, DANCING male child: the microcosm "nailed" to the
   macrocosm, the law of the New Aeon of the Child Horus. Where the two adults
   are rigid, the child is open and free.
3. **The Scarlet Woman before him, girt with a sword** — a woman (Venus / Isis,
   ruler of Taurus) stands before/below him, ARMED with a SWORD and MILITANT
   (no longer the passive vehicle of the male). She carries the MOON (crescent /
   Artemis bow — the Moon exalted in Taurus; links her to the Priestess).
4. **The oriel window: snake, dove, rose, nine nails** — behind/above him a
   diaphanous stained window. In it the SNAKE and the DOVE ("there is the dove,
   and there is the serpent"), and the ROSE OF FIVE PETALS in blossom behind a
   phallic headdress (Venus / Love uniting high and low). The window is fixed by
   exactly NINE NAILS (Yesod, the Moon; the Moon exalted in Taurus).

Tier 2 — named and emphasized:
5. **The four Kerubic beasts, one per corner** — guardians of the shrine, the
   four fixed signs. Harris's New-Aeon placement: BULL lower-left, LION
   lower-right (traditional), and the EAGLE and ANGEL SWAPPED — EAGLE upper-left
   (now fixed-air Aquarius), ANGEL upper-right (now fixed-water Scorpio).
6. **The elephants flanking the throne** — Taurean beasts of burden on either
   side of the seat, grounding his teaching on the earthly plane. Also Apis, the
   Bull-god of Memphis.
7. **The three-ringed wand** — three interlaced rings crowning his sceptre = the
   three Aeons: UPPER ring SCARLET (Horus, on top), two lower rings GREEN (Isis)
   and PALE YELLOW (Osiris), all based on deep indigo (Saturn, Lord of Time — his
   rhythm moves only once every 2,000 years).

Tier 3 — atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The dark-blue Nuit ground** — the whole background is the starry night of
   Nuit, from whose womb all phenomena are born.
9. **The sandal-strap on the child's right foot** = the fifth power of the Sphinx,
   TO GO (spirit / freedom; the other four = the elements).
10. **The rich olive/brown vestments** — heavy embroidered robes; keep the palette
    warm and deep, a felt weight of tradition.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- STRICTLY FRONTAL, near-symmetric, hieratic — the opposite of the Magus's
  motion. The Hierophant sits dead center on the axis (col 23); mirror the
  throne, elephants, window and corner Kerubs about `AXIS = 23.0`.
- Nest the two geometry figures: the HEXAGRAM enclosing the whole seated body,
  and the PENTAGRAM on the chest with a tiny dancing child inside. These two
  shapes are the single most important read — macrocosm around microcosm.
- Put the Scarlet Woman small and central-low in front of him, sword vertical.
- The oriel window is an arched panel behind his head: snake + dove curling its
  frame, a five-petal rose, and NINE small nail marks fixing it.
- One Kerub per corner (eagle UL, angel UR, bull LL, lion LR); an elephant head
  to each side of the throne.
- Palette in the `.ans`: deep RED/ORANGE figure + robe, DARK INDIGO/blue Nuit
  ground, warm BROWN/OLIVE throne, gold crown + three-ring wand (top ring red),
  pale Scarlet Woman, rose accent.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu V):** "Stubborn strength, toil,
endurance, placidity, manifestation, explanation, teaching, goodness of heart,
help from superiors, patience, organization, peace."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Offer thyself Virgin to the Knowledge
and Conversation of thine Holy Guardian Angel! All else is a snare. Be thou
athlete with the eight limbs of Yoga; for without these thou art not disciplined
for any fight."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Traditional teaching, or learning the
fundamentals of a subject before making your own decisions (which come in the
Lovers). Follow rules / traditions that give life structure and meaning; group
or institutional membership. A period of intense learning and education. Follow
tried-and-trusted means rather than novel tangents (a grounded path). Becoming a
mentor, teaching fundamentals. Marriage (an institution); a place in an
established order.

**Reversed (esotericmeanings):** Trapped by following doctrine blindly without
questioning, or in conflict with it. Workplace problems from rules and
restrictions. An over-controlling master allowing no personal freedom. Too much
reliance on tradition and not enough on the inner guide / intuition (the
Priestess). Loss of structure; rebelling against social norms. Going off at a
tangent instead of tried-and-tested methods.

**BoT texture:** Taurus — the Throne surrounded by elephants, he seated on a
bull, the four Kerubs guarding the shrine. The essential arcanum is the uniting
of the microcosm with the macrocosm: before him a hexagram (macrocosm) with a
pentagram at its centre holding a dancing male child (the New Aeon of Horus,
supplanting the Dying-God Aeon of Osiris). The woman girt with a sword = the
Scarlet Woman, Venus armed and militant. The face is benignant and smiling yet
mysterious, even sinister — "a very secret joke," a sadistic aspect from the
Legend of Pasiphae, prototype of the Bull-gods. The wand's three rings = the
Aeons of Isis (green), Osiris (pale yellow), Horus (scarlet), on indigo Saturn.
Background = the dark blue starry night of Nuit.

**DuQuette:** "The Magus of the Eternal," Vau ("nail"), Taurus (Venus rules,
Luna exalted), Path 16 Chokmah→Chesed. Not the Aeon-of-Osiris Pope but a
Babylonian priest-king with sex appeal, supported by his sword-bearing Scarlet
Woman. The window held by nine nails (Yesod, the Moon). The four Kerubic beasts
guard the shrine, with the New-Aeon eagle/angel swap (eagle = Aquarius, angel =
Scorpio). Throne flanked by elephants; seated on the bull; right hand a wand of
three rings (ascendancy of Horus over Isis and Osiris); left hand open in
benediction. Above all: he is the 1 Vau of YHVH — the six of divine macrocosmic
consciousness to whom we "nail" the five of our earthly microcosmic
consciousness; the K&C of the HGA is illustrated by the union of pentagram and
hexagram, our microcosmic self the dancing child in the pentagram upon his breast.
