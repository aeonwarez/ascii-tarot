## Element ranking (BoT p.92-95 + DuQuette Atu XI + esotericmeanings)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **Babalon, the Scarlet Woman, riding the Beast** - a woman astride, drunk in
   ecstasy, one hand reining the Beast, the other raising the Grail - the hero
   figure. Her body / spine read on col 23 as the vertical of the card.
2. **The flaming Holy Grail, raised in her right hand** - a blazing CUP held
   ALOFT (her right / spiritual side), brimming with the Two-in-One elixir (Sun +
   Moon, male + female fluids), kundalini rising from it. Keep it lit and high, the
   second focus.
3. **The seven-headed lion-serpent Beast** - a great tawny LION-SERPENT beneath
   her with SEVEN heads (Angel / Saint / Poet / Adulterous Woman / Man of Valour /
   Satyr / Lion-Serpent), aflame with lust; the Beast is the phallic base she
   rides. Fill the lower/central mass with it.
4. **The red reins** - in her LEFT (carnal) hand, RED reins to the Beast: passion
   guided, not repressed; her connection to the lower nature.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The ten luminous rayed circles (latent Sephiroth)** - ten glowing rayed
   CIRCLES scattered above and below the horizon ("as above, so below"), NOT in
   Tree order - the Sephiroth un-organised because a new Aeon is dawning.
6. **The horizon + the new-Aeon light** - a HORIZON of dawn (new life); above it,
   the emblem of the new light and the ten serpent-HORNS of the Beast destroying
   the old order.
7. **The bloodless saints trampled below** - at the base, grey / bloodless SAINTS
   trampled by the Beast (the old purities that deny the carnal nature); the group
   shaped like the letter SHIN (the Aeon).

Tier 3 - atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The Grail's rays / the 13 rays of the Lion-Serpent head** - 13 = Love (Achad)
   + Unity (Ahbah); glowing rays from the serpent's head if space allows.
9. **Her ecstatic gesture** - she looks toward the lion-serpent, touching herself
   (breasts / vagina) - strong sexual passion; keep implied, not explicit.
10. **The hot golden-amber ground** - keep the field flaming: greenish-yellow,
    reddish amber, deep purple accents, the Leo fire.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- NOT strictly mirror-symmetric - a dynamic diagonal composition: the rider's
  spine on col 23, the Beast's mass filling the lower center, the raised Grail high
  (slightly off-axis toward her right is authentic; keep the figure's spine on the
  axis and verify with `--axis`).
- Cells are 1:2 - draw the Grail-cup and any circles ~2:1 wider than tall; the ten
  rayed circles as small wide discs.
- The single most important read: a woman astride a many-headed lion-beast, holding
  a blazing cup aloft. Vertical spine = Grail → her body → the Beast's back.
- Distinguish the Beast's seven heads by silhouette (don't blur them into one
  mass); keep the trampled saints small, grey and low.
- This is a HOT card in an otherwise cool run (VII-X) - let the `.ans` blaze:
  golden-amber field, scarlet rider, tawny Beast, a white-gold flaming Grail, grey
  saints.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu XI):** "Courage, strength, energy and
action, *une grande passion*; resort to magick, the use of magical power."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Mitigate Energy with Love; but let Love
devour all things. Worship the name ... Babalon, for the Empire is thereof."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** The bliss of integration with something higher than
yourself; the intense attraction of polar opposites resolving their tension.
Develop natural talents and live unconventionally, but not in mere wanton
indulgence (Debauch). Living in the moment; casting off the old to bring in the new
(Chesed builds, Geburah destroys). Courage to accept ALL of who you are and merge
with the ecstasy of spirit.

**Reversed / lower expression:** Sheer debauchery, wanton desire, the serpent
crawling on the ground; passion repressed OR passion indulged without love - both
a burden. The middle way: neither denial nor slavery to the animal nature.

**BoT texture:** Teth, the serpent; Leo, the Lion. Formerly "Strength," renamed
Lust "for the sake of no confusion" - the word implies not only strength but the
joy of it, "une grande passion." Babalon rides the Beast (the Lion-Serpent), the
Scarlet Woman drunk on the blood of the saints, holding the Grail whose contents
are the Two-in-One elixir; her love devours all. The Beast's seven heads are given
as the Angel, the Saint, the Poet, the Adulterous Woman, the Man of Valour, the
Satyr, and the Lion-Serpent; behind, latent Sephiroth as rayed circles, the ten
horns destroying the old order and dawning the new Aeon.

**DuQuette:** "The Daughter of the Flaming Sword." Teth ("serpent"), Leo (Sol
rules), Path 19 Chesed→Geburah. Crowley swapped the numbers of Strength and Justice
(VIII↔XI) so the zodiac ran in order (Leo after Libra). Babalon (synonymous with
Revelation's Babylon; the woman on the scarlet beast, seven heads = the seven
chakras, ten horns = the Sephiroth) offers the Grail of the Two-in-One. The card's
formula is the union of the Woman and the Beast, passion mitigated by love and love
devouring all - the sacrament of the Aeon of Horus.
