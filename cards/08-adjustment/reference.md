# VIII — Adjustment

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the three project sources —
Crowley's *Book of Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the

## Element ranking (BoT p.86-88 + DuQuette Atu VIII + esotericmeanings)

Tier 1 — the card IS these:
1. **The masked goddess poised on the sword's point, dead-center** — a young,
   slender woman on TIPTOE upon the very POINT of her upright sword, perfectly
   balanced left-to-right and top-to-bottom. Masked (Harlequin), in diaphanous
   dancing wings. She is the axis of the card — col 23.
2. **The Phallic sword, vertical, between her thighs** — she grips the hilt of
   the magician's PHALLIC sword in BOTH hands, the blade held vertical BETWEEN
   HER THIGHS (the daughter becoming the mother; Geburah's weapon cutting away
   what does not serve). The sword IS the axis line.
3. **The great balances hung from her crown** — from the perfect point of her
   CROWN OF MAAT (ostrich plumes of the Twofold Truth) hang the SCALES by chains,
   ALPHA in the left pan balanced against OMEGA in the right — the beginning and
   end weighed in perfect equilibrium.
4. **The concealed vesica / diamond** — the lines from her TOES to the two scale
   pans to the CROWN-TIP form a great LOZENGE / VESICA PISCIS around her, the
   diamond through which adjusted experience passes on (links to the Fool's
   diamond). The whole figure sits inside this diamond.

Tier 2 — named and emphasized:
5. **The throne of spheres and pyramids** — she poises before a throne of SPHERES
   and PYRAMIDS, four in number = Law and Limitation, maintaining the same equity
   on an impersonal plane.
6. **The corner spheres of light and darkness** — at the corners, balanced
   SPHERES of light and dark (blue and green, in fours = Chesed); from them,
   constantly equilibrated RAYS.
7. **The feathered curtain of rays** — those rays form a diaphanous FEATHERED
   CURTAIN behind her, the interplay of all forces she sums up and adjudicates;
   the slightest act disturbs it.

Tier 3 — atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The Harlequin chequer** — mask and leggings of crossed green-and-dark
   DIAMONDS; Beardsley-styled; the feminine counterpart of the Fool's motley.
9. **The Uraeus / plumes detail** — the ostrich plumes of Maat crowning her;
   (the Uraeus serpent on the brow is conceptual, faint or absent in the paint).
10. **The green Libra ground** — keep the field emerald / deep blue-green, cool
    and exact, the autumn color of Venus-ruled Libra.

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- THE most literally symmetric card in the set — perfect left/right AND top/bottom
  balance. The figure, the vertical sword, and the crown-tip all sit on col 23;
  mirror the two scale pans (alpha left, omega right), the throne, the corner
  spheres and the curtain about `AXIS = 23.0`. Verify with `--axis`.
- The single most important read: a masked figure balanced on a sword point
  inside a great DIAMOND, with a hanging balance overhead. Vertical spine =
  crown/plumes → head → sword hilt → blade → toe-point.
- Draw the vesica/diamond as clean mirrored diagonals from toe (bottom point) to
  the pans to the crown (top point); the figure centered inside it.
- Alpha in the left pan, omega in the right pan, hung by chains from the crown.
- Palette in the `.ans`: emerald / deep blue-green ground, a paler masked figure,
  a steel sword, glowing alpha/omega discs, blue-and-green corner spheres.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu VIII):** "Justice, or rather justesse,
the act of adjustment, suspension of all action pending decision; in material
matters, may refer to law suits or prosecutions. Socially, marriage or marriage
agreements; politically, treaties."

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Balance against each thought its exact
opposite. For the Marriage of these is the Annihilation of Illusion."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Take stock; keep what is useful and let go of what
no longer serves; use the sword (WILL) to act decisively and restore harmony,
even breaking ties or habits. Weigh consequences; see Karma acting personally and
in the world. Live by your own truth (the principles of Maat: truth, order,
justice, righteousness, morality); all your actions matter — the butterfly effect.

**Reversed (esotericmeanings):** Life out of balance; recognise it but be gentle
with yourself; step back and watch the results of your actions; grow stiller like
the figure (meditate). Refusal to change (Saturn / restriction); stuck in a rut
though reaping poor consequences. Or the actions of others are unbalancing your
life and you have not addressed it.

**BoT texture:** Libra, ruled by Venus, Saturn exalted — the equilibrium of all
things; the final adjustment of Tetragrammaton, the daughter set on the mother's
throne, "awakening the Eld of the All-Father." Beyond the planetary, the feminine
complement of the Fool (Aleph-Lamed = AL, key of the Book of the Law) — hence she
dances, with the suggestion of Harlequin. A young slender woman poised exactly on
tiptoe, crowned with the ostrich plumes of Maat, the Uraeus on her brow, masked,
her expression the secret satisfaction of her domination of every disequilibrium.
The Magic Sword held in both hands; the balances weigh Alpha against Omega (Judex
and Testes of Final Judgement), within the concealed Vesica Piscis. A throne of
spheres and pyramids (four = Law and Limitation); at the corners balanced spheres
of light and darkness whose equilibrated rays form a curtain. "The Woman
Satisfied"; in Eastern philosophy she is Karma; the sphinx without a secret,
purely a matter of calculation.

**DuQuette:** "The Daughter of the Lords of Truth, the Ruler of the Balance."
Lamed ("ox-goad"), Libra (Venus rules, Saturn exalted), Path 22 Geburah→
Tiphareth. In perfect balance, left-to-right and top-to-bottom, she stands on
tiptoe at the point of her sword; an enormous set of balances suspends from the
point of her crown (the plumes of Maat), alpha in the left pan, omega in the
right. The lines from toes to pans to crown-tip create a lozenge/vesica that
stretches the fabric of space to reveal her. Crowley calls her "the feminine
complement of the Fool"; Aleph + Lamed = AL = 31 ("God"); reversed, LA = "not" —
the Western-Zen "God-Not-God" of Thelema. Crowley pressed Harris hard for a
DYNAMIC, dancing balance ("every adjustment a grande passion; compensation a
festival"), not a grocer weighing sugar.
