# Queen of Cups

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes all project sources for this court
card; built on the XVII - The Star sheet template, adapted for courts (no
numeral plaque, elemental title band), and following the Queen of Wands sheet
already done in this project. A Harris card scan DOES exist for this court
card, so a genuine observed-palette section is included.

## Element ranking (art priorities)

Tier 1 - the card IS these:
1. **The great cup whose stem is the goddess herself** - DuQuette: the card
   presents "the image of a great cup, the stem of which is formed by the body
   of a goddess," and it is Atu II (High Priestess) turned upside down. The
   whole figure reads as a chalice standing in water. This is the single most
   important compositional read: not a woman holding a cup, but a woman who
   IS the cup. The chalice-body is the stage, centered on the axis.
2. **Enthroned upon still water** - "She is represented as enthroned upon
   still water." The throne is water itself; there is no solid seat. The lower
   half of the card is a mirror-flat surface, and she rises out of it.
3. **The mirror water carrying her unbroken reflection** - "the sea upon which
   she is enthroned conveys the almost unbroken images of the image which she
   represents." Everything above the waterline is answered below it. Water of
   Water is mirror to mirror. The reflection is nearly as strong as the figure.
4. **Veiled by endless curves of light** - "She is robed in, and veiled by,
   endless curves of light." Her robe is not cloth but overlapping arcs and
   ribbons of light; the whole upper card is a shell of nested curves that
   hide and half-reveal her. Extreme purity, infinite subtlety, hard to see
   the truth of her.

Tier 2 - named and emphasized:
5. **The shell-like cup with the crayfish issuing** - "In her hand she bears a
   shell-like cup, from which issues a crayfish." The cup reads as a scallop /
   sea-shell shape, and a crayfish (DuQuette: crayfish issuing from a river)
   crawls out of it. The crayfish is the specific Thoth detail, not a plain
   goblet.
6. **The Lotus of Isis, of the Great Mother** - "she bears also the Lotus of
   Isis, of the Great Mother." A lotus on a long stem, sacred to the mother
   goddess. In the scan the lotus and its pads sit low on the water beside her.
7. **The ibis** (GD crest) - the wading bird of Thoth stands in the still
   water in the Harris painting, a heron/ibis form mirrored in the surface.

Tier 3 - supporting:
8. **Her image of extreme purity and beauty, reflecting the observer** - "to
   see the Truth of her is hardly possible, for she reflects the nature of the
   observer in great perfection." Her face is veiled, indistinct, given over
   to reflection rather than expression. She is the perfect agent and patient.
9. **The light source at the crown** - a radiance breaking at the top of the
   card (the source of the veiling curves of light), the head of the chalice.

Tier 4 - fine detail, as space allows:
10. **Lotus pads / lily pads floating on the mirror**, small reflected
    highlights, the ripple-free calm that makes the mirror possible.

## Meanings

**Crowley's character description (BoT chapter, canonical):** the
characteristics associated with this card are principally dreaminess, illusion
and tranquillity. She is the perfect agent and patient, able to receive and
transmit everything without herself being affected thereby. Everything that
passes through her is refracted and distorted; her characteristics depend
mostly upon the influences which affect her. Normally, people of this type
have no character at all of their own, unless it can be called a characteristic
to be at the disposition of every impact or impression.

**Reflection as her whole nature (BoT / DuQuette):** Water of Water is
tantamount to reflection of reflection, or mirror to mirror. She reflects the
nature of the observer in great perfection; to liberals she looks like a
liberal, to conservatives a conservative, when in truth she may be neither.
She is popular and makes friends easily because, when others look at her, they
see only themselves.

**Ill-dignified (BoT / DuQuette):** all these qualities are degraded. She can
be dangerous and cruel, distorting the reflections she casts back on her
unsuspecting victims, who then see only monsters in the mirror.

**Yi King (BoT):** 58th hexagram Tui - the commentary is as colourless as the
card; mild exhortations on the subject of pleasure. There is a hint (line 6)
that the chief pleasure of people of this type is to lead and attract others,
and such are accordingly often exceedingly popular.

**DuQuette (character read):** Harris's Queen of Cups bears striking
similarity to Atu II, the High Priestess; trim the borders, set the Queen
upside down above the Priestess, and they match in a display of synthetic
projective geometry. Both cards present a great cup whose stem is the body of
a goddess: Atu II is the cup of the virginal Moon goddess, the Queen of Cups
that of the great mother goddess. DuQuette's fellow Queens of Cups include
Paul McCartney, Jacques Cousteau, Nikola Tesla, George Orwell, and Gerald
Gardner.

**As a person (court-card use):** anyone whose Sun or rising sign falls in her
zodiacal span (late Gemini into Cancer, roughly June 11 to July 11) may carry
her traits. She is someone's birthday card first, an abstraction second.

## Observed Harris palette (from the card scan)

`reference/queen-cups-card.jpg` (scan source: esotericmeanings.com court-cards
page). Unlike the Queen of Wands, a Harris scan of this court card exists, so
this palette is observed from the painting, cross-checked against the text.

- **Field / cool dominant:** cool blues and blue-greens fill the card, exactly
  as DuQuette notes for all the Cups court cards. Deep teal and slate blue in
  the upper masses, softening to pale water below.
- **Veiling curves of light:** silver-white and pale blue-green ribbons arcing
  down over the figure, the brightest values in the card.
- **Crown radiance:** a warm pale yellow / cream light source breaks at the top
  center, the one warm note against the blue, feeding the veiling arcs.
- **The goddess-chalice / figure:** blue-green and grey-white, veiled and
  indistinct; she is more suggested than drawn, purity read as low contrast.
- **Still water / mirror:** the lower half is a darker blue-green mirror
  carrying the near-unbroken inverted reflection; reflected highlights are
  paler blue-white.
- **Ibis / wading bird:** a pale grey-white heron/ibis form standing in the
  water, mirrored below the surface.
- **Lotus and pads:** green lotus pads and a paler bloom low on the water;
  green is the secondary accent against the blue.
- **Shell-cup and crayfish:** the cup reads as a pale shell tone; the crayfish
  a small darker form issuing from it.
- **Border:** pale warm-grey art deco frame, consistent with the deck chrome;
  romanized He and the Cancer / water attribution on the title band.

Keep any ANSI mapping inside this cool blue / blue-green / silver-white range
with a single warm cream light source at the crown; green only for the lotus.
Introduce no color with no referent in the scan.
