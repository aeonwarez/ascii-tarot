# III — The Empress

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the three project sources —
Crowley's *Book of Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the
esotericmeanings.com tutorial — plus the color card scan.

## Element ranking (BoT p.75-77 + DuQuette Atu III + esotericmeanings + scan)

Tier 1 — the card IS these:
1. **The enthroned goddess + Mater Triumphans gesture** — imperial crown and
   vestments, seated frontal. Her ARMS form the alchemical glyph of SALT: the
   lotus scepter in one hand, the other arm curved as if cradling an invisible
   baby to the breast — Mater Triumphans (Isis suckling the infant Horus). She
   is PREGNANT (DuQuette: baby floating in a salty amniotic sea). She faces the
   Emperor. The still, nurturing axis of the card.
2. **The Crown of Isis + revolving moons** — waxing / full / waning moon crown;
   she sits BETWEEN a waxing moon (growth, fertility, love) and a waning moon
   (letting go). Both moons REVOLVE = the balance of opposites in Nature. The
   cross on the crown is the MALTESE CROSS (4 elements in perfect balance).
3. **The twisted-flame / twisted-grass throne** — uprights suggesting blue
   twisted flames (BoT: "her birth from water"), read by Harris as twisted
   blades of grass (goddess of vegetation). Fluid, feminine, water-born.
4. **Venus / Universal Love as the whole** — rose + green (colours of Venus)
   everywhere; Venus glyph hidden in the shield (green + white field). The card
   is love uniting Will (Chokmah) with Understanding (Binah). "In no other card
   is it so necessary to disregard the parts and concentrate on the whole."

Tier 2 — named and emphasized:
5. **The lotus of Isis** — held over the heart chakra (heart-path, not
   head-path). Its roots in the water, petals open to the Sun whose image is
   the belly of the chalice — a living form of the Holy Grail. 4 petals = Chesed
   (Love manifesting into matter). Blue = water, her element. **Hand discrepancy:
   BoT + esotericmeanings say RIGHT hand; DuQuette says LEFT hand — draw it in
   the RIGHT hand (majority), held to the heart.**
6. **The sparrow & dove** — her two sacred birds of Venus, perched on the
   flame/grass uprights of the throne (Catullus/Martial). Dove = the male and
   female forces on her shield.
7. **The heraldry: Pelican + White Eagle** — LOWER LEFT: the white Pelican
   feeding its young from the blood of her own heart (the Great Mother + the
   Daughter of Tetragrammaton; continuity of life; an earlier Aeon of Osiris).
   LOWER RIGHT: a shield with a white DOUBLE-HEADED EAGLE holding the (waxing)
   Moon in its beaks = the alchemical WHITE TINCTURE (Moon / silver), consort to
   the Red Eagle of the Emperor.
8. **The girdle of the Zodiac** — a golden belt of the twelve signs about her
   waist = her universality across all creation.

Tier 3 — atmosphere / as space allows:
9. **The floor tapestry** — carpet embroidered with fleurs-de-lis (lily of 3
   petals = Binah / the Holy Trinity) and tiny FISH (the feminine principle),
   seeming to adore the **Secret Rose** (the white rose at the base of the
   throne, from which flow the green-tinged waters of Universal Life).
10. **The robe details** — bees (energy, vitality) and dominos (black+white =
    male-female = Chokmah-Binah) surrounded by continuous spiral lines (rose
    petals = love uniting duality).
11. **The Arch / Door behind her** — the interpretation of Daleth; the card
    summed up is "the Gate of Heaven."

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- She is warmer and more organic than the Priestess: no rigid light-web. The
  field is soft VEGETATION and cool green/blue, drawn as dithered leafy masses
  and grass rather than straight rays.
- Crown of Isis at the head (moon phases + Maltese cross); a small revolving
  moon to each side (waxing left-of-view / waning) — keep them legible.
- Throne uprights = twisted vertical ropes of flame/grass (`{ } ( ) S`-like
  curls), sparrow + dove tiny silhouettes perched at their tops.
- Arms = the Salt glyph (a circle over a horizontal bar); lotus in the right
  hand at the heart; left arm curved low cradling the pregnant belly (a faint
  round infant in the amniotic curve).
- Lower left: white pelican over its brood. Lower right: heater shield with a
  double-headed eagle + small moon. Base: white Secret Rose, green waters,
  two tiny fish; carpet of fleur-de-lis diamonds.
- Palette in the `.ans`: cool greens + sky blue field, red blouse accent, gold
  zodiac belt, white heraldry, warm-white rose.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, p.290):** "Love. Beauty, happiness,
pleasure, success, completion, good fortune, graciousness, elegance, luxury,
idleness, dissipation, debauchery, friendship, gentleness, delight."

**DuQuette verse (title-page):** "This is the Harmony of the Universe, that
Love unites the Will to create with the Understanding of that Creation:
understand thou thine own Will! Love and let love. Rejoice in every shape of
love, and get thy rapture and thy nourishment thereof."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** Birth / pregnancy, fertility; being in touch
with the beauty of your life; someone needing nurturing; exploring the
relationship with the mother; an empathic, caring personality; in touch with
universal love / spirit; spirituality expressed from the heart not the head;
time in nature; nurturing others and yourself; connecting with love in
meditation / spiritual practice.

**Reversed (esotericmeanings):** Vanity; sexual/emotional manipulation; unable
to give or experience love, emotionally damaged; unresolved conflict with the
mother; lack of self-love; unwanted pregnancy; unable to connect with universal
love spiritually; not enjoying spiritual work, conflicts.

**BoT texture:** Complement of the Emperor, but her attributions are far more
universal. On the Tree, Daleth unites the Father (Chokmah) with the Mother
(Binah), above the Abyss. She combines the highest spiritual with the lowest
material qualities and so represents the alchemical SALT — the inactive
principle of Nature, matter that must be energized by Sulphur (the Emperor) to
maintain the whirling equilibrium of the Universe. The Pelican, White Eagle,
lotus-Grail, revolving moons and Secret Rose are all cognate: no contradiction,
only the opposition necessary to balance. At the back is the Arch/Door of
Daleth — "the Gate of Heaven." Because of the omniform beauty of the symbol,
the student dazzled by any single manifestation may be led astray: disregard
the parts, concentrate upon the whole.

**DuQuette:** "The Daughter of the Mighty Ones." Woman is the door of heaven at
conception and the door of life at birth. The second alchemical trump = Salt.
The path of Daleth is the hallway down which the queen tiptoes to the king's
bedroom — the exchange of unimaginable love. Harris's is the most tasteful,
soothing card in the pack; she is the goddess of vegetation, throne of twisted
grass blades, green skirt + passionate red blouse, golden zodiac belt, lotus
scepter (DuQuette: left hand), Mater Triumphans, and PREGNANT. White pelican
lower-left; shield with a white double-headed eagle holding the Moon (white
tincture) lower-right; carpet of fleurs-de-lis + fish adoring the Secret Rose.

**Letter/Aeon note:** The Pelican (self-sacrifice, feeding young from its own
blood) figures the Aeon of Osiris; the purified shield / white tincture figures
the New Aeon of Horus. Antimony (orb + cross on the crown) is the catalyst that
purifies the Salt (the Empress) into white tincture (shield) and impurity
(Pelican).
