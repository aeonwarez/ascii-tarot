# XII — The Hanged Man

Definitive working reference. Synthesizes the three project sources —
Crowley's *Book of Thoth*, DuQuette's *Understanding...Thoth Tarot*, and the
esotericmeanings.com tutorial. Color scan TBD.

## Element ranking (BoT p.96-99 + DuQuette Atu XII + esotericmeanings)

Tier 1 — the card IS these:
1. **The inverted hanged figure, head-down, hanging by the LEFT foot** — a young
   fair man, the hero figure, suspended upside down; his body/spine reads on col
   23 as the vertical of the card (inverted). Draw him ON TOP of the grid.
2. **The body's cross-over-triangle geometry** — right leg crossed behind the left
   into a figure 4 / cross; arms clasped behind the head into an upright TRIANGLE
   that radiates light, the head centred inside it (like the eye in the pyramid).
   Triangle (Trinity/spirit) surmounting cross (matter): light into darkness.
3. **The inverted Ankh he hangs from** — the suspension point at the TOP, an ankh
   (Rose & Cross, union of male/female), replacing the old wooden gallows; tied to
   the letter Tau and the completion of the Great Work.
4. **The serpent coiled around the foot** — a serpent wound about the suspending
   (left) foot, binding it to the ankh; creator and destroyer, the wisdom/will of
   God (Chokmah), that operates all change.

Tier 2 — named and emphasized:
5. **The green disks + green air of Grace** — GREEN disks (Venus, Grace, Love) at
   the terminations of the limbs and the head; the air above the water green,
   infiltrated by the white rays of Kether's light from the top.
6. **The black womb-waters + the serpent of new life below** — beneath the figure,
   a BLACK enclosed shape (the black waters of Binah / the womb) in which a serpent
   (black flecked gold, the lowest colour scale) stirs, coiled, as the serpent of
   new life; the Child begotten through the Work.
7. **The unbounded grid of small squares** — the background is a boundless grill of
   small squares: the Enochian Elemental Tablets, the sigils of all the energies of
   Nature; the crossed legs (cross of spirit) sit within a dark square.

Tier 3 — atmosphere / as space allows:
8. **The 18 rays** — rays from the man's head down into the womb below; 18 = Chai
   ("Life"), spiritual light carried into the darkness of matter.
9. **The Vau-shaped nails** — nails at hands/feet shaped like the letter Vau (links
   him to the Hierophant as transmitter of spirit); keep as tiny marks.
10. **The white Kether light / closed mouth** — the white light entering from the
    top; the figure's mouth resolutely closed (the return to Eternal Silence, M).

## ASCII treatment notes (to prove in drafts)

- Bilaterally symmetric and INVERTED: this is the strongest axis-symmetry card so
  far. The figure hangs head-down centred on col 23 — verify with `--axis`. The
  danger is not lateral drift but reading right-way-up; keep the head LOW and the
  bright foot/ankh HIGH so the inversion is unmistakable.
- The body must read as cross-over-triangle: legs a 4/cross at the bottom (head
  region), arms + torso a triangle around the head. Do not let it blur into a plain
  standing figure.
- Cells are 1:2 — draw the ankh loop and the green disks ~2:1 wider than tall.
- Background grid of small squares should tile the whole field behind the figure
  (occluded where he overlaps); the black womb-shape a solid dithered mass low
  centre with the coiled serpent inside.
- Palette is the tell: GREEN air + white top light + BLACK water below. Let the
  `.ans` read green and pale against a dark base, distinct from the hot Lust card.

## Meanings

**Crowley divinatory (BoT appendix, Atu XII):** "Enforced sacrifice, punishment,
loss, fatal or voluntary, suffering, defeat, failure, death." (Read in the New Aeon
as the illusion of debt to be dissolved, not literal doom.)

**Title-page verse (BoT / DuQuette):** "Let not the water whereon thou journeyest
wet thee! And, being come to shore, plant thou the Vine and rejoice without shame."

**Upright (esotericmeanings):** The chance to redeem a situation that has turned
sour (bringing light into darkness); repair of an emotional or spiritual
transgression (water = emotion/spirit, which should flow); losing emotional ties to
past guilt; giving spiritually for the highest good; freedom from guilt or blame; the
surrender of a lesser thing for a more fulfilling one.

**Reversed / lower expression:** Self-punishment and guilt about the past;
detrimental self-sacrifice when unnecessary; someone manipulating spirituality for
gain; a life turned upside down; waiting too long, scared to change; but non-action
can also let better options emerge.

**BoT texture:** Mem, the mother letter of Water; "a baptism which is also a death."
In the Aeon of Osiris this was the supreme formula of adeptship (INRI/IAO); now,
under a Fiery Aeon, water below the Abyss is hostile unless the opposition is the
right opposition of marriage — "the annihilation of the self in the Beloved." Crowley
insists "the whole idea of sacrifice is a misconception of nature" and answers it
with Liber AL: "I give unimaginable joys on earth ... nor do I demand aught in
sacrifice." He hangs the man from an ankh (Rose & Cross); a serpent stirs in the
Abyss below as the Child begotten by the Work; the background is the Enochian tablets.

**DuQuette:** "The Spirit of the Mighty Waters," Path 23 Geburah→Hod, Mem (water).
Traces the sacrifice through the Aeons: Isis (water — the unborn baby floating upside
down in the womb), Osiris (air — the tortured, hung/nailed dying god), Horus (fire —
where water and fire clash, and the only right "marriage" is the ecstatic dissolution
of separateness). The Thoth Hanged Man still symbolizes the descent of light into
darkness to redeem it, but "redeem" no longer implies a debt. Note Harris's projective
geometry linking this to other water cards (place it top-to-top with the High
Priestess and the Queen of Cups), her stylised Enochian tablets, and the Venusian
green.
