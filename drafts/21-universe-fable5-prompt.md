# Fable Prompt - Atu XXI, The Universe (Thoth)

## Invariants (recap of FABLE_TEMPLATE.md)
Monospace Thoth tarot art. Canvas art 47x32, framed 51x39, aspect 0.64, exact leading
whitespace preserved. **Anchor to the axis (col 23):** this card is a symmetric SHELL
holding an asymmetric EVENT - the great oval mandorla touches top, bottom and both
sides centred on column 23, the four corner Kerubs balance in pairs, the green sphere
and the house of matter sit ON the axis; inside that shell the dancing maiden and the
coiling serpent are deliberately off-balance. Build the oval, the corners, the sphere
and the pyramid with the mirror helpers (`PM`/`PMB` about `AXIS = 23.0`); place the
dancer at `23 - len(s)//2` and the serpent by mirror-offset from the axis, NOT by left
edge. Verify with `--axis`. The classic bug tell is a centred oval with the dancer and
sphere leaning a few columns left. Cells are 1:2, so draw the great oval mandorla, the
eye-vesica and the green sphere ~2:1 WIDER than tall; this card is nearly all curved
geometry and dies fastest on that ratio. Courier New; extended alphabet `´ ‾ ¡ ·` +
line-glyphs `o O v V T L 7 U c C x X` allowed. Solid masses dithered for volume, never
open outlines, lit directionally - the green sphere is a lit globe, not a ring.
Foreground figure drawn ON TOP; break the lattice behind her and behind the serpent.
Full-bleed to the border. Keep outer frame + bottom title band. Color mapped to the
Harris painting. Sign `aw` or unsigned, never `jgs`. Output one `.txt` + one `.ans`
(256 + 16 fallback).

## Reasoning tier
Author at the highest tier (x-high / best). Low-volume, high-craft work; the marginal
quality shows in the esoteric synthesis (Tau as the cross of extension, Saturn plus

## Subject
**Atu XXI - The Universe** (old decks: The World; DuQuette: "The Great One of the Night
of Time"). Hebrew letter Tau ("the Sign of the Cross," extension; value 400). SATURN,
outermost and slowest of the seven sacred planets, plus the element EARTH thrust upon
the symbol for its heaviness. Path 32, Yesod -> Malkuth: the last path, the bottom of
the descent, the influence falling upon Malkuth the daughter - "setting the daughter
upon the throne of the Mother," and so the end returning automatically to the
beginning. The complement of the Fool: Aleph plus Tau spell ATh, Essence. A glyph of
the completion of the Great Work in its highest sense; the Celebration of the Great
Work accomplished. "Treat time and all conditions of Event as Servants of thy Will."

## The composition, in one sentence
The naked dancing maiden - eyes closed, posed in the sign of Earth, spiral-haired -
dances inside a great woven oval of seventy-two circles that touches all four edges,
wound about by an enormous gold spotted serpent she manipulates as the radiant spiral
force, a pure bright green sphere behind her, the eye of the great vesica bursting into
rays above, the blueprint house of matter at her feet, and the four Kerubim - Man,
Eagle, Lion, Bull - in the corners.

Hold two things above all: THE DANCER AND HER SERPENT (the completed Great Work in a
body, the hero read) and THE GREAT WOVEN OVAL WITH THE FOUR KERUBS (the established
Universe, the shell that makes it this card and not another).

## Ranked directives

**Non-negotiable (1-4, it isn't the Universe without these):**

1. **The dancing maiden.** A naked shining dancer with female breasts, eyes closed,
   spiral-coiled hair, posed in the sign of Earth (right foot advanced, right hand
   raised, left hand lowered and thrown back), a small red scarf at the loins. She is
   the Daughter, the final Heh of Tetragrammaton, the Virgin of Eternity. Centre her on
   column 23; draw ON TOP of lattice, sphere and rays. She DANCES - no stiff frontal
   pose; give her a real diagonal of limbs inside the symmetric oval.

2. **The great serpent / the radiant spiral force.** An enormous gold, dark-spotted
   serpent-ribbon sweeping in one great S from the top of the card down across her
   body: the "radiant spiral force, the active and passive, each possessing its dual
   polarity," gripped dextro-rotary in one hand and levo-rotary in the other. The Snake
   to Heru-Ra-Ha's Star. Give it a spotted texture so it reads as the Harris snake and
   not a plain ribbon; place it by mirror-offset from the axis, not by left edge. This
   is the card's central action.

3. **The great oval mandorla of seventy-two circles.** A vesica piscis touching the
   top, bottom and both sides of the art, woven as a LATTICE of circles for the
   quinaries of the Zodiac, the Shemhamphorasch (the original design says 400, the
   value of Tau; Harris gives 72). Do not attempt 72 legible circles at 47 wide: render
   a woven MESH (diagonal cross-hatch with `o` nodes at the crossings, gold stars
   scattered) that READS as a net of circles. ~2:1 wider than tall. Break it behind the
   figure and the serpent.

4. **The four Kerubim in the corners.** Man/Aquarius, Eagle/Scorpio, Lion/Leo,
   Bull/Taurus: the four fixed signs, the four Elements, the established Universe,
   cornered exactly as in the Hierophant. Orange-terracotta heads in dark red-brown
   scrollwork, balanced in mirrored pairs about the axis. All four must be legible as
   distinct beasts; do not let them decay into generic corner ornament.

**Makes it Thoth (5-7):**

5. **The wheel of Light and the green sphere.** At centre, a great PURE BRIGHT GREEN
   sphere (New Aeon Earth, "no longer black, or of mixed colours, but pure bright
   green") carrying a triple-half-twist Mobius band - Harris's endless glyph standing
   in for Crowley's wheel of Light and the Tree of Life of the ten solar bodies, "not
   visible except to those of wholly pure heart." A dithered LIT globe, ~2:1 wider than
   tall, never an open circle.

6. **The eye of the vesica and its rays.** A second small eye-vesica set high in the
   great oval, either projecting all the images onto the card or drawing them back into
   itself: the eye of the Great Architect. Pale rays burst from it toward the top
   corners. The dancer's raised hand drives a black crescent (a sickle, apt for a
   Saturnian goddess) into the middle of it.

7. **The house of matter at the foot.** At the bottom point of the vesica, ON the axis:
   the skeleton plan of the building of the house of Matter - the ninety-two known
   chemical elements as tiny verticals ranked in hierarchy and joined by diagonals
   (after J. W. N. Sullivan) - projected onto the largest of three Giza-like pyramids,
   its apex swelling with finishing stones. Draw it as a pale blueprint, not a solid.

**Soul + garnish (8-10, tiny marks or drop if crowded):**

8. The continuous ambience of loops and whorls: keep the whole field in motion, gold
   stars scattered through the lattice, nothing sitting still.
9. Saturn's indigo ground turned bright: the indigo derived from the blue velvet of the
   midnight sky, red-brown maroon in the corners, but the whole "as bright and glowing
   as any in the Pack" - the traditional subfusc overturned by the New Aeon.
10. The Tau of extension: the fourfold cross implied under the design (the New
    Jerusalem squares boxing the vesica, the red scarf shaped to the letter, the four
    corners as the arms of the cross).

## Design note (specific to this card)
The Universe is a SYMMETRIC SHELL holding an ASYMMETRIC EVENT, and that is the whole
compositional problem. Build the oval, the four Kerubs, the green sphere and the
pyramid rigidly about the axis with `PM`/`PMB`; then let the dancer and the serpent
break that symmetry hard inside it. Two traps. First, the ratio: this card is nearly
all curves, so every oval, the eye-vesica and the sphere must be built ~2:1 wider than
tall from the start, baked into the geometry, or the whole card renders as a stack of
squashed eggs. Second, the lattice: 72 circles is not drawable at 47 wide, and the
failure mode is either a bald outline (dead) or a mesh so dense it eats the figure
(illegible) - go for a mid-density woven cross-hatch with `o` nodes, and BREAK it
behind the dancer and the serpent so the occlusion reads. Palette is the third trap:
this is a dark-attributed card (Saturn, Earth, indigo, black) that Crowley explicitly
says is bright, so do NOT let the `.ans` sink toward Death's murk. Indigo/midnight
ground, deep red-brown corners, a blue-green starred lattice, a GOLD dark-spotted
serpent, a PURE BRIGHT GREEN sphere, a flesh-and-copper dancer, ORANGE-terracotta
Kerubs, pale blue-white rays from the eye, a pale blueprint pyramid at the foot. It is
the deck's last card and its answer to the Fool; it should feel like arrival, not like
weight.

## Render & review
Do not judge the oval ratio, the lattice density, the occlusion, the placement, or the
palette by reading the source. Run the chain and LOOK: `compose_21-universe_lg.py` ->
`frame.py <art> "THE UNIVERSE" "~ tau · saturn ~" -w 47 -s majors -n XXI` ->
`cardkit.py 21-universe` -> `render_png.py 21-universe --axis`, then OPEN the PNG and
critique it against the Harris scan (`reference/21-universe-card.jpg`): does the great
oval sit centred on the axis guide and touch all four edges? is it ~2:1 wider than tall
rather than a squashed egg? does the lattice read as a woven net of circles instead of
a bald outline or a solid block? does the dancer read as DANCING, centred but
asymmetric? does the serpent sweep one clear great S with spotted texture? is the green
sphere a lit dithered globe? are all four Kerubs legible as Man, Eagle, Lion, Bull? is
the house of matter legible at the foot? does the `.ans` read BRIGHT against Death and
the Devil? Fix the compositor and repeat. Ship at ~80% once the render holds (2-3
passes max). Note: `21-universe` must be added to `cardkit.CONFIGS` before render_png
will run. See FABLE_TEMPLATE.md "Render & review loop."
