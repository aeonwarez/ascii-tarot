# Fable Prompt - Atu XVI, The Tower / War (Thoth)

## Invariants (recap of FABLE_TEMPLATE.md)
Monospace Thoth tarot art. Canvas art 47x32, framed 51x39, aspect 0.64, exact leading
whitespace preserved. **Anchor to the axis (col 23):** this card is NOT hieratic-frontal
and NOT symmetric; the one bilaterally symmetric object is the EYE at the top, so put the
eye's pupil on column 23 with `PM`/`PMB` about `AXIS = 23.0` and let everything else be
asymmetric BY DESIGN - the tower leans left of the axis, the falling figures and the
mouth's opening fall to the right. Verify with `--axis`: the eye centred, the collapse
deliberately off-centre. The classic bug tell here is the opposite of usual - an
accidentally symmetric card, or an eye that drifted because it was placed at the axis
instead of `axis - width//2`. Cells are 1:2 so draw the Eye ~2:1 wider than tall (a lidded
almond, not a circle) and give the mouth's banded sheets a wide shallow sweep. Courier New;
extended alphabet `´ ‾ ¡ ·` + line-glyphs `o O v V T L 7 U c C x X` allowed. Solid masses
dithered for volume, never open outlines, lit directionally - but keep the cubist shard
EDGES crisp; this painting is faceted, not smoky. Foreground drawn ON TOP; break background
rays behind the tower and the figures. Full-bleed to the border. Keep outer frame + bottom
title band. Color mapped to the Harris painting. Sign `aw` or unsigned, never `jgs`. Output
one `.txt` + one `.ans` (256 + 16 fallback).

## Reasoning tier
Author at the highest tier (x-high / best). Low-volume, high-craft work; the marginal
quality shows in the esoteric synthesis (the Eye of Horus that is also the Eye of Shiva,
the Harris reversal of blasting the tower from BELOW by the mouth of Dis, the garrison
turned into geometrical expressions, the dove and the Lion-Serpent as the two forms of
desire) and in clean compositor structure. It does NOT fix placement drift - for that, use
the render & review loop.

## Subject
**Atu XVI - The Tower**, subtitled WAR (ancient title "The House of God" / "Destruction of
the House of God," retained only because of AL I:57; DuQuette: "The Lord of the Hosts of
the Mighty"). Hebrew letter Pe' ("mouth"), one of the seven double letters. The planet
MARS - a planetary trump, not zodiacal. Path 27, Netzach -> Hod, the horizontal reciprocal
path across the lower face of the Tree. "The manifestation of cosmic energy in its grossest
form"; the preface to Atu XX, the Coming of a New Aeon. "Break down the fortress of thine
Individual Self, that thy Truth may spring free from the ruins."

## The composition, in one sentence
A great lidded Eye of Horus opens at the top of the card and rays hard straight fire across
the whole field, the ochre fortress below it tilting and coming apart into cubist facets,
its broken garrison thrown out and falling headlong having lost their human shape, down
into the enormous banded jaws of Dis that gape across the bottom and belch flame up at the
roots of the structure, the Dove with its olive branch descending on the left and the
lion-headed Serpent Xnoubis coiling out of a gold sun-disc on the right.

Hold two things above all: THE OPENING EYE (the hero read, on the axis, whose opening
destroys the Universe) and THE MOUTH DEVOURING THE FALLING TOWER (the letter Pe' made
literal, the blast coming from BELOW, not from heaven).

## Ranked directives

**Non-negotiable (1-4, it isn't the Tower without these):**

1. **The Eye of Horus / Eye of Shiva.** "The dominating feature of this card." A great
   lidded eye at the TOP, gold/ochre rim, orange iris, dark red pupil ring, black centre,
   throwing straight hard rays out across the entire field and a zigzag spark down into the
   structure. Pupil on column 23 (use the mirror helpers; it is the only symmetric object).
   Draw it ON TOP of its own rays. Its opening destroys the Universe.

2. **The blasted, burning Tower.** An angular ochre/yellow fortress LEFT of centre, tilting
   and fracturing into flat cubist facets, sheets of flame tearing through it. It must still
   read as architecture at 47 wide: hold the dark arched GATE and two or three black windows
   even as the walls come apart.

3. **The jaws of Dis at the base.** The MOUTH - Pe' made literal - an enormous gaping maw of
   long banded red/orange/yellow sheets sweeping across the bottom third, opening upward from
   the right, belching flame at the roots of the structure and swallowing what falls. Harris's
   great reversal: the blast comes from BELOW, not lightning from above. Wide shallow sweep,
   cells are 1:2.

4. **The broken figures falling headlong.** The garrison thrown from the tower, tumbling
   down-right into the mouth. "They have lost their human shape. They have become mere
   geometrical expressions" - pale grey jointed angular manikins, drafting-figure joints,
   NOT people. The GD original design says their attitude suggests the letter Ayin; keep them
   angular enough to read as glyphs.

**Makes it Thoth (5-7):**

5. **The Dove with the olive branch.** Upper LEFT, pale cream, descending, bathed in the
   effulgence of the Eye, olive sprig in its beak. The Will to Live, the feminine impulse.

6. **The Serpent, Lion-Serpent Xnoubis / Abraxas.** Upper RIGHT, a lion-headed serpent
   coiling out of a radiant gold SUN-DISC, body descending left. The Will to Die, the
   masculine impulse. With the dove it makes AL I:57: "there is the dove, and there is the
   serpent. Choose ye well" - love under will. The two must balance left/right about the Eye
   without making the card symmetric.

7. **The sheets of flame and the cubist shard-field.** The whole background split into
   hard-edged wedges of fire and flat black void radiating from the Eye - the destruction of
   the old-established Aeon by lightning, flames, engines of war. Straight lines everywhere;
   the only real curve on the card is the mouth.

**Soul + garnish (8-10, tiny marks or drop if crowded):**

8. The flat BLACK wedge voids between the flames (Shiva's Nothingness; all manifestation is
   a stain).
9. The collapse diagonal held from upper left to lower right, into the mouth - the Eye is
   centred, nothing else is.
10. The Mars heat held to the very edge: every value in the scarlet/orange/ochre band, grey
    only for the dove and the falling manikins.

## Design note (specific to this card)
The Tower is the anti-Hierophant: where Art and the Hierophant are hieratic and balanced,
this one is a COLLAPSE, and a composition that comes out symmetric has failed. Centre the
Eye and then deliberately break the balance below it - tower left, figures and maw right,
everything sliding down-right. The first trap is drawing the traditional RWS/Marseille card:
do NOT put lightning striking from heaven. Harris inverts it - the fire comes UP from the
mouth of the underworld, and the eye's zigzag is only the spark that lit it. The second trap
is smoke: Harris paints hard cubist facets, so dither for volume but keep shard edges crisp
and the black voids flat. The third trap is drawing the garrison as human silhouettes; they
must be geometrical, jointed, dehumanized. Palette from BoT/DuQuette/the scan: a gold-rimmed
ORANGE eye with a dark pupil; ochre/yellow tower walls with red and black facet shadows and
a dark arched gate; pale GREY manikins; a cream dove on a black crescent field; a gold
sun-disc with an orange lion-serpent; banded RED/ORANGE/YELLOW maw across the base; flat
black everywhere between. GD scale: Scarlet; Red; Venetian Red; Bright Red rayed azure or
emerald. This is the hottest `.ans` of the run - no blue, no green - and it must not be
mistakable for Art's rainbow, the Star's blues, or the Moon's indigo.

## Render & review
Do not judge the collapse diagonal, the eye's centering, the legibility of the tower, or the
palette by reading the source. Run the chain and LOOK: `compose_16-tower_lg.py` ->
`frame.py <art> "THE TOWER" "~ peh · mars ~" -w 47 -s majors -n XVI` -> `cardkit.py 16-tower`
-> `render_png.py 16-tower --axis`, then OPEN the PNG and critique against the Harris scan
(`reference/16-tower-card.jpg`): does the eye's pupil sit exactly on the axis guide while the
tower leans left and the maw opens right? does the tower still read as architecture (gate,
windows) while fracturing? do the falling figures read as geometrical manikins rather than
people? does the mouth read as a MOUTH across the base and not just as flame? do the dove
(left) and the lion-serpent sun-disc (right) both land under the eye's rays? are the shard
edges crisp and the black voids flat? is the whole thing scarlet-orange-ochre with no cool
colour? Fix the compositor and repeat. Ship at ~80% once the render holds (2-3 passes max).
Note: `16-tower` must be added to `cardkit.CONFIGS` before render_png will run. See
FABLE_TEMPLATE.md "Render & review loop."

## Full ultracode panel (creation + review)
Produce this card via the FABLE_TEMPLATE.md full ultracode panel: three composer agents in
parallel (each running the Render & review loop above to a finished candidate), three judges
scoring each against the Harris scan + axis guide, then synthesis / polish / integration
merging the strongest read. Three strategies to seed the composers:
- **A. Eye dominant** - the opening Eye of Horus/Shiva as the hero read, huge at the top
  with its ray-fan owning two thirds of the field; the tower, the falling garrison, and the
  maw are what the ray-fan lands on. The card as an act of seeing.
- **B. Mouth dominant** - the jaws of Dis as the hero read: the banded maw enlarged to
  command the lower half and bite upward into the frame, the tower toppling into it, the eye
  a small hard aperture at the top. The card as the letter Pe', devouring.
- **C. Tower-collapse dominant** - the fracturing fortress as the hero read, filling the
  centre and shearing apart into cubist facets with the garrison spraying out of it; eye and
  maw as the two poles that squeeze it. The card as an architecture failing.
Tier: **full panel** - an asymmetric, cubist, single-hue card whose composition is genuinely
contested (which of eye / mouth / tower is the subject); spend the full cost.
