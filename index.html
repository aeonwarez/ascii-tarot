||                   THE FOOL                    ||
||                ~ aleph · air ~                ||
`=(+)=========================================(+)=´

Must-have elements

  1. Central horned figure — pale, young, leaping mid-stride, head tilted back, both arms flung wide (Bacchus/Dionysus). The axis the card spins around.
  2. Prismatic rainbow vortex — spiral of colored light coiling outward: warm core (yellow) → blue → red → violet. The spiral IS the card.
  3. Solar emblem at the groin — small golden winged sun / solar disk. The "0 = All" made flesh.
  4. The dove ascending — upper area near an open hand, rising. Holy Ghost / Venus.
  5. The tiger — leaping at his lower right, teeth at his thigh. Fear/beast tearing at him, unheeded.
  6. The crocodile (Sebek) — below, half in the Nile at his feet. The devourer-initiator.
  7. Grapes + coins — vine/grape cluster (Bacchus fertility) and 2–3 coins carried loosely.
  8. The vulture (Maut) — upper corner, the mother-goddess bird.
  9. The butterfly — many-colored, fluttering (Greek psyche / soul).
  10. The caduceus / twin serpents — coiling near the figure, union of opposites.

||                   THE STAR                    ||
||              ~ heh · aquarius ~               ||
`=(+)=========================================(+)=´

Must-have elements

  1. Nuith personified — naked goddess kneeling (GD pose suggesting a whirling swastika).
  2. Golden cup held high, pouring on her own head — eternal renewal, "milk of the stars"; cups fashioned as breasts.
  3. Silver cup in lowered left hand, pouring on the land/water junction — Amrita, Nepenthe, blood of the Grail.
  4. Spiral vs. rectilinear energy — every form spiral EXCEPT the silver cup's straight streams: the illusion of straightness that blinds humanity.
  5. The celestial globe — "dominates the card"; the whole heavens (not Earth), pink/rose in the painting.
  6. Three seven-pointed Stars of Babalon/Venus — largest upper-left spinning CCW, middle on the sphere, smallest star-seed tumbling from the gold cup.
  7. Land/water junction — Sea of Binah meeting the fertile shore.
  8. Whirling clouds as her hair — "my hair the trees of Eternity," hiding the Abyss.
  9. Three roses, right corner — fertility, Venus, love.
  10. Star field — "nothingness with twinkles... but what twinkles!"
  11. River / hills — river flows at the bottom, hills on the distant shore.
  12. Five butterflies, lower right — liberated souls past the Abyss (5 = spirit).
  13. Crystalline earth — seven-sided translucent solids; the geometry of matter.
  14. Pyramid City across the sea — home of enlightened souls (Babalon, Lady of the City of Pyramids).

Must-have elements

  1. Bilateral symmetry — the whole card mirrors on the vertical axis.
  2. The great moon orb, top center — pale gold, waning, red serpentine swirl inside, flanked by the towers.
  3. The V-cone of tainted light — pale stream widening from the moon, narrowing DOWN to a flame-point at bottom center; serum tinged with blood.
  4. Nine drops of impure blood, Yod-shaped — exactly nine, falling inside the cone.
  5. Khephra the scarab bearing the solar disk — bottom, under the water, in a gold-ringed aura; the sun carried through midnight (emotional heart of the card).
  6. Two black towers of nameless mystery — on the hills, upper corners, hooded with tiny lit windows.
  7. Twin Anubis — jackal-headed guardians between the Ways, tall staffs, Mercury symbol in hand.
  8. The jackals at their feet — small black beasts on watch.
  9. Pool of blood-tinged water — horizontal ripple lines.
  10. Red-and-blue bell-curve wave-mountains — flanking the aura (Harris's signature waveforms).
  11. Starless indigo sky — keep it empty; the darkness IS the atmosphere.
