||                   THE FOOL                    ||
||                ~ aleph · air ~                ||
`=(+)=========================================(+)=´

Must-have elements

  1. Central horned figure — pale, young, leaping mid-stride, head tilted back, both arms flung wide (Bacchus/Dionysus). The axis the card spins around.
  2. Prismatic rainbow vortex — spiral of colored light coiling outward: warm core (yellow) → blue → red → violet. The spiral IS the card.
  3. Solar emblem at the groin — small golden winged sun / solar disk. The "0 = All" made flesh.
  4. The dove ascending — upper area near an open hand, rising. Holy Ghost / Venus.
  5. The tiger — leaping at his lower right, teeth at his thigh. Fear/beast tearing at him, unheeded.
  6. The crocodile (Sebek) — below, half in the Nile at his feet. The devourer-initiator.
  7. Grapes + coins — vine/grape cluster (Bacchus fertility) and 2–3 coins carried loosely.
  8. The vulture (Maut) — upper corner, the mother-goddess bird.
  9. The butterfly — many-colored, fluttering (Greek psyche / soul).
  10. The caduceus / twin serpents — coiling near the figure, union of opposites.

||                   THE MAGUS                   ||
||              ~ beth · mercury ~               ||
`=(+)=========================================(+)=´

Must-have elements

  1. Airborne Mercury-glyph youth — a naked, androgynous, winged-head-and-heels youth in dynamic swastika/thunderbolt motion; his body forms the alchemical glyph of Mercury (two serpents = horns above, wide foot-wings = the arrowhead below).
  2. The eight juggled objects — orbiting him in midair: Wand (phoenix wand), Cup (two-handled Grecian), Dagger, Disk (8-fold star of Mercury), Winged/Orphic Egg (spirit), Stylus, Scroll, and the Wand of Double Power (Horus / New Aeon).
  3. The great caduceus — full-height golden axis; rod reaching below his feet to the card's bottom, winged head spanning the top; a dove descends in its circle (eye of Horus, spirit into creation).
  4. Kether light above, Binah dark below — a white V-wedge of Kether light behind his head (a formless triangle the form-lines never enter); the dark womb of Binah below with an arc at the bottom birthing form.
  5. The lemniscate of serpents — two serpents twined as an infinity sign over his head, reaching down through his feet; left serpent wears the throne-headdress of Isis (Binah), right a plain crown (Kether) — uniting Kether and Binah.
  6. Winged sandals + serpent strap — the winged heels of Hermes the messenger; the sandal-strap a serpent shedding its skin (life anew).
  7. The Ape of Thoth (Cynocephalus) — groping up from the lower-right by his right foot: the distortion of truth inherent in speech and form. "Manifestation implies illusion."
  8. Golden sunburst — barely visible behind the left foot-wing: Mercury as herald of the Sun.
  9. Yellow caduceus outline — Vau of Tetragrammaton (union of Yod / phallus and He / dove).
  10. The two-way face — drawn to read looking up-left or down-right by focus, fitting his dual trickster nature (truth and falsehood, wisdom and folly).

||                 THE PRIESTESS                 ||
||               ~ gimel · moon ~                ||
`=(+)=========================================(+)=´

Must-have elements

  1. The veil of light — she is clothed only in the luminous veil; a stretched crystalline net of straight rays filling the whole field. Seen THROUGH it; nothing organic sits behind it.
  2. Enthroned figure + upsweeping arms + crescent cup — Isis the Eternal Virgin / Artemis; her arms sweep UP, pulling the webbing into the crescent bowl of a Moon-cup. Latticed body dissolves into the veil.
  3. Crown of Isis — moon-phase crown (waxing / full / waning), 7 crescents beneath (Venus / Empress link), an infinity sign about her eyes (Kether).
  4. Bilateral symmetry — mirrors on the vertical axis, "in exact balance in the middle pillar." Serenity IS the symmetry: "Change is stability."
  5. The lyre / bow / sistrum — the scrolled horizontal instrument forming the Moon-cup; willpower / true will and the AUM sound. Harris = sistrum, Crowley = bow of Artemis, reads as a three-stringed lyre.
  6. The two pillars — Mercy and Severity (RWS: Jachin & Boaz), faint verticals flanking her, spread by the veil and half-lost in the webbing.
  7. The camel (Gimel) — small and white, DEAD CENTER at the bottom between the two flowers, mirroring her seat between the pillars; carries the inner water across the desert.
  8. Crystalline foreground garden — yellow 10-petal spiral shell right (force / Chokmah), concave flower left (receptive / Binah), grapes (Dionysus), pine cone (Bacchus), Plato's-solid crystals.
  9. Inverted crescent moon on the throne base — the subconscious (also on the Moon card).
  10. Hidden Book of Mysteries under the lyre — esoteric knowledge, the path through hidden Daath.
  11. Full moon disk behind her head; star-points caught in the net's intersections.

`=(+)=========================================(+)=´

Must-have elements

  1. Enthroned goddess + Salt-glyph arms + Mater Triumphans — her arms trace the alchemical glyph of Salt: blue lotus lifted to her heart, the other arm curved low cradling a pregnant belly (Isis suckling Horus). Green skirt, red blouse.
  2. Crown of Isis + revolving moons — moon-phase crown topped by the Maltese cross (4 elements balanced); a waxing moon she faces and a waning moon, both revolving (balance of opposites).
  3. Twisted throne + sparrow & dove — uprights of blue twisted flame / blades of grass (birth from water), her two sacred birds of Venus perched at their tops.
  4. Venus / the vegetal whole — cool emerald + spring-green + sky-blue field of soft growth (not rays); golden zodiac girdle; Venus glyph hidden in the shield. Love unites Will (Chokmah) and Understanding (Binah).
  5. The lotus of Isis — blue, held over the heart chakra (heart-path, not head); roots in water, petals open to the Sun as the belly of the Grail. 4 petals = Chesed.
  6. Pelican + White Eagle heraldry — lower left: white pelican feeding its young from its own breast (Aeon of Osiris); lower right: shield with a white double-headed eagle holding the Moon (white tincture, New Aeon of Horus).
  7. Girdle of the Zodiac — golden belt of the twelve signs = her universality across all creation.
  8. Floor tapestry + Secret Rose — carpet of fleurs-de-lis (Binah / Trinity) and tiny fish adoring the white Secret Rose at the throne's base, over green-tinged waters.
  9. Robe details — bees (vitality) and black/white dominos (Chokmah-Binah) wrapped in spiral rose-petal lines (love uniting duality).
  10. The Arch / Door of Daleth behind her — the "Gate of Heaven"; antimony glyph (orb + cross) echoed at the crown.

||                  THE EMPEROR                  ||
||              ~ tzaddi · aries ~               ||
`=(+)=========================================(+)=´

Must-have elements

  1. Enthroned Emperor in the Sulphur posture — crowned king in scarlet regalia, frontal; his body forms the alchemical glyph of Sulphur: head + arms an upright triangle, crossed legs the cross below. He faces toward the Empress.
  2. The ram throne — the throne's arm-capitals are the heads of the Himalayan wild ram (Aries = a ram); a 16-point star disk (atomic number of sulphur; ChACh/Tzaddi = 16) on each throne arm.
  3. Dominant flame-red field + diagonal light — scarlet / brilliant flame red fills the card (Mars in Aries, Sulphur the fiery element); a shaft of white light descends diagonally from the upper right.
  4. Sceptre + orb — right hand bears a sceptre surmounted by a ram's head (raised into the light); left hand an orb topped with a Maltese cross at the navel (government established, creative rulership).
  5. Shield: red double-headed eagle + crimson disk — the alchemical red tincture (Sun / gold), consort to the Empress's white eagle (Moon / silver); its left corner brighter than the darker right.
  6. Lamb and Flag, couchant at his feet — the tamed ram (Agnus Dei), subservient, the Aeon of Osiris beneath the sovereign. "This is the theory of government."
  7. Four-point crown + Chesed — gold crown of 4 points, jewels of 4 facets; a Sun disk behind his head (Sol exalted in Aries); his legs' Four echoes Chesed (paternal Jupiter).
  8. Bees + fleur-de-lys on the robe — paternal power and rulership.
  9. Looping arrow-tipped lines across the robe — directed energy driven to specific tasks; the Emperor wastes nothing.
  10. Tzaddi note — letter Tzaddi ("fish hook") per Crowley's swap; qabalistically Netzach→Yesod, though Harris painted the light from the traditional Chokmah→Tiphareth direction.

Must-have elements

  1. Nuith personified — naked goddess kneeling (GD pose suggesting a whirling swastika).
  2. Golden cup held high, pouring on her own head — eternal renewal, "milk of the stars"; cups fashioned as breasts.
  3. Silver cup in lowered left hand, pouring on the land/water junction — Amrita, Nepenthe, blood of the Grail.
  4. Spiral vs. rectilinear energy — every form spiral EXCEPT the silver cup's straight streams: the illusion of straightness that blinds humanity.
  5. The celestial globe — "dominates the card"; the whole heavens (not Earth), pink/rose in the painting.
  6. Three seven-pointed Stars of Babalon/Venus — largest upper-left spinning CCW, middle on the sphere, smallest star-seed tumbling from the gold cup.

  8. Whirling clouds as her hair — "my hair the trees of Eternity," hiding the Abyss.
  9. Three roses, right corner — fertility, Venus, love.
  10. Star field — "nothingness with twinkles... but what twinkles!"
  11. River / hills — river flows at the bottom, hills on the distant shore.
  12. Five butterflies, lower right — liberated souls past the Abyss (5 = spirit).
  13. Crystalline earth — seven-sided translucent solids; the geometry of matter.
  14. Pyramid City across the sea — home of enlightened souls (Babalon, Lady of the City of Pyramids).
