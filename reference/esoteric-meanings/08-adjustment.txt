(iv) Justice vs Adjustment

Old-deck "Justice" = a human, relative judgement of legal implications, man's personal
discretion on the facts. ADJUSTMENT = karmic cause and effect, impersonal and universal.
The judgement is already made — balance is shown in the card's SYMMETRY; harmony
established after disharmony is cut away by her sword. Nature is not just; Nature is EXACT.

(v) Hebrew & Astrology

LAMED = OX-GOAD — the pointed stick that spurs and guides the ox (which is Aleph, the
Fool). A form of RESTRICTION / self-control: the ox goes only where the goad directs.
Astrology: LIBRA (cardinal air), ruled by VENUS (beauty; Tiphareth = beauty), SATURN
exalted (time, discipline). Equilibrium, karmic weighing, bringing a situation into
balance.

(vi) Tree of Life — Path 22: The Faithful Intelligence, GEBURAH to TIPHARETH.

Geburah = the Destroyer (the Sword); Tiphareth = the heart of man, Christ Consciousness.
Follow the true will and bring order to the chaos and infinite possibilities of the Fool,
using Geburah's destructive power to peel away all that does not serve the highest good,
revealing the heart-centre. Borne out of LOVE ("Love is the Law, Love under Will"). May
also happen karmically, beyond conscious control, when we stray. The figure is the He
Final of Tetragrammaton (lower consciousness) reabsorbed and raised — the Princess dying
to become the Queen.

(vii) Adjustment & the Fool

Lamed (L) + Aleph (A) = AL = 31, the most secret key of Liber AL, the title of the Book of
the Law; reversed, LA = "nothing." AL(2) and LA(0) give the Thelemic 0 = 2. Adjustment is
the FEMININE COMPLEMENT OF THE FOOL. The DIAMOND CURTAIN here links to the diamond in the
Fool's right hand.

(viii) Symbolism

FIGURE — a young, slender woman poised exactly on TIPTOE upon the very POINT of her sword,
perfectly balanced left-to-right and top-to-bottom. Masked, in chequered mask and leggings
of crossed diamonds = HARLEQUIN, the feminine counterpart of the Fool; diaphanous cloak of
"vivid wantonness," dancing wings. "The ultimate illusion which is manifestation... the
dance of life itself; all possibilities enjoyed under the phantom show of space and time."
CROWN OF MAAT — the ostrich plumes of the Twofold Truth; URAEUS serpent on the forehead
(concept; not in the final draft).
SWORD — the Phallic sword of the magician, held in both hands, blade BETWEEN HER THIGHS
(the daughter becoming the mother; "Love is the Law, Love under Will"); Geburah's weapon
cutting away what does not serve.
SCALES — suspend from the perfect point of her crown by CHAINS of Cause (black chains =
SATURN, exalted in Libra; time). ALPHA in the LEFT pan balanced against OMEGA in the RIGHT
— beginning and end of possibilities; the Two Witnesses, Judex and Testes of Final
Judgement.
THRONE / GEOMETRY — she poises before a throne of SPHERES and PYRAMIDS, four in number =
Law and Limitation. The lines from her toes to the scale pans to the crown-tip form a
LOZENGE / VESICA PISCIS (concealed diamond) through which the adjusted experience passes
to its next manifestation; links to the Fool's diamond.
CORNERS — balanced SPHERES of light and darkness; from them constantly equilibrated RAYS
form a FEATHERED CURTAIN, the interplay of all forces she sums up. Blue and green spheres
(masculine/feminine) in groups of 4 (Chesed).
GREEN BACKGROUND — Libra / Venus / autumn ("season of mists and mellow fruitfulness").

(x) Divinatory Meanings

BoT appendix: Justice, or rather justesse, the act of adjustment; suspension of all action
pending decision; in material matters, law suits or prosecutions; socially, marriage or
marriage agreements; politically, treaties.

Verse: "Balance against each thought its exact opposite. For the Marriage of these is the
Annihilation of Illusion."

Crowley renamed Justice to Adjustment: balance is ACTIVE, not passive — we maintain it by
adjusting to ever-changing circumstances. Self-control and self-order. Personal
responsibility; Karma — every act has consequences (this life, past, or future).

Upright: Take stock; keep what is useful, let go of what no longer serves; use the sword
(WILL) to act decisively and restore harmony, even breaking ties or habits. Weigh
consequences; see Karma acting personally and in the world. Live by your own truth (the
principles of Maat: truth, order, justice, righteousness, morality); all your actions
matter (the butterfly effect).

Reversed: Life out of balance; recognise it but be gentle with yourself; step back and
watch the results of your actions; grow stiller like the figure (meditate). Refusal to
change (Saturn/restriction); stuck in a rut though reaping poor consequences. Or: the
actions of others are unbalancing your life and you have not addressed it.
