(ii) Overview

Challenges conventional views of what a spiritual path/teacher should be. We are not
punished by a God with moral issues against our actions; guilt and self-inflicted
punishment are not necessary. Lust lets us experience unconventional life and develop
natural talents, but is not mere wanton indulgence (Debauch, seven of cups). Lust in its
HIGHER sense is the bliss of experiencing integration with something higher than yourself.
It relates to the intense attraction between polar opposites (the Lovers separated into
opposites, creating a tension that wants to resolve). Connected to the sexual act, creation
and life, but also very much to death and dissolving identity into something higher.
Crowley wanted the card to bring in the new Aeon of Horus (ruled by Aquarius and Leo).

(iv) The Tree of Life

Connects Chesed (Mercy) to Geburah (Power) — the highest ideals below the Abyss, so a high
vibration; NOT on the lower Tree. Its path is dissected by the High Priestess (Tiphareth to
Kether), connecting it to the Ethical triad. Chesed builds up, Geburah destroys — two
opposite processes. Emphasis on the expression of passion, living in the moment, casting off
the old to bring in the new. "Newness needs room to grow."

(v) Hebrew and Astrological Attribution

TETH = a serpent. Two aspects: (i) lowest: sexuality, sexual deviancy, lower mind, earthly
things (the seven deadly sins); (ii) highest: the KUNDALINI serpent, divine energy at the
base of the spine rising through the chakras. The serpent (top of the card) = renewal and
growth (sheds its skin). Sign LEO: the Lion, King of the Beasts, courage and leadership; we
need courage to accept all of who we are and merge with the ecstasy of spirit.

(vi) Symbology

(a) Middle third:
Scarlet Woman (Babalon) = the desires and pull of the lower nature (Crowley called his
personal Lovers the Scarlet Woman). Exhibiting ecstasy, holding up the Holy Grail.
Holy Grail = in her RIGHT hand (spiritual side); the Moon's energies (and the Sun's) flow
into the cup — hence the "battle" of the card's energy. It contains the male and female
sexual fluids: life and death, birth, death and renewal. Kundalini energy arises out of the
cup. She touches her breasts and vagina (strong sexual passion).
Red reins = in her LEFT hand (carnal side); red for passion; the reins = her connection to
the beast / lower nature.
Lion-Serpent = the phallus; the Scarlet Woman gazes at it; 13 rays from its head. 13 = the
Hebrew for Love (Achad) + Unity (Ahbah) ("There is no bond that can unite the divided but
love" AL I:41).
7 Heads of the Beast = aspects of Crowley (Book of Lies ch.49): 1 Angel (his mission /
spiritual impulse), 2 Saint (of the Gnostic Catholic Church), 3 Poet (his interest), 4
Adulterous Woman (his sexual preferences), 5 Man of Valour (himself), 6 Satyr (lustful,
drunken woodland god), 7 Lion-Serpent (Leo his ascendant).

(b) Upper third:
Horizon = the dawn of a new life / new perspective.
10 circular emanations = 10 energised sephiroth (internally and externally spiritualised);
they also appear below the horizon ("as above so below"); NOT ordered as on the Tree,
because a new Aeon is dawning and they need reorganising.
10 horns of the beast = serpent-like, destroying the old order to recreate a new Aeon
(mirrored by the Beast trampling the Saints below).

(c) Lower third:
Beast trampling on Saints = the beast ignores their "purity" (they deny their nature as
humans; we all have a carnal side, and repressing it is as much a burden as always
relenting — the Buddha's middle way). The figures resemble the Hebrew letter SHIN,
associated with the Aeon card (old religious orders replaced by a new philosophy).

(vii) Book of Revelations

Babalon is synonymous with Babylon in the Bible. Revelation 17: a woman on a scarlet beast
with seven heads and ten horns; 7 heads = 7 chakras, 10 horns = sephira on the Tree; the
woman in purple and scarlet with a golden cup = the alchemy of her actions; overcoming the
lower nature by the spiritual impulse.
