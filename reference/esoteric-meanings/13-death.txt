(ii) Overview

Death is the fundamental basis of a spiritual path: it leads from one transformation to
another as we shed the old and take on ever finer forms of identity resting in spirit
("die daily"). In a departure from Rider-Waite, the skeleton is at the BOTTOM OF THE OCEAN.
It is OSIRIS in the waters in which he died. Crowley's card shows transformation AND
resurrection — a glyph of the complete cycle, not an end.

(iii) Mythology

Seth chops Osiris into pieces, scatters them across Egypt. Isis collects every part but his
penis, which was swallowed by a FISH — hence the Hebrew letter NUN ("fish"). The fish became
a symbol of the FERTILITY of the dying god.

(v) The Tree of Life

Path 24, connecting TIPHARETH and NETZACH (Netzach, though Venusian, sits on the masculine
pillar). Also connected to BINAH, the "50 gates of death"; Binah = Understanding, and one
cannot understand the nature of God without dying. The 24th path = "Imaginative
Intelligence": imagination at its highest inspires the ascent from Netzach (emotions,
personality) to Tiphareth (spiritual beauty, Christ consciousness) — the dissolution of the
ego, the essence of the card.

(vi) Hebrew and astrological attribution

NUN = fish. Symbol of life beneath the waters, life travelling through the waters. The fish =
fertility of Osiris, a dying god; dying gods are solar, located in Tiphareth (Osiris the
precursor of Christ, Tammuz, Adonis, Attis, Dionysus). SCORPIO (Mars rules, Pluto exalted),
sexuality and death, the 8th house; in older cultures death and sex were synonymous. The fish
is also associated with semen.

(vii) Symbolism — the three forms of Scorpio

(i) SCORPION = lowest form of death, in the lower area between the ALPHA and OMEGA (a lily on
the left and a lotus on the right, both Saturnian, shaped as alpha/omega): physical life
trapped in the limits of TIME. Legend: a cornered scorpion stings itself to death — the gross
illusory side of death; by denying our infinite nature we die to living fully.
(ii) SERPENT / FISH = middle; life is a flux between conscious living and unconscious living
(death) — two curves of the one serpent. The masculine serpent and feminine fish in union =
the act of creation, placed just behind the origin of the phallus of Osiris.
(iii) EAGLE = highest, exalted form; death of the ego, oneness with God; the ecstasy of the
spiritual orgasm that initiates the creation of life; threads connect it to the creative
process.
The three also map the three states of water: Scorpion = ice (hard shell), Serpent = water
(flowing), Eagle = gas (rising).

Bubbles = soul incarnations of various ages (adult male, adult female, a child, a single
sperm), held together by threads forming the PHALLUS of Osiris. With his scythe Osiris
destroys life; with his phallus he creates it — an individual soul incarnating in different
lives through the creative will of God.
Osiris = BLACK skeleton (black = Saturn, which rules Binah: form/structure with decay
inherent, and "time"). He DANCES — change, transformation, preventing stagnation; the scythe
stirs up new-life bubbles from the decaying sediment. Wears the crown of Upper Egypt with the
FEATHERS OF MAAT (as in Adjustment and the 5 of Wands). His legs form the glyph of SATURN.
Process of death = two figures between the legs: the LOWER figure being beheaded as the scythe
crosses its neck; the UPPER figure arising from the head after death, enveloped in bright
light — the higher mind freed from the "death" of the lower vehicles. "Beheading" = the
separation of the higher centres (head) from the carnal desires (body), an analogy, NOT
literal (as misread in scripture).

(viii) Hanged-Man-to-Death transition

Death here = dying to our emotions and personality (Netzach); the outcome of the surrender in
the Hanged Man, a transition to a new, more contented self. Old habits lose their grip. Like
becoming child-like again (the Waite card's infant does not fear change) but with adult
experience. Personality is surrendered/neutralised so the spirituality within can express;
the sense of "self" as personality dies, transcended in meditation. Warning: a balanced,
free-flowing personality can feel like great freedom and be seductive — but it is still a
self based in personality, and it will not last; do not settle for the short-term freedom.

(ix) Divinatory meanings

Upright: (1) something is dying/dissolving — a structure, pattern, habit, or way of thinking
— necessary for new beginnings; (2) great transformation, facing your darkest fears, the
darkest hour before dawn; (3) abandon the old; death protects against stagnation; (4) an
active life is continually changing as we follow our purpose — death as servant to our
evolution; (5) crisis that must be met with confidence in the future; (6) a time to cut away
what does not serve.
Reversed: resistance to change, reluctance to let go of the past; life stagnated, stuck in a
rut; issues to resolve that hold you in the moment; feeling trapped like the scorpion by
time/limits; a fear of death itself needing higher insight.

Title-verse (BoT / DuQuette): "The Universe is Change; every Change is the effect of an Act
of Love; all Acts of Love contain Pure Joy. Die daily! Death is the apex of one curve of the
snake Life: behold all opposites as necessary complements, and rejoice."
