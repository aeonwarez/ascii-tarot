(ii) Thoth Hierophant

Hierophant = one who interprets sacred mysteries or esoteric principles.

(iv) Tree of Life — Path 16: Eternal Intelligence

The Hierophant forms a bridge between the Supernal Triangle and the remaining
sephiroth, occupying the path between CHOKMAH and CHESED. He 'hears' the Divine Will
from above and transmits it WITHOUT BIAS. Summary: "the learning and teaching of cosmic
law." The wisdom/force flowing through Chokmah (2nd sephira) is organised in Chesed
(4th sephira) into the word of God — structure, traditions, organisations classically
associated with religious groups.

(v) Hebrew & Astrology

VAU = NAIL = that which JOINS. The Hierophant joins the macrocosm (universe) with the
microcosm (the spiritual seeker) through esoteric teaching. Vau = 6 = hexagram = the
macrocosm (the cross as the unfolded cube; the hidden 7th side is spirit).

Astrology: TAURUS (Earth sign) — tangible, material teachings through books, internet,
study, classes, physical masters on the earth plane. Taurus ruled by VENUS; the MOON is
EXALTED in Taurus (subconscious/meditative quality; the 'hearing' is information coming
from the spiritual realms).

(vi) Symbolism — 4 sections: Background, The Hierophant, Isis, Child of Horus

BACKGROUND:
- Dark BLUE = NUIT, from whose womb everything is born ("the All, pure potentiality").
- SNAKE and DOVE around the window = snake (internal, kundalini, rising up the spine
  through the 1000-petal lotus) + dove (spirit coming into matter); together the
  ever-flowing energy between spirit and matter. Link to Mars / the Tower card.
- 9 NAILS in the top background fix the oriel (window) where the light of the higher
  sephiroth (Supernal Triangle) shines through to the seeker. Nine = the 9th sephira
  YESOD, ruled by the Moon (Moon exalted in Taurus, Taurus rules the Hierophant).
- ROSE (of five petals, in the oriel behind the phallic headdress) = VENUS = LOVE that
  unites the higher and lower of creation.
- 4 CHERUBS/KERUBS in the corners = the fixed signs of the zodiac.
- GEOMETRICAL SHAPES: a HEXAGRAM imposed over the Hierophant; within it an inverted
  pentagram which in turn contains an UPRIGHT PENTAGRAM = union of macrocosm (hexagram)
  with microcosm (man, 5 senses, the pentagram).

THE HIEROPHANT:
- CROWN of Osiris.
- STAFF (right hand) = glyph of the 3 Aeons: 3 interlocking rings = Isis, Osiris, Horus.
  Upper ring SCARLET = Horus (most recent Aeon, logically on top). 2 lower rings: GREEN
  for Isis, PALE YELLOW for Osiris (colours named by Crowley in BoT but not painted in
  the card). All on deep indigo (Saturn, Lord of Time — he moves only every 2,000 yrs).
- BLESSING SIGN (left hand): 2 fingers up, 2 fingers down = a bridge between heaven and
  earth (like the Rider-Waite Magician gesture).
- THRONE = a BULL (Taurus, earth) — grounding his esoteric teaching on the earthly
  plane, practical teaching to the masses. Also linked to Apis, the Bull God of Memphis.
- ELEPHANTS outside the Hierophant = also Taurean, beasts of burden.

ISIS (Venus, the Scarlet Woman):
- Isis = Venus, but NOT passive — armed with a SWORD. The Scarlet Woman of the New Aeon
  of Horus, on a par with her male counterpart. "Let the woman be girt with a sword
  before me" (Liber AL III:11). "Venus as she now is... no longer the mere vehicle of
  her male counterpart, but armed and militant."
- SWORD = the phallus. Moon exalted in Taurus → the Moon's influence shown by the woman
  AND the 9 nails (Yesod). She relates to the 5-petal rose in the window. Moon + bow
  link her to the Priestess (both carry Artemis's bow).

CHILD OF HORUS:
- Emanates from the woman; appears in the 5-pointed star (pentagram) on the Hierophant's
  breast. Woman = Isis (wife of Osiris, mother of Horus) → the 3 Aeons again.
- Horus DANCES, playful, open body — where the two adult figures are rigid. He is the
  Son / Vau of Tetragrammaton (like the Hierophant's own Hebrew letter).
- SANDAL STRAP on the right foot = the 5th power of the Sphinx, TO GO (spirit, freedom).
  The 5 powers: To Know, To Dare, To Will, To Keep Silent, To Go/Spirit (added by
  Crowley); the first four = the four elements.
- The upright pentagram containing the child implies the GOLDEN RATIO (Phi) = beauty,
  perfect proportion, harmony. The dancing child = the New Aeon of Horus, spirit brought
  down into the world through teaching, set free, not restrained by religious boundaries.

(vii) Emperor → Hierophant transition
Emperor = leadership, self-control, discipline, command of the path. Hierophant =
applying spiritual realisations in the day-to-day world; the spiritual group, rules of
conduct, self-study; EXTERNAL actions and how we apply knowledge.

(viii) Hierophant vs High Priestess
Hierophant = OUTER guide: practical actions, structure, books, a master, church,
courses, traditions. Connects the Will of God (Chokmah) into structure (Chesed).
Priestess = INNER guide: intuition, meditation, source; connected DIRECTLY to Kether.

(ix) Divinatory Meanings

Upright: Traditional religious teaching, or learning fundamentals before making your own
decisions. Follow rules/regulations/traditions (structure and meaning; group/org
membership). A period of intense learning/education. Follow 'tried and trusted' means
rather than novel tangents (a grounded path). Becoming a mentor, teaching fundamentals.
Marriage (an institution) or a position in an established order.

Reversed: Trapped by following religion/doctrine blindly without questioning, or in
conflict with it. Workplace problems from rules/restrictions. An over-controlling
spiritual master allowing no personal freedom. Too much reliance on traditional teaching
and not following your inner guide/intuition (High Priestess). Lost control of your
life's structure; rebelling against social norms. Going off at a tangent when
problem-solving instead of tried-and-tested methods.
