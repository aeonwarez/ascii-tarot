(ii) Thoth Lovers

Far more symbolism than the Waite card; heavy alchemy that began with the Magus. It is
the FIRST card to hold more than one figure and therefore represents SEPARATION. In
summary a glyph of DUALITY. Fully understood only in relation to Atu XIV (Art). One of
the most complex cards in the pack.

(iv) Tree of Life — Path 17: The Disposing Intelligence, BINAH to TIPHARETH.

Binah is "the birth of form," so the unity of God is lost — the Lovers carries the idea
of DIVISION. We must understand the parts of ourselves, surrender what does not serve,
to reach "Victory" (Tiphareth, also Christ Consciousness).

(v) Hebrew & Astrology

ZAYIN = SWORD — a blade has two edges; division/analysis. The exact OPPOSITE of the
Hierophant's nail (which binds). The sword makes duality out of unity, form from the
formless, separates night from day, pleasure from pain; through it we recognise our
individuality, and it drives our wish to connect with another.
Astrology: GEMINI (mutable air, The Twins) — a CHOICE must be made; extends the duality.
Mercury rules; Dragon's Head (North Node) exalted.

(vi) Alchemy — SOLVE et COAGULA

SOLVE (separating) = the process of the Lovers: break an impure metal (an aspect of
self) into parts and purify them. COAGULA (combining) = the Art card (Atu XIV): the
purified parts reintegrated into wholeness. Lovers = 4th alchemical trump. Magus =
Mercury unites Sulphur (Emperor) and Salt (Empress); here all three meet.

(vii) Symbolism

HERMETIC MARRIAGE — a royal wedding of the black King and white Queen, officiated by a
hooded figure (a form of the HERMIT / of Mercury). The Hermit = creative power of the
universe (the Yod) and fertility; his SCROLL = the word/intelligence of the LOGOS,
twisted round his arms into a mobius/eternal loop. He makes the SIGN OF THE ENTERER
(also Benediction and Consecration), offering the intelligence of the universe. He is
CLOAKED — the mysteries are hidden beyond eye and intellect. WHITE LIGHT behind his head
= the Light of Kether.

CUPID / EROS above — son of Hermes, blindfolded, golden wings, golden quiver inscribed
THELEMA (Greek). Blindfold = the unconscious desire of the soul to unite opposites.
His ARROW = directed WILL, spiritual intelligence — "Love is the Law, Love under Will."
The downward arrow shot from Kether toward Chokmah (the topmost point of the Yod).

BACKGROUND — EVE upper-RIGHT (life; the serpent entwined, hood spread behind her head),
LILITH upper-LEFT (death; Kali-like but more seductive). They are the duality of Binah
(life/creation and death/decay). ARCH OF SWORDS overhead (Zain) = the gateway to the
white Spiritual Light of Kether; the Royal Marriage takes place beneath it. YELLOW HUES =
Tiphareth.

KING and QUEEN — KING = Emperor = red tincture = Sulphur; GOLD crown (Sun), 5 points
(Geburah), snakes coiled into the lemniscate on his robe (birth-death-rebirth), holds the
SACRED LANCE (phallus). QUEEN = Empress = white tincture = Salt; SILVER crown (Moon), orb
& cross (Chesed), bees on her robe (fertility), holds the HOLY GRAIL (womb) bearing the
DOVE of the Holy Spirit (Venus/Love) with 5 white rays (spirit). Their free hands are
JOINED (consenting to the marriage). ORANGE ROBE where the King's red and Queen's yellow
overlap, carrying serpent and bee (seen again in Art).

FOREGROUND — RED LION (with the King) = Leo = Fire; its tail rises between its legs (erect
phallus) with 5 kinks (Geburah). WHITE EAGLE (with the Queen) = Scorpio = Water; tail = 4
feathers (Chesed). The two hold the Chesed-Geburah axis, opposites emphasised.
TWINS (Gemini) attend, counterchanged: BLACK child opposite the white Queen, WHITE child
opposite the black King. White child carries 4 white ROSES (Chesed, receptive/female);
black child carries a CLUB (Geburah, phallic/male) and the father's Lance. They stand on
the wings of the ORPHIC EGG, haloed white — intimately linked to it.

ORPHIC EGG (bottom) — grey speckled black-and-white (the Supernal Triangle; the high
spiritual nature of its contents); product of the King and Queen's union. A WINGED SERPENT
= HADIT (Kether-like); the egg opens and Kether's potential becomes the Universe,
sacrificing unity for diversity. Serpent purple (Mercury, Queen scale), wings crimson
(Binah, King scale). CONCEALED BOW at the bottom — the black twin's lance is also its
arrow; links the Lovers to Art (Sagittarius, the Archer).

(ix) Divinatory Meanings

Layers: between two people; balance of opposites within the self (inner marriage);
connection between the self and God (soul unification); brotherhood/sisterhood under a
common purpose.

BoT appendix: Openness to inspiration, intuition, intelligence, second sight,
childishness, frivolity, thoughtfulness divorced from practical consideration,
indecision, self-contradiction, union in a shallow degree with others, instability,
contradiction, triviality, the "high-brow."

Verse: "The Oracle of the Gods is the Child-Voice of Love in Thine own Soul; hear thou
it. Heed not the Siren-Voice of Sense, or the Phantom-Voice of Reason: rest in
Simplicity, and listen to the Silence."

Upright: The card is essentially about CHOICE — who you want to be, how you connect and
on what level. A moral dilemma; consider consequences; take the higher path. Can signify
a beautiful soul-connection relationship / soul mate. Self-love is the key to a lasting
bond. Time with like-minded people; a longing for union of soul with its spiritual source.

Reversed: Depth of feeling not mutual; restore balance or accept you have grown apart.
Out of sync with loved ones; strained relationships, hard communication. Not enough
self-love/self-respect. No one to share values with; imbalanced within, lost sight of
self; loneliness; feeling cut off from the spiritual source through inner conflict.
