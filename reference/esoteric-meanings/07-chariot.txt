(iv) Tree of Life — Path 18: The House of Influence, BINAH to GEBURAH.

Binah = the great sea where "form and structure" take birth, highest on the Right Pillar.
Geburah (Mars) = both the source of the Charioteer's movement (spiritual impulse) and the
destructive force that breaks down his limits of perception and attachments, expanding
consciousness. The path crosses the ABYSS; the Charioteer must lose his ego moving from
Geburah (5th) to Binah (3rd). Strong link to the (High) Priestess (Cancer = house of the
Moon).

(v) Hebrew & Astrology

CHETH = FENCE / FIELD / enclosure — the container for the soul within the body, just as
the Charioteer's armour sheathes him. Pertains to destroying the fences (barriers) in the
way of progress; when these "identity" fences dissolve, the field of view on life widens.
CHETH = 418 = ABRAHADABRA.
Astrology: CANCER (cardinal water, the first onrush of the element). Sensitive, hence the
"suit of armour." Soul lesson "Peace in Action." Luna rules; Jupiter exalted.

Symbolism

CHARIOTEER — has no reins; does NOT drive the chariot; his one function is to bear the
HOLY GRAIL. Throned, in a meditative posture (will turned inward). Vizor covers the face
("no man may look upon his face and live" — to see his true identity is to die to the
current perspective). Full AMBER armour, no flesh exposed. CRAB crest on the helmet
(Cancer). 10 STARS / sapphires on the armour = the 10 sephiroth in Assiah (celestial dew
inherited from his mother Binah). RED cloak (passion; Geburah/Mars destroying limits) +
WHITE cloak (purity of purpose). Sits on the Moon (not visible) — rides above the tides
of emotion.

HOLY GRAIL — the central, most important feature: pure AMETHYST (Jupiter, exalted in
Cancer), its shape suggesting the FULL MOON and the Great Sea of BINAH. In its centre is
RADIANT BLOOD (spiritual life; light in the darkness) whose rays REVOLVE (Jupiter). Four
SPIRAL handles = Chesed (Jupiter, the last sephira before the Abyss). Crowley identifies
the Grail with sexual alchemy — the Two-in-One elixir of male and female.

CANOPY — night-sky-blue of BINAH, starry. ABRAHADABRA embroidered across it (disguised as
ABRACADABRA — a Lady Harris typo); the word of the New Aeon of Horus, 418, the marriage of
microcosm (pentagram, 5 vowels) and macrocosm (hexagram, 6 consonants).
FOUR PILLARS = the four letters of Tetragrammaton, the four pillars of the Universe,
linking macrocosm and microcosm. SCARLET WHEELS = Geburah/Mars, the original energy
causing the revolving motion.
FOUR SPHINXES draw the chariot, each composed of the four Kerubs with elements
counterchanged = the 16 sub-elements: LION (Leo, fixed fire), EAGLE (Scorpio, fixed
water), MAN (Aquarius, fixed air), BULL (Taurus, fixed earth).

(x) Divinatory Meanings

BoT appendix: Triumph, victory, hope, memory, digestion, violence in maintaining
traditional ideas, the "die-hard," ruthlessness, lust of destruction, obedience,
faithfulness, authority under authority.

Verse: "The Issue of the Vulture, Two-in-One, conveyed; this is the Chariot of Power.
TRINC: the last oracle."

An armoured vehicle moving with haste in one direction, blind to left and right, stopping
for nothing — a symbol of VICTORY achieved by unwavering progress and dominion over wild
forces (the sphinxes). If the beasts rebel, conflict and disorientation.

Upright: Faith in going with the flow of the universe (the Charioteer does not control the
sphinxes); the path will have challenges, like a river routed around obstacles. Not
passive — inner strength and determination (the meditative posture); act toward a chosen
goal even out of your comfort zone, protected by the armour of inner confidence.

Reversed: Slow down; make sure actions rest on solid foundations; do not get out of your
depth too fast. A tendency to exert too much control (forcing the sphinxes), hampering the
natural flow; ease off, loosen the reins. Connect with what you truly think and feel and
align with the forces pulling you before acting.
