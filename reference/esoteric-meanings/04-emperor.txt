(ii) Thoth Emperor

"Emperor" means "he who sets in order." He looks toward the Empress. She is the
universal, the totality, the LOVE that binds creation; he is Lord of his personal
Universe (Kingdom) and must learn that self-sacrifice is necessary to be a great
ruler. His body forms a TRIANGLE on a CROSS = the alchemical symbol of SULPHUR.
The dominant RED of the card = Mars in Aries.

(iii) Mythology

Consort to the Empress, whom he faces. She is Venus (Aphrodite), he is Mars.
Venus/Love in a passionate affair with Mars/War. The ruler understands what can
and cannot be controlled; success is knowing the difference. To keep power he
must meet others' needs as well as his own (Atu 4 = stability, security). The
lesson: to conquer you must also serve, and father your offspring (in the world
and through the Empress); "true gain comes through surrender" (Christ's
self-sacrifice).

(iii) Rider Waite Emperor

Stone throne, four rams' heads (Aries / Mars); ankh (life) in right hand, orb
(dominion) in left.

Empress & Tree of Life — Path 28: The Path of Natural Intelligence

"Natural" = the vital / life forces in Yesod and Netzach. Book of Thoth says the
white light in the far right corner = the wisdom of Chokmah (the Emperor's
authority) directed upon Tiphareth (the heart of man) — TRADITIONALLY the
Emperor's path. BUT Crowley later SWAPS the Emperor with the Star ("Tzaddi is not
the Star"), so updated the Emperor sits on the path between NETZACH and YESOD:
- Netzach = ruled by Venus (links him to the Empress); emotion, enjoyment,
  pleasure, bliss.
- Yesod = the final FILTER organising the forces of the Tree before Malkuth;
  associated with the ASTRAL LIGHT the magician manipulates; root chakra =
  sexuality and drive.
So the Emperor regulates his emotions and desires and REDIRECTS his sexual
energy to manifest his will in the world (like the Yogi raising Kundalini) —
another form of the "sacrifice" he makes for the greater good.

Hebrew & Astrology

TZADDI. Pictograph = a man on his side; later defined as "fish hook" from its
shape. "Fishing" = searching for information into the unknown, i.e. meditation,
searching for answers in the depths of the divine waters. Astrology: Aries (Mars
rules, Sol exalted).

Symbolism

- Triangle above cross = domination of spirit over matter; also SULPHUR, the
  fiery, volatile element of the universe; an active (not passive) personality.
- Crown = 4 points = CHESED; jewels have 4 facets; gold crown = the Sun behind
  him (Sol exalted in Aries).
- Number Four = the shape of his legs = Chesed (4th sephira, ruled by Jupiter);
  strong Chesed association throughout.
- Orb with Maltese cross = in his LEFT hand, over the navel / procreative
  chakra = his creative energy and ability to govern (energy reached a
  successful issue; government established).
- Bees on garment = energy, industriousness, organisation = his ability to
  govern; a leader.
- Looping lines terminating in arrowheads = directed energy to specific tasks;
  he does not waste energy.
- Star disk on throne = one on each arm; 16 points = the atomic number of
  SULPHUR; 16 = gematria of ChACh ("hook") = the meaning of Tzaddi.
- Fleur-de-lys = symbol of rulership (French monarchy) = the theme of rulership.

Alchemical attributes:
- The Emperor IS SULPHUR — one of the three alchemical forms (with Mercury and
  Salt). Sulphur = the ACTIVE part of Nature that stimulates Salt to maintain
  equilibrium; masculine, active, fiery; the vehicle for manifestation into
  form (hence the Chesed links).
- Sceptre with Ram's Head = held to receive light from the RIGHT side of the
  card, channelled down to the shield; ram's head = Aries.
- Shield = the RED TINCTURE = purified sulphur, analogous to Gold and the Sun
  (vs the Empress's white tincture / Moon / silver). Contrasting energies.
- Golden Double-Headed Eagle = like the phoenix, regenerative; a red disk
  behind it.
- Lamb and Flag (Agnus Dei) = the Lamb of God; Old Testament blood sacrifice,
  Christian symbol of Christ's sacrifice; the Aeon of Osiris. Here the lamb is
  TAMED, subservient to the Emperor, having lost its independence and drive.
- Alchemy rules the card: the left corner of the shield is much brighter than
  the darker right corner, emphasising Crowley's alchemy / New Aeon of Horus
  over the traditional Ram / Aries / religion association.

The Empress to Emperor Transition

Leadership, responsibility, self-control, discipline, focus. After setting goals
with the Magus, connecting with the soul in the Priestess, and tasting LOVE with
the Empress, we take responsibility for our life and actions. We must stand for
something and live accordingly. Yet, like the Emperor, understand self-sacrifice
for the greater good (surrender the individual Atman into the great Brahman).
Foster discipline and focus on the spiritual journey.

Divinatory Meanings

Upright: Taking responsibility for others (family relying on you for security).
A natural leader and organiser, fair, prefers to be your own boss. Break problems
into manageable chunks and work systematically. Someone who has lived many life
lessons and can help others. Success through self-discipline, logic, rationality.
Learning to sacrifice yourself for a greater cause; only by surrender do we gain
spiritually.

Reversed: Not sticking to goals / lacking focus and discipline. Under an overly
dominant figure who stifles creativity; an overbearing, possessive partner. Poor
relationship with the father. Over-intellectualising. Lack of ambition and
direction. Wanting to break free from rules / regulations. Viewing the spiritual
path as what you can get rather than give. Over-controlling, dominant, cruel,
violent or insecure; suppressing your feminine / softer side.
