(ii) Overview

A glyph for the mechanism of how the Universe works, expressed as the 3 alchemical
principles spinning on the wheel of life. The wheel has 10 spokes and spins
counter-clockwise, drawing 10 plumes of energy like a whirlpool.

(iv) The Tree of Life

Connects Chesed to Netzach (mercy/benevolence with the emotions and pleasure). Exists
solely on the pillar of mercy; opposite is the Hanged Man. Chesed = 4 = structure, the
number of the Tetragrammaton and materialism. 10 spokes of wheel = Malkuth = the physical
world / manifestation.

(v) Hebrew and Astrological Attribution

KAPH = palm of the hand = turns the wheel (we create our own destiny; the palm also
contains our destiny, an imprint of the Soul's life path). JUPITER = growth, expansion,
good luck; the "Greater Benefic."

(vi) Alchemy and Symbology

Firmament of stars at top = the Goddess Nuit, the cosmic clock. 5-pointed stars = pentagram
and man ("Every man and woman is a star"). Stars distorted to show our uniqueness; some
dark, some bright (differing fortunes), but balanced (harmony in the universe).
10-pointed star in centre of upper wheel = reflected in a 10-pointed star in the lower wheel
("As above, so below"). 10 = completion / fulfilment; the image of the manifested universe
in its completion.
3 symbols on the wheel = 3 alchemical substances / 3 gunas of Hindu philosophy.

Sphinx (at TOP) = carries a sword (mind, willpower); represents the power of SULPHUR (heat,
explosiveness, as the Emperor), not Mercury.
Typhon (on RIGHT) = SALT, death or stagnation (as the Empress); salt symbol on his head
behind the eye; an inverted Ankh (contradicts the principle of life/motion); serpentine,
associated with hell.
Hermanubis (on LEFT) = a variation of Anubis; a monkey; the Greek version of Anubis
(Hermes/Mercury + Anubis); a psychopomp (mediator/guide for souls of the dead, as in the
Hermit). Associated with Air (Mercury, the Magus). Anubis = Egyptian god of mummification &
the weighing of the heart; Thoth also present at that scene.

All the characters are Greek in origin, so the card is more Greek than Egyptian despite the
Sphinx. Zeus (Jupiter) rules the card; Jupiter rules Chesed. Jupiter = the Demiurge (a god
of the material universe who believes he is the highest, too self-indulgent to realise he
was created by something higher; Chesed is the last sephira before the Abyss, clinging to
the last vestiges of form). Zeus meddles in human affairs as the Wheel of Fortune / Karma
meddles in the lives of men; Fortune is in some ways blind.

Lightning bolts = Jupiter (Zeus), striking Typhon.
Triangle within wheel = the 3 gunas stabilised; within its centre is the Axle of the wheel
(cf. the masonic all-seeing eye) = ENLIGHTENMENT, understanding, clarity.
Centre of wheel = Kether, Hadit, the point. Spokes and gunas are emanations from this point.
In the centre is the symbol of the Sun (Christ the Sun-god); Kether = the Will of God, so
all other aspects are just extensions of that will (everything that happens to us is God's
Will).
Fist at bottom = 6 rays emanating (Kaph = fist / closed hand); the wheel turned by the hand
of God. 6 = the Sun, Tiphareth, Christ consciousness / single eye.

Summary of the Gunas: the Universe seems to oppress us and we feel powerless (Typhon); we
are goaded into action by will (Hermanubis / Mercury / the Magus) and move to the top of the
wheel, taking on the Sulphur of the Sphinx to resolve the situation into harmony
(Adjustment) and attain Victory (Netzach). The cycle can represent one lifetime or many.

(viii) Hermit to Fortune transition

The Hermit is being centred and strong within to follow one's true calling; that
centredness lets us stay at the centre of the Wheel of Fortune, strong within as life goes
through its endless cycles of change and difficulty.

(ix) Divinatory Meanings

Crowley means Fortune to represent GOOD FORTUNE. It shares the Fool's spontaneity /
randomness as the wheel spins, but with more organisation. Represents an unexpected change
that will be favourable but may not seem so at first.

Upright: Be ready to act on unexpected opportunities. Go with the flow of life; let good
fortune come; don't cling to the status quo. Enjoy good times but don't become complacent;
in challenging times don't be discouraged (things can only get better). Keep optimistic,
have faith in the Universe. Change is about to happen soon; you may need to alter course.
If we struggle against the flow, we struggle to ascend the Wheel or are crushed beneath it.
