(ii) Overview

Temperance was renamed ART by Crowley for the ART OF ALCHEMY (same theme as Temperance:
balance, harmony, moderation, the "middle way"). The central figure is an ANDROGYNE (both
masculine and feminine), related to the Roman goddess Diana. Waite's Temperance angel already
shows the integration of fire and water; Art carries it into full alchemical symbolism.

(iv) The Tree of Life

Path 25, connecting TIPHARETH (Sun) and YESOD (Moon) on the MIDDLE PILLAR. Tiphareth =
manifesting beauty, the Christ centre; Yesod = the subconscious, which like dreams throws up
unresolved material. Art integrates the two: bringing the unresolved to the surface and
purifying it in the higher self — a challenging process that tempers our nature toward balance.

(v) Hebrew and astrological attribution

SAMEKH = a prop / crutch. Giving up our material props and trusting the spiritual path
enriches life; facing the fears in the subconscious, we throw away the prop of comfort and
move to a place of inner beauty. Ruled by SAGITTARIUS (Jupiter rules, Dragon's Tail / South
Node exalted).

(vi) The integration of opposites

Art is OPPOSITE the Lovers in the zodiac; both are the alchemical marriage. The Lovers = SOLVE
(dissolving, the pain of separation); Art = COAGULA (reassembling, reintegration and harmony).
Separate a substance, refine it, recombine it and you have something NEW and improved — the
spiritual seeker purified and reintegrated, a better conduit for spirit into matter.
1. The King and Queen of the Lovers merge into ONE two-headed androgyne. Arms swapped: the
black arm goes with the white face, the white arm with the black face. Blond hair now on the
dark-skinned side; dark curly hair on the white side. The White King's SILVER crown now on
the Black Queen; the Black Queen's GOLD crown now on the White King. Robe decorated with BEES
(fertility) and SERPENTS (renewal of life's cycles), from the Emperor/Empress/Lovers, now
united in a higher octave.
2. LION and EAGLE, colours interchanged from the Lovers: the red lion is now WHITE, the white
eagle now RED. They stand on BURNING WATER (union of fire and water) and trade their gluten
and blood in the cauldron between them.
3. CAULDRON = between the figure's feet; bears the RAVEN on a SKULL (the caput mortuum,
linking to the Death card — the "dross" filtered out in purification). Cauldron is YELLOW =
Tiphareth (Sun), balance and harmony of its contents. TAU CROSS on the rim = integration of
elements / new life. The androgyne pours WATER (silver chalice, one hand) and FIRE (torch,
other hand) into it.
4. Behind: a huge golden ORPHIC EGG (from the Lovers), everything happening in front of it is
the secret work inside. A golden AURA / RAINBOW carries the motto VISITA INTERIORA TERRAE
RECTIFICANDO INVENIES OCCULTUM LAPIDEM — "visit the interior parts of the earth; by
rectification (purification) thou shalt find the hidden stone" (Enlightenment / philosopher's
stone). Initials = VITRIOL, the balance of the three alchemical principles (sulphur, mercury,
salt / the three gunas).

(vii) Additional symbolism

Artemis/Diana = the central Moon-goddess figure; her BOW transformed into the MOONS at the top
of the card. Green robe = the feminine / fertility (Spring) from the union of Emperor and
Empress; with the Sun in the heart region and abundant green, a strong fertility message.
LAMEN of 6 spheres on the breast (heart region) = 6, the Sun, Tiphareth: a feminine body with
masculinity ruling the heart. Diana of Ephesus was many-breasted — the deep neckline reveals
three columns of stacked breasts. On the breastplate an ARROW rests on the sphere of Yesod
(Moon) and points up toward Tiphareth — the path Art represents; directed WILL integrating the
subconscious (Yesod) with the Christ centre (Tiphareth). A stream of light rises up the figure
forming RAINBOWS on the breastplate and in the background. Two BOWS near the top; a tiny
upward ARROW in the rainbow fumes of the cauldron.

(viii) Death-to-Art transition

Death purges what no longer serves; Art is the step of working on ourselves while spirit works
with us to reintegrate into a higher evolved being — potentially difficult but bringing greater
harmony.

(ix) Divinatory meanings

Upright: learning to bring balance and moderation; taking the middle road, avoiding extremes,
keeping calm under stress; higher learning and integration of what had been holding you back;
adaptation and coordination with others from inner stability; patience and conscious intent
("do not rush; let the situation evolve"); a period of self-evaluation and re-examined
priorities, seeking balance between inner and outer selves and a higher meaning — expect a
clash between old habits and the new evolved vision, met with calm determination.
Reversed: something out of balance, in conflict or excessive, creating stress; impatience or
refusing the moderate approach (hasty action worsens imbalance); a time to reflect on needed
changes; return to the Death card's energy to see what to leave behind; uncooperative people
or competing interests; unresolved conflicts not yet expressed.

Title-verse (BoT / DuQuette): "Pour thine all freely from the Vase in thy right hand, and lose
no drop! Hath not thy left hand a vase? Transmute all wholly into the Image of thy Will,
bringing each to its token of Perfection! Dissolve the Pearl in the Wine-cup: drink, and make
manifest the Virtue of that Pearl!"
