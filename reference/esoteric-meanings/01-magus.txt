(ii) Earlier Versions

The Juggler / "Le Bateleur" (bearer of the Baton = Mercury, bearer of the wand
transmitting the Will / Word of cosmos). Crowley: the medieval Juggler is "one of
the crudest and least satisfactory in the mediaeval pack" (BoT p.70).

(iv) Thoth Magus = Hermes Trismegistus

Based on Greco-Egyptian tradition. "Trismegistus" = "thrice great": a messenger of
the Gods (Mercury), Wisdom (Thoth), and magical/alchemical (Hermes). Three cultures:
- Roman Mercury: messenger between Gods and mortals; "word of creation whose speech
  is silence" (BoT p.70); psychopomp (guides the dead to Hades).
- Egyptian Thoth: ibis-headed; the ibis stands on one leg = powers of concentration,
  "a symbol of the meditative spirit" (BoT p.71) — helps the Magus channel spiritual
  energy from above.
- Greek Hermes: hermetic; alchemy, magic.

(v) Variations: three versions painted by Harris; only the one approved by Crowley is
in the deck (the other two are a publisher's bonus filling the 80-card print sheet).

(vii) Tree of Life — Path 12: The Intelligence of Transparency (ruled by Mercury).

Lies between Kether (formlessness) and Binah (the womb from which form is created).
Transparent = "allowing light to pass through so objects behind can be distinctly
seen" = NO distortion of the energy flowing Kether → Binah; the true Magician is a
CLEAR CHANNEL. Macrocosmically he channels the creative impulse issued in the Fool's
11th path; microcosmically he is a great communicator applying intelligence to build
his life. Yet "he is duality, he represents both truth and falsehood, wisdom and
folly" (BoT p.70) — hence the TRICKSTER, giving birth to form/illusion (Binah) out of
the pure formlessness of Kether.

(viii) Hebrew & Astrology

BETH = HOUSE (construction, making of things; has a "window"; also the human body).
The Magus must build himself / his world to be as clear (transparent) a conduit for
spiritual light as possible. Bet = 2 = duality / creation = form & illusion; the first
of the double letters (hard & soft pronunciation). Astrology: MERCURY (the Egyptians'
Thoth, inventor of writing; competent with words, language, speech — each holding a
vibration).

(ix) Symbolism

- Androgynous = naked, combined masculine + feminine features (like the Fool); from
  his association with the genderless unity of Kether.
- JUGGLES 8 OBJECTS:
  1. Censer = burning incense = FIRE.
  2. Cup = WATER; two-handled Grecian style.
  3. Dagger = AIR (Swords).
  4. Disk = EARTH (Coins); the 8-fold star of Mercury (Hod, 8th sephira, ruled by
     Mercury).
  5. Winged Egg = SPIRIT (the fifth element); the Orphic Egg; the nothingness out of
     which everything is born; reappears in the Lovers. Opposite of the Magus:
     manifestation vs. the formless potential of spirit.
  6 & 7. Stylus & Scroll = the tools of Thoth the scribe (hieroglyphs); intellect and
     Will communicated through writing.
  8. Wand of Double Power = the power of Horus (Lord of the New Aeon); the harmonising
     energy of Tiphareth on the middle pillar; resurrection (the Fool's path completed).
- Sunburst (lower right, barely visible) = Mercury's close relationship with the Sun.
- THE CADUCEUS (all about fertility, the birth of new things; BoT p.273 =
  "the balanced energy that has created the illusion of existence"):
  - Head = a winged phallus.
  - Circle = eye of Horus.
  - Dove descending in the circle = spirit descending into creation; links to the
    Empress (both cards have a dove; Venus = love, fertility) — the caduceus is
    sexually complete and fertile.
  - Yellow outline = Vau of Tetragrammaton (union of Yod/phallus and He/dove).
  - Lemniscate (infinity of serpents) above his head = the infinite nature of his
    source in Kether; stretches down through his feet, channelling into Binah.
  - Left serpent = wears the throne-shaped headdress of Isis = Binah (feminine of the
    Supernal Triangle). Right serpent = a basic crown = Kether. Together = the Magus
    connecting Kether and Binah. White light of Kether = a V-shape behind him; the
    darkness of Binah = below him.
- Winged Sandals of Hermes = the messenger; the channel for spirit arising in Kether.
- Sandal strap = a serpent (like the caduceus), shedding its skin = life anew.
- Background crossing lines = the making of form in creation; they do NOT penetrate
  the formless inverted triangle of Kether. The arc/semicircle at the bottom = Binah,
  giving birth to the illusion of form.
- Ape of Thoth (Cynocephalus), by his right foot = the distortion of "truth" inherent
  in form/speech; "Manifestation implies illusion" (BoT p.72). (A HermAnubis form of
  the Cynocephalus appears in the Fortune card.)

(x) Fool to Magus Transition

Macro: Fool (Path 11, Scintillating Intelligence, Kether→Chokmah) → Magus (Path 12,
Intelligence of Transparency), a clear channel for the fire of Path 11. Micro: leaving
the carefree bubble of the Fool for the Magician who deals with creation, forms, and
the karmas of creative action; stepping outside the comfort zone to create what's
needed for a bigger understanding of the universe. First step — support/encouragement
needed; his skills can create meaningful things or, misused, detrimental ones.

(xi) Divinatory Meanings

Upright: You have all the skills/tools to move forward; bring them together well.
Watch the content and purity of your thoughts (all actions have consequences). Have
the discipline/focus to translate ideas into action. Clear communication needed.
Assess your skill set honestly. Bring spirituality into life — be a channel for spirit
(the lemniscate connecting Kether and the material). Separate truth from what deceives
you. A good time to share spiritual knowledge with seekers.

Reversed: Using skills to others' detriment (tricksters, con men, claiming skills not
possessed). Out of touch with your reality; lost insight/objectivity. Creating things
detrimental to your spiritual path / distractions. Poor communication hampering
progress. Lost the "magic" — life feels mundane, uninspired; reassess skills and
motivation. Not sharing spiritual insights that would help others.
