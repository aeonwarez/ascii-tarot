(ii) Thoth Priestess

The Thoth Priestess sits behind a veil on her throne with a bow in her lap.
There are fruit, flowers and precious stones in the foreground.

(iii) Rider Waite High Priestess

The High Priestess sits between two pillars and in front of a veil decorated
with pomegranates. The veil separates realms of consciousness, with spiritual
realms unseen behind it.

(iv) Thoth Priestess & the Tree of Life — Path 13: Unifying Intelligence

The Thoth Priestess occupies Path 13 titled 'Unifying Intelligence' linking
Kether and Tiphareth, and represents the 'bridge' over the Abyss. This path
unites the Triad (Kether, Chokmah, Binah) above from the Hexad (remaining 6
sephira) below. It is the only path on the Tree that passes through the hidden
sephira DAATH — emphasizing hidden knowledge.

(v) Hebrew & Astrological Attributions

GIMEL (means Camel). A Camel appears in the foreground. The camel holds water
(a state of consciousness for the Greeks; Baptism in the Bible) = internal
consciousness. Just as the camel carries physical water across the desert, we
carry spiritual water (sound) inside through meditation while crossing the
desert. Without inner focus on spirit (light and sound), our vision is black
and landmarkless like the desert. This is the path of the Son (Tiphareth)
returning to the Father (Kether).

The Moon = the highest aspect of the divine feminine, harmonious changeability.

(vi) Symbolism

(a) The Priestess, Main Veil and Pillars

- Crown of Isis on her head = waxing, full and waning phases of the Moon /
  cycles of life, going with the flow.
- 7 moon crescents underneath the crown = 7 = Venus, her link to the Empress.
- Infinity sign around her eyes = connection to Kether / spirit, formless and
  infinite.
- Throne = stability. Aphorism: "Change is stability" — engage the flow of
  life (Moon), accept change rather than always controlling events.
- Bow = willpower; the middle path of balance is attained by following one's
  true will. Also the AUM sound (meditate upon it). Harris calls it a SISTRUM
  (a musical instrument of Isis); Crowley identifies it as the bow of Artemis;
  close examination shows a LYRE (associated with Apollo, brother of Artemis;
  Liber 777 attributes Tiphareth to Apollo — linking the path she connects,
  Tiphareth to Kether). Three-stringed.
- Arrow of Sagittarius = pointing to truth (Sagittarius overcomes the lower
  nature / horse by reaching upward).
- Inverted crescent moon on base of throne = the unconscious/subconscious,
  receptive, hidden/pointing downward (also found on the Moon card). The
  Priestess has a positive relationship with her subconscious; highly
  developed intuition.
- Book of Mysteries (hidden, hard to see) under the lyre on her lap = her
  esoteric knowledge, different from the Hierophant's outer instruction. Fits
  the path through hidden Daath.
- Main Veil (of the Abyss) she sits BEHIND — in RWS she sits in front of the
  veil; in Thoth she sits behind it, spreading it out to the two pillars. The
  veil of illusion between form and formlessness, truth and Maya. No organic
  forms behind the veil. She is the conduit for truth behind the illusion of
  separateness; represents the HGA taking us to our highest state in Kether.
- Two Pillars = Mercy and Severity; she sits between them (the path she rules).
  In RWS marked J & B = Jachin and Boaz, the two pillars of Solomon's Temple.

(b) In front of the Veil

- Camel = Hebrew attribution (Gimel), between the two opposite flowers, much
  like the path of the Priestess between the pillars.
- Fruits/flowers/crystals = Tiphareth is beauty; these outwardly represent it
  but the Priestess is truth CONCEALED within these illusory forms. Crowley
  associates them with the 'seeds'/beginnings of form: illusion in front of
  the veil, truth behind it.
- Flower on RIGHT = yellow, 10 petals = Malkuth & manifestation; spiral in
  shape = force = Chokmah.
- Flower on LEFT = concave petals = receptive = Binah.
- Pine cone = Bacchus. Grapes = Dionysus. Creative potential of the Fool
  (Kether–Chokmah) but also intoxication with illusory things (in front of
  the veil).
- Geometric forms = 2 on each side = manifestation of the formed universe;
  look like Plato's Solids.

(vii) Magus to Priestess Transition

From structuring our lives / creating tools for the spiritual quest (Magus) to
being in touch with higher intuition and spiritual energy guiding us internally
toward union with spirit (Priestess). She connects Tiphareth (soul/highest
self) with Kether (spirit). Difficult transitions: intellect too dominant to
access intuition/AUM; mistaking emotional urges for higher intuition (leading
to negative cults in the name of spirituality).

(viii) Divinatory Meanings

Upright:
- A platonic friendship based on spirituality.
- A period of solace to get in touch with your inner self.
- Changes on the inside are needed to bring changes outside.
- Connect and be more confident with your intuition / feminine side.
- An active spiritual path — inspired, intuitive, going with the flow, in
  balance.
- Your spiritual guide calls you to raise your bow (consciousness) inward and
  upward.
- Yearning to see behind the illusion (veil) of life.
- In times of darkness (desert) where you may be lost, look to your inner guide
  and connect with your intuition.

Reversed:
- Looking at the surface of things without depth or understanding.
- Ignoring your spiritual side/intuition; being mind-dominant.
- Unwillingness to accept your feminine side (in males or females).
- Not making enough time for spiritual study.
- Getting too caught up in the material side (things in front of the veil).
- Struggling spiritually, trying to get behind the veil but not succeeding.
